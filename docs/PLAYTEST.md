# Playtest: the First Playable (Windows)

This is a rough but complete thread through the first chapters of *Hymn of the Undertow*: the Prologue, Ch1 (greybox) and the Haluna slice. Most of what you see is placeholder: capsules stand in for people (Asha is the only real model, and she has no animations yet), the Shell Shrine's art is a first pass (its fixes are still to come), and the music uses the three prototype cues in place of the scenes' own. **The point is to feel the flow.** Please note anything that confuses you, drags, or feels wrong.

## What you play (about 40–50 minutes)

1. **Title.** Choose **New Game**.
2. **Prologue.** A dead city at dusk, with one line of Rhen's narration.
3. **Ch1, "Lamp-Diver"** (about 25–30 minutes; every place is grey blocks and capsules for now, and Rhen fights alone all chapter).
   - **The Underway.** A card ("One month earlier"), then Rhen on his tether in a drowned tower: dark teal, and his lantern is the only warm light.
     - At a **tether point** (the tan post), press **E** to drop a floor.
     - In the **bell-room**, Rhen talks to nobody. It heals you on every visit; there's no save. **Cut** the brass hinge by the door.
     - In the **galleries**, hold **F** (left face button) to open the lantern's shutter and widen the light. Opening it finds a gap in the green coral on the left wall, with an old diver's tin behind it.
     - Two scripted fights: a glass-shrimp shoal, then an oil slug that blinds you (use **Brine Eyewash** from **Items**).
     - Stop at the stairwell and its cord. Cut the brass **gauge-wheel** free. The limpet by the shaft is optional (you can flee it). Then clip on to the **current shaft** to ride up.
   - **Oruvel by day.** Sell the wheel at the scale-hull: **Haggle him up** or **Take his price**. Walk the raft-town and talk to the cook, the net-menders, the roof children (talk twice), the Reefball girl, Skipper Sadi and old Sabe. Crossing the east bridge to Mother Kell's stall plays the wick scene. **Stay a minute** is the optional side scene.
   - **The storm.** Walk on to the dive mooring: the carved gulls cry and the wall rises. Choose **Go to the net-pen** or **Kell first** (both end at the net-pen). Then:
     - fight two scuttlers;
     - **Cut** the two lashings so the low hull drifts clear;
     - reach the children on the pen.
   - **Hullgnaw** is a two-phase boss, fought alone. Watch the timeline:
     - quick strikes bring Rhen's turn sooner, and heal when its Coil shows the Gnaw coming;
     - at half health it breaks free, faster, and **Kick Off the Wall** lets you take your turns first.
   - Win, and "Going under" plays and fades to black.
4. **Cutscene A, "The Girl on the Beach".** Rhen wakes in the shallows at Haluna. There are two choices; both are fine.
5. **First battle.** Walking up the beach path toward the village starts a fight against a reef crab and a lantern eel, with Rhen and Asha (Toma in reserve). Win it, and the screen fades to **Neme's table**: noon, Rhen waking at Asha's aunt's door to far too much food. Flee it, and you're back on the beach below the path, with the fight still ahead. Lose it, and you can **Fight again** or **Return to last save** (your newest save; with none yet, this reads **Return to title**).
6. **Haluna, free to walk.** Four people, at the market, the chapel court, the dock and in the lane (a child skipping), each say something that hints at what a "Cantor" is. You overhear each one as you walk past, without stopping, and you can also talk to them.
   - In the lane, **Look** at the carved gull on a roof ridge.
   - **Optional, "Pila's Granddaughter":** Grandmother Osu washes the wake-shells at a wall at the causeway head (on the village side, left of where the causeway starts). Talk to her before you go on: it is the only time she's there, and a later scene remembers it.
   - **Shops and the inn**, open once you've met Asha:
     - the market stall sells tonics and the like;
     - the Net-Rack Forge sells weapons and gear;
     - the Mooring Lamp's keeper rents a bed for 30, which heals the party.

     Both shops add new stock after the Drownbell.
   - **Escape** opens the field menu (bestiary, Swell fill modes) for Rhen, Asha and Toma.
7. **Save** at the shrine: the glowing teal stone with a shell canopy, on the third lane into the village (past the tide-wall lanes). Resting there heals the party and opens the save menu.
8. **The causeway head.** Once you have saved, walk north along the beach, away from the dock, to the causeway head at the beach's far end. (Before you've saved, Toma and Asha send you back to the shrine in town as you walk in; you keep walking.) **This leaves Haluna's day behind:** you come back only at night. Asha asks first: choose **Go now** to set off, or **Not yet** to step back with nothing lost (Osu, the barks and the lintel-gull are still there, and walking in again asks again). After Go now, Toma and Asha talk about the low tide, and you set off along the **Saltgrass Causeway**.
   - **The causeway** (greybox for now): a long tidal path north to the shrine islet. Rhen, Asha and Toma talk as they pass the marker posts, a tidepool and a half-buried ring with a glint of teal in it; you overhear it as you walk.
   - **The first fight is a gull harrier over the saltgrass:** fliers dodge most blows, and Rhen's precise strikes find them. After it, the shallows can throw a fight at you every so often as you walk.
   - Climb the islet's steps into the **Shell Shrine** for its trial.
   - **The trial:** four stone basins, seen from above. Water runs from the spring through whichever channel each basin's lip faces, and the channels carrying water light up. Use a basin (**E**) to turn it a quarter turn. Send the water into the big pool on the far side; when the pool fills, the door at the end sinks (this also saves your progress: a loss to the Drownbell brings you back here, with the trial already solved), and walking through it plays **Cutscene B, "The First Verse"**, at sunset. Stuck for a minute and a half? Toma gives one hint. **Optional:** route the water into the small sluice near the second basin to drain the side pool, and pick up what's in it. Cutscene B notices.
   - The room is a first art pass: a fan-shell hall with channels inlaid with mother-of-pearl, shafts of light from three openings, and water you can see running. Beyond the sunken door is only a dark passage for now.
9. **The Drownbell.** The bell answers the verse. It's a two-phase boss, with Rhen, Asha and Toma and no reserve.
   - **Phase 1, the Toll:** watch the timeline. The bell gathers a toll that shoves everyone back, and the timeline shows it two turns out. Three fast strikes (Rhen's Attack or Quick Hit) break its rim and silence it.
   - **Phase 2, the Flood:** at about 40% health the water rises, and polyps heal the bell faster than you can cut them down. Asha's **Call** row brings in **Ilun**, the first Vowbound. The party steps back and he fights alone, his gauge filling as he drinks the flood. When it's full, his **Floodnet** clears the water. Dismiss him from his own menu, or he stays until he falls.

   Lose, and you can fight again or return to your last save (the shrine's open door, past the trial).
10. **The dock at night.** Win, and you're back in Haluna after dark: moonlight, stars, a faintly glowing sea and oil lamps under the eaves. Rhen finds Asha at the end of the dock and asks to come with her. Talking to the villagers again now gives their night lines.
11. **End card.** Rhen's voice-over on black, then the card and a short list of credits. When the prompt shows, press accept to return to the title. **Continue** loads your save, which now shows the chapter you reached.
12. **Ch4, "The Pilgrim's Wake"** (greybox, about 40 minutes; optional, editor runs only for now). The end card doesn't lead on to it yet. After the end card, **Continue** your save, press **F5** and pick **Ch4: Haluna, the wake night**. It plays only once the dock scene has sworn Rhen in.
   - **The wake night.** Dusk: the village carries its lamps to the wall at the causeway head, and the windows go dark. Walk the dark town (the child in the lane, the netmender on the dock, the Reed at the chapel, the inn's door), then north into the lamplight.
   - **The feast.** Talk at the four tables. **Neme's** (the right-hand table) is the one that counts: after it, walk up to the wall. The shell is set, the village sings, and Rhen walks out.
   - **The dock in the dark.** Asha sits with him. **Tell her about Oruvel** or **Not tonight**; both are fine. Watch her face while he looks at the water.
   - **Optional, "The Ink":** back at the wall, someone kneels with a lamp. **Look**. Going to **Neme's door** (**Sleep**) instead ends the night, and the scene is gone.
   - **Dawn.** The causeway at low water, then the ferry's landing, where Ysolde is waiting. She joins **in reserve**.
   - **Brightwater Crossing.** Walk the deck: three conversations play as you pass the rail, the awning and the wheel. The purser sells supplies; the deckhouse shrine **rests** the party (it can't save yet). Nothing attacks the ferry. Walk to the **bow**.
   - **The Seventh Terrace.** The party crosses to the drifting ship. Sail leeches drop on you first (an ambush). In the hold, a **bottle hermit** in a lamp glass: **Swap** Ysolde in on anyone's turn (it costs no time), **Read** it, then answer with the right element. The deck can throw a fight at you as you walk.
   - **The Spar-Wraith** (two phases; the party is rested first). Blows slide off its shroud: Read it, burn it. At about 60% it drops to the deck and its weakness changes: Read it again. It also telegraphs **Haul**, which shoves someone back on the timeline.
   - **The name-board**, the crossing and a fade. The end card then stands in for Ch5.

## Also playable: Ch7, "The Tidecup" (greybox, about 15 minutes)

Ch4 to Ch6 aren't built yet, so Ch7 isn't on the New Game thread. To play it from an editor run, press **F5** (debug teleport), choose **Ch7: Tamsule, the river mouth**, and press Enter. Every place is grey blocks and capsules, and the chapter is silent (its music and sounds don't exist yet). Ch7 has no fights.

1. **Into Tamsule.** The barge ties up under the stilt city; the bronze Bell hangs on its gantry beyond the north edge, with the sea held in its mouth.
2. **The port.** Talk to the market trader, the Reefball board-caller, the child, the fisher and the old woman on the stilt lane (west), and the boatman and Ysolde at the gantry foot (north).
3. **Tavi.** Cross the small bridge east of the gantry foot to the market boats. Oruvel's Reefball captain finds Rhen. There's one choice; both are fine. Rhen takes Pio's side-band (a key item).
4. **The Bell.** Walk to the far (north) edge of the gantry foot to go up to the galleries. The galleries scene plays; then Rhen is on the pool steps, and **the game autosaves**.
5. **The naming.** Walk down to the catch-pool's rim. The two captains each name one person the sea has taken, and the crowd answers.
6. **The match is a stand-in for now.** The Reefball match isn't built, so the naming goes straight to its end: the zone reloads at the pool and "After the Bell" plays. It's written to read the same whether Oruvel won or lost.
7. **Optional, "The Empty Moorings":** walk east over the bridge to the timber staging with six small dark rings and talk to Tavi at his boat.
8. **The afternoon.** Leave by the gallery entrance (south-west) to return to the port, where some of the talk has changed. Walk to the harbour (east) to board the trader for Corrow. The end card stands in for Ch8.

## Running it

### Option A: the Godot editor (recommended)

1. Download **Godot 4.6.3 stable**, the standard build (not .NET), for Windows: <https://godotengine.org/download/archive/4.6.3-stable/>. Unzip it anywhere. There is no installer.
2. Get the project: clone the repository, or download the integration branch (currently `claude/undertow-lead-handoff-423ixt`, with the latest art) as a ZIP from GitHub and unzip it.
3. Run `Godot_v4.6.3-stable_win64.exe`. In the Project Manager, click **Import**, choose the repository's `project.godot`, and click **Import & Edit**.
4. **The first open imports every asset, which takes a few minutes.** Wait until the progress bar at the bottom of the editor finishes.
5. Press **F5** (or the ▶ "Run Project" button at the top right). The game starts at the title.

To close the game, press **Alt+F4**, or click ■ Stop in the editor. **F8** also stops it from the editor.

**If it runs slowly:** on the title, choose **Settings**. Left and Right change the preset (try **Low**) or any single setting; each change is applied and kept at once. Escape goes back.

### Option B: an exported .exe

This needs the Godot 4.6.3 **export templates**: in the editor, open **Editor → Manage Export Templates → Download and Install**. Then open **Project → Export…**, select the **Windows Desktop** preset (already in the project), and click **Export Project**. The default output is `build/windows/hymn_of_the_undertow.exe`. Run the `.exe` next to its `.pck`.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Controller |
|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD or arrow keys | Left stick |
| Walk slowly | hold Shift | Left shoulder |
| Talk, examine, use the shrine | E, Enter or Space | A / Cross |
| Turn the camera (open areas only) | Q / R | Right stick |
| Open the lantern's shutter (Ch1, underwater) | hold F | hold X / Square |
| Advance dialogue, confirm | Enter or Space | A / Cross |
| Choose an option | Up / Down, then Enter | D-pad, then A |
| Back, cancel | Escape | B / Circle |
| Skip a cutscene (it stops at each choice, so you still choose) | hold Escape for 1 s | hold B / Circle |
| Field menu (bestiary, Swell fill modes) | Escape in the field | B / Circle |
| Battle menus | Up / Down / Left / Right, Enter, Escape | D-pad, A, B |

In battle, the timeline on the right shows who acts next. Highlighting a command previews how it changes the order. Swap brings Toma in from the reserve. In the Drownbell fight, the panel at the top left says what the bell is building toward (the Toll and how many of its turns away it is), how many rim hits you've landed, and later how many polyps feed it.

Debug keys (editor runs only): **F1** lists the debug pages. **F4** shows the story flags and **F5** teleports. They're useful if you get stuck.

## Known placeholders and limits

- People are capsules, apart from Asha, who stands in a rest pose because she has no animations yet. Asha's face does change: her expression follows each line's emotion, and her mouth moves as her text appears. Body animations are still missing.
- The music is a prototype stand-in: the hush theme sketch plays on the beach and in Haluna, the temple cue plays at the shrine, and the hush theme v2 plays for the verse. Nobody has signed off on any of them.
- The glimmer motes in the cutscenes are simple glowing dots, not the real particle effect.
- Haluna's world is the art team's scene, now shipped with the zone, with simple collision added. The camera mostly follows Rhen from behind; only a few spots use composed shots.
- The night has no colour grade of its own yet.
- Ilun is a placeholder shape, and his call has no cinematic yet. The bell and polyps are placeholder shapes too.
- Entering Haluna holds the black fade for about a second while the village builds itself; the picture no longer freezes when it appears.
- Ch4 is grey blocks and capsules: Haluna's wake night is a stand-in town, not the real Haluna at night. It has no music or sounds of its own yet, the song at the wall is shown as text, and the ferry's shrine heals without saving.
- In the field, Escape opens the field menu, which pauses the game. From it you can open the bestiary or choose each hero's Swell fill mode. Escape backs out a step at a time and then closes it. It doesn't open during a cutscene or a conversation. **E** also advances dialogue.

- **Ch1 is greybox:** blocks and capsules, and a teal stand-in for the underwater look. Its music and sounds don't exist yet, so the chapter plays mostly silent. The sea between the hulls is a floor you can walk on. Ch1 has no save shrine (its chapter start autosaves).
- **Ch7 is greybox too**, and its Reefball match is a stand-in that skips straight to the result. The port has no shop, inn or save shrine yet (its chapter start and the pool steps autosave). Liss and Kerrid appear only as capsules: they can't join the party yet.

## Ch5, "Terraces of Ash" (greybox; about 35–45 minutes)

Ch5 isn't on the New Game thread yet: Ch4, which brings the ferry in, is being built alongside it. From an editor run, press **F5** (teleport) and pick **Pemakar** (`ch05/pemakar`). The party starts as Rhen, Asha and Toma, with Ysolde in reserve. Everything is grey blocks and capsules, and the chapter has no music or sounds yet.

1. **The ferry comes in** at night. Rhen notices Asha's first band, and Ysolde reads the Tenders' letter.
2. **Pemakar before the boats.** Walk up the town from the harbour. You overhear people as you pass: an old ferryman by the boats, the boat-folders and a lacquer shop in the market, a child in the forge lane counting the thunder, and a woman and a child on the grave terraces (east, by the paddies).
   - **Optional, "The Smith's Question":** talk to **Garo** at the forge on the west side of the forge lane. If you miss him tonight, he's there again the next afternoon.
3. **Lanternfall.** Walk to the bell at the top of the town (past the open-hand gate). The boats go down the mountain, and Rhen chooses who his boat is for: **For Mother Kell**, **For Oruvel** or **Not yet** (which keeps it; check **Items**). Then the party walks down to the weir by the harbour, and a stranger joins at the back of the line.
4. **Ashcrown, the next day.** You start at the foot of the pilgrim stair with Rhen, Asha and Kerrid (Toma and Ysolde in reserve).
   - The first fight is two **slag tortoises**. Their shells turn blows aside; Kerrid's **Fluke Cleave** breaks them for a while, and the timeline shows how long.
   - Further up, the stair can throw a fight at you every so often: ashwings (fire hurts them; their dust brings sleep), a vein sparker (magic finds it), and more tortoises.
5. **The trial.** At the top, walk east into the red crown pavilion, and the Tender gives the rite. Then the strike goes down four tiers, south along the copper.
   - On each tier, use the **catch-rod** (west side) to charge the bowl, turn the bronze **vein-gates** (**E**) until the copper runs from the bowl to the tier's edge, then pull the **release** (east side). The strike pours down to the next tier's bowl, and the stair down opens.
   - The charge fades as you **walk** (about 64 m per charge), not while you stand and think. If it fades, or you route it into an ash drift, catch it again at the same tier's rod.
   - On tier 3, the two outer gates turn together. One of their positions runs the strike into a sealed vein in the east wall: it costs the charge, but it opens an alcove with the **Quench Charm** in it.
   - On tier 4, a gate only passes while someone stands on its plate. Use a plate to send Toma there.
   - Stuck on a tier for a minute and a half? The Tender gives one hint.
6. **The Cinder Matron.** Walk on south into the anvil court when the strike lands. She's a two-phase boss: break her crust with Kerrid, then hit hard while it's broken. At half health she pours slag on everyone. In this phase, water and cold (Ysolde's Frost, or calling Ilun) harden her again.
7. **Kahre.** Asha goes into the chamber alone, and the party waits. Then she sings the second verse.
8. **Pemakar, after.** You're back in town in the afternoon. A few things have changed, and Garo is there if you didn't meet him. Walk down to the harbour road for the dawn, and the road goes on to the Brine Shoals (Ch6, below).

**Ch5 placeholders:**
- **Kerrid's battle kit and Kahre's are provisional**, so expect odd numbers. Kahre can't be called yet in practice: no battle after the binding uses him.
- **The Quench Charm is a key item** for now, not the Fire ward it will become.
- **The trial has no lightning yet:** a charged bowl just glows pale violet.
- **The Matron's two phases:** there's no slag on the floor, and her timeline telegraph for the re-crust uses the standard charge marker.

## Ch9, "The Ebbing" (greybox; the end of Act I; about 20–25 minutes)

Ch8 isn't built yet, so Ch9 isn't on the New Game thread. From an editor run, press **F5** (teleport) and pick **Ch9: Corrow, the night of the Ebbing**. Everything is grey blocks and capsules, and the chapter is nearly silent (its music and sounds don't exist yet). **Ch9 has no fights.**

1. **The strand of the drowned,** at night after the storm. The dead lie in rows under sailcloth. Pale cyan lights hang over the flooded town behind the broken wall.
2. **Walk among them.** Talk to Marra at the east end of the soldiers' row (the one with the shields). You overhear Hobb reading names and a guard by the rows. Liss is on the wall-head at the west end, but she only speaks if Ch8 has played.
3. **Toma** is kneeling in the soldiers' row. Talk to him (**Go to Toma**).
   - **Optional, "Shield-Mate":** this side scene needs the Ch8 flag `ch08_met_ivo`. In a direct start, set it with **F4** (the flag inspector) first. There's one choice; both are fine.
4. **The Ebbing.** Walk onto the raft's landing in the middle of the strand. The gilt line is painted, Asha goes out on the raft, and the dance has three parts.
   - In each part, **hold E** (or Enter, Space, or A on a controller) to raise the staff. Let go and the dance waits. You can't fail, and nothing is timed.
   - Between the second and third parts there's a short cut to somewhere else.
5. **After.** Asha comes ashore, and a stranger comes down the rocks. Then the screen goes dark.
6. **Dawn.** You're on the road outside the town gate. Walk east up the road to the milestone, where someone is waiting.
7. **The act break.** The road scene cuts to the plaza from the very start of the game, a voice-over plays, then "Act II: The Truth". The **end card** stands in for Act II.

**Ch9 placeholders:**
- **The lights are placeholders.** The Ebbing will be thousands of lights streaming after the staff; for now a few small spheres drift down and out to sea. The "Hold to raise the staff" prompt is plain text.
- **The raft cuts** between the landing and the middle of the harbour. The harbour is a floor you can walk on.
- **No save shrine and no shop yet.** The chapter start autosaves, so quitting at dawn replays the night.
- **The plaza at the act break** is grey blocks, lit by the dawn instead of dusk.

## Ch6, "Salt Edict" (greybox; about 25–30 minutes)

Ch6 follows Ch5's dawn. To start it on its own from an editor run, press **F5** (teleport) and pick **Ch6: The Brine Shoals**: the party starts as Rhen, Asha and Kerrid, with Toma and Ysolde in reserve. Everything is grey blocks and capsules, and the chapter has no music or sounds yet.

1. **The Brine Shoals.** Walk north along the dark wreck-ridges. You overhear the party at a brass wreck and at a chalk-marked hull, and there's a midday stop on a hull. The road throws a fight at you every so often: shoal skippers (quick, hard to hit; Rhen's), wreck crabs (shells; Kerrid's Fluke Cleave cracks them) and gull harriers. The paler mud-flats to the west are the same road for now.
2. **The smoke and the raid.** At the last ridge, Toma stops first. Then the camp burning, the brine-irons, a girl running into Rhen, and the hold.
3. **The Stillguard Inquisitor and his hound.** Toma, Rhen and Asha are on the field, Kerrid and Ysolde in reserve. **Toma will not strike the Stillguard:** his Attack and Shell Breaker are greyed out, and the help says why. He still has his guard (Stand Fast, Brace, Challenge, Shield Wall), or you can swap him for a striker. At half health the Inquisitor fires the hulk, and the smoke blinds the party now and then (Ysolde's magic still lands). Nobody dies: both of them give up at the end, and the banner says so.
4. **The barge.** A white barge comes up the channel. Watch it all the way through; there's one choice for Rhen, and both are fine.
5. **After the barge.** The wrights are led away, and Liss joins on Toma's papers (in reserve; she has no fights until Ch8). Then she looks at Rhen's lantern.
6. **Camp at dusk.** On the high hull east of the camp: the shrine in the wreck's bow (it rests the party and saves), the vendor's boat, and three people to talk to by the fire. Kerrid keeps watch at the seaward end.
   - **Optional, "The Burned Lamp":** walk down to the tideline below the camp. There's one choice; both are fine.
7. **The channel at dawn.** Walk to the north end of the channel (west of the camp) to wade out toward Tamsule. Ch7 starts at its river mouth.

**Ch6 placeholders:**
- **Liss's battle kit is provisional** (two stand-in gadgets), and so are the Inquisitor's and the hound's. The hound doesn't guard the Inquisitor or pounce on whoever strikes him yet, and the smoke is a status every third turn, not a field.
- **Toma's "No. Not them."** shows as his first-turn prompt in the fight, not as a bark when you pick a greyed command.
- **The barge's look** (no wake, no water sound, Sefir's stillness) needs the water shader and the character art. In the greybox it's a white block with capsules on it.
- **The flats** have the same random fights as the ridges.
