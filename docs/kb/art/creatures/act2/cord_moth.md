# Cord moth (`cord_moth`), Ch12

**Where:** Keth Pass, along the pass-cords. **Kind:** beast. **Role:** it **unravels** one buff; fire-weak.

**The idea:** a pale moth whose larvae live in old keth cords and eat the knots. The adults carry the knot patterns
they ate on their wings, as real moths carry what they fed on. Ysolde can read a knot on one: a name. The one
exaggeration: **the wings carry readable knots**.

**Silhouette:** a broad-winged moth with a 1.4 m span, hovering at chest height, wings held flat and wide. A furred
body, feathered antennae, and the knot rows across both wings like stitched lines.

**Palette:** wings `cumulus_lit` to `sand_cloth`, the knot rows `lacquer_red` faded and `sea_blue_cloth` (the cords'
own dyes); body `overcast_slate`; eyes `lacquer_black`; weak point (the thorax) `spawn_wound`, small.

**Clips:** `idle` (a slow hover, wings beating in bursts); `attack` (a dart and a wing-strike: contact 0.5 s of
1.1 s); `cast` (it opens its wings full and the knot rows catch the light; the unravel); `hit` (it tumbles in the air;
peak near 0.1 s); `ko` (it drifts down like a dropped scarf). Budget ≤ 5k triangles. Rig: body, four wing bones a
side, antennae.
