# Tannin archer (`tannin_archer`), Ch10

**Where:** Ossa Fen, the mangrove channels. **Kind:** fen beast. **Role:** a ranged striker whose jet **delays** its
target on the timeline.

**The idea:** an archerfish grown to a dog's length in the tannin-dark water. It still hunts the way archerfish do:
it holds still under the surface, sights along a groove in its mouth and knocks prey off the roots with a jet of
water. The one exaggeration: **size, and the force of the jet** (it can knock a grown man off his footing).

**Silhouette:** a deep-bodied, flat-sided fish 1.1 m long, half out of the water on a root, the mouth upturned, the
eyes large and set forward. Six dark vertical bars on its flanks (the archerfish's own pattern) read at battle
distance.

**Palette:** back `deep_teal_shadow` to `abyss_navy`; flanks `sand_cloth` with `lacquer_black` bars; belly `bone`;
eye `dusk_gold`; weak point (the gill slit) `spawn_wound`, small.

**Clips:** `idle` (fins scull, the body tracks a target); `attack` (it rises, sights, spits: contact 0.55 s of 1.2 s,
the jet is VFX); `cast` (a long aimed jet at the back row); `hit` (it flinches and slaps the water; peak near 0.1 s);
`ko` (it rolls off the root and sinks). Budget ≤ 6k triangles. Rig: a spine of 5, jaw, pectorals, tail.
