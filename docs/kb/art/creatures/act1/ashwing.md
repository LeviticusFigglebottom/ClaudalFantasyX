# Ashwing (`ashwing`), Ch5 Pemakar terraces

**Idea.** A broad grey moth of the ash terraces, drawn to the Lanternfall leaf-boats' lights. Its wing-dust is fine
ash; where it settles, sleep. A beast. One exaggeration: a moth with a 1.6 m span.

**Silhouette and palette.** Hovering at 1.4 m, broad rounded wings held flat, feathery antennae. Wings `overcast_slate`
to `storm_slate` with pale `bone` eye-spots that read as two faces at battle distance; body furred `timber_weathered`;
the weak point a `spawn_wound` speck in each eye-spot's pupil (it fed on grave-offerings near the dead).

**Clips.** `idle`: slow flutter with glides. `attack`: a buffet, the wings clapping. `cast`: it hovers and shakes
dust (VFX). `hit`: it tumbles. `ko`: it spirals down, wings closing. Budget ≤ 5k.

*Update (m2-slice act1_ch05 §9): the eye-spots are ember (`ember_orange` toward `dusk_gold`), reading like a leaf-boat's flame in the dark.*

*Battle read (the Act I final-quality audit, 2026-10-29): at battle distance its flat wings read as two small pale slivers
in the Ch5 stair arena. Its battle scene stands it at 1.35× (a 2.2 m span) and its `stand_in.hover` is 1.86 m (1.38 ×
1.35, so its KO still lands on the floor; tools/tests/test_creatures.py reads the battle scene's Model scale).*
