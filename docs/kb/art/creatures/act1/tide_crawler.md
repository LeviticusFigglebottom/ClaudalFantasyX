# Tide crawler (`tide_crawler`), Ch8 the seawall

**Idea.** The Undertow's foot soldiers at Corrow: crab-legged spawn that climb out of the storm-surge and up the
Fisher Stair in waves. Each is a grotesque echo of a shore crab (bible: grotesque echoes of sea animals): too many
legs, a carapace like wet black lacquer split by glimmer wounds.

**Silhouette and palette.** 0.7 m high, 1.2 m across, legs higher than the body (it climbs). `lacquer_black` /
`abyss_navy` clearcoat, wounds `spawn_wound`, motes `glimmer_held`. At battle distance: black spiders with cyan seams.

**Clips.** `idle`: legs tapping out of rhythm. `attack`: it rears and strikes with both front legs. `cast`: the wounds
pulse. `hit`: it skitters back. `ko`: it collapses and the wounds go dark. Budget ≤ 5k (several on screen).
