# Tower limpet (`tower_limpet`), Ch1 Oruvel Underway

## The idea

A limpet the size of a cartwheel, clamped to the drowned tower's stair. When the tower went under, one grew its shell
over a fallen **window shutter** and took it along: half its cone is slatted wood, salt-white and grown through
with shell. A reef beast. It moves a finger's width an hour, until it doesn't.

The one exaggeration: **scale and the shutter in its shell**.

## Silhouette and palette

- A low ribbed cone 1.2 m across and 0.55 m high, tilted (it clings to a slope), the shutter's slats breaking the
  cone's ribs on one side. The muscular foot shows as a dark wet lip at the base.
- Shell `coral_stone_weathered` ribs with `bone` growth lines; the shutter `timber_weathered` faded toward `sand_dry`;
  the foot `tortoiseshell` and wet; the weak point a `spawn_wound` seam where shell meets shutter.

## Clips

- `idle`: barely moves: the shell lifts 2 cm off the foot and settles, once (a held breath).
- `attack`: it lifts, slides forward 0.3 m fast, and clamps down (a crushing shove).
- `cast`: the shell rises high and tilts, the foot rippling (a slow delaying grip).
- `hit`: it clamps down flat.
- `ko`: the shell lifts, tips onto its side, the foot curls.

Rig: root, shell, foot (a few ripple bones). Budget ≤ 10k triangles (a lone foe; 9.1k after the 2026-10-29 primitive pass: sharp ribs into a scalloped margin, growth ledges, a worn pitted apex, the shutter as boards with gaps and two rusted iron straps, barnacle clusters, a fringe of mantle tentacles round the foot).
