# Flue moth (`flue_moth`), Ch8 Coilspire

**Idea.** Not spawn: a soot-moth the size of a dog, drawn up Coilspire's copper flues by the keeping flame (m2-slice
act1_ch08 §9; fire absorb, ice weak; Ysolde's fights). Embers smoulder in its fur, and it has eaten so much soot that
it is soot. A beast. The one exaggeration is its size.

**Silhouette and palette.** 0.85 m long and low, crouched on short legs, with feathered antennae. At rest the forewings
fold over its back like a roof of soot. That keeps it apart from the Ch5 ashwing, which shows its wings spread. Fur
`lacquer_black` into `volcanic_stone`, with embers (`ember_orange`, a slow pulse) in the fur. The forewings are
`volcanic_stone` and `overcast_slate` with black soot zig-zags. The hindwings are hidden at rest: `ember_orange`
edged with `lacquer_red_deep`, shown only when it flares. Ember light is fire, never glimmer.

**Weak point (M3-CRE-650, ADR 0151).** It is a beast, so its weak point is one small closed mark: a seam seared shut
across its brow, above the eyes, where the keeping flame once caught it. The seam of light is 7.6 cm long and 2.6 cm
wide, in a raised soot-black lip, a little to its near side (its right, -x, the battle camera's side once the stage
turns it to the party). The brow faces forward and up, so both the battle camera and the review three-quarter (+x
front) see it. It sits on the smooth head because a first try on the thorax collar was buried by the shaggy fur. It
is never an open seam and never sheds motes. It has its own `wound` slot (#3FE0C8, 1.4, pulse 0.35 Hz at depth 0.35,
the reef crab's values), because the fur's emission is the embers. Clips, bones and rests are unchanged.

**Clips.** `idle`: the antennae comb the air, the wings shiver. `attack`: a soot buffet (wings clap open, a lunge).
`cast`: the flare (it rears, every wing spread, the ember hindwings shown). `hit`: a flinch. `ko`: it tips onto its
side, wings splayed. Budget ≤ 5k (3.2k).
