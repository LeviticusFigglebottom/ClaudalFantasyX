# Stormgull (`stormgull`), Ch8 the headland path

**Idea.** A palette variant of the gull harrier (m2-slice act1_ch08 §9; a lightning attack, Rhen's answer): the same
build, rig and clips in a storm-dark plumage. The lightning bolt itself is VFX.

**Palette.** Mantle `storm_slate` deepened toward `lacquer_black`, with black wing tips. The breast is slate, not
white. The beak and legs are slate, with a black hook and gonys spot. The eye-ring is `starlight` for the charge it
carries, the same charge light as the vein sparker's, never a glimmer hue.

**Build.** `assets_src/creatures/stormgull/build_stormgull.py` imports `build_gull_harrier` and overrides its palette.
The job lists the gull harrier's folder as a source.
