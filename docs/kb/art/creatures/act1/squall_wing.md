# Squall wing (`squall_wing`), Ch8 the seawall

**Idea.** Undertow-spawn in the shape of a gull, as if the storm remembered the gull harrier and got it wrong: the
wings are black wet membranes, the body hollow with held light showing through tears in it. It flies in the rain at
the wall-head.

**Silhouette and palette.** 2.0 m span, hovering at 1.2 m, wings too long and too thin, ragged. `lacquer_black` wet
membranes, `spawn_wound` tears, `glimmer_held` motes trailing. Reuses the gull harrier's rig (a variant body).

**Clips.** As the gull harrier's (idle wingbeat, dive attack, cry cast, buckle hit, fall KO), stiffer. Budget ≤ 6k.
