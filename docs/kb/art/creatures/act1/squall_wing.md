# Squall wing (`squall_wing`), Ch8 the seawall

**Idea.** Undertow-spawn in the shape of a gull, as if the storm remembered the gull harrier and got it wrong: the
wings are black wet membranes, the body hollow with held light showing through tears in it. It flies in the rain at
the wall-head: "a gull-shaped hole in the rain" (m2-slice act1_ch08 §9). In Wave 2 and the Reach's Dark phase it
harries Liss's rope and basket on the hoist, so its attack is a steep stoop at something below it. High evasion;
Rhen is its answer.

**Silhouette and palette.** 2.0 m span, hovering at 1.2 m (the origin is the hover point, as the gull's). The body is
small and gaunt and the wings too long and too thin, ragged: a membrane on a thin black arm-spar, the trailing edge
scalloped and torn, ragged rents torn clean through the membrane so the sky shows through, the hand frayed into four
long streamers, and five frayed streamers for a tail. `lacquer_black` wet membranes with an `abyss_navy` underside;
the right wing (toward the camera) is torn worst. The body is a hollow lacquer shell; its tears are open holes, each
with a thin lit lip, onto a dark hollow where the held `spawn_wound` light hangs (the weak point), and where the eyes
should be are two lit slits. A long black beak, a little open on the same light. The `glimmer_held` motes it trails are VFX.
Reuses the gull harrier's rig layout, bone names, wing poses and feather-blade primitive (imported from its build
module, not edited).

**Clips.** As the gull harrier's, stiffer (held keys, snaps instead of follow-through): `idle` (a beat that stops dead
at the top and bottom, the head cocking in jerks), `attack` (a hard climb, a held beat, then it folds and stoops head
first, talons and beak reaching forward and down; contact 0.62 s, well below its hover), `cast` (it spreads and holds,
beak wide), `hit` (it buckles), `ko` (one wing goes slack, it slews and falls 1.2 m like a torn kite). Budget ≤ 6k;
built at 5.6k.
