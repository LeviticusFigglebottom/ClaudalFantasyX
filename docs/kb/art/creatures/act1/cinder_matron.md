# Cinder Matron (`cinder_matron`), Ch5 boss

**Idea.** Ashcrown's temple guardian: a construct the old temple-builders raised from the mountain's own stone to
keep the crown, a seated matron figure of laterite blocks, eyes closed and mouth open like the Watchers (references.md:
closed eyes, open mouths), crusted with magma that has cooled and cracked over her in layers. Phase 1 she is crusted;
phase 2 the crust breaks and the glowing stone underneath shows (act_outline: two phases, armour break). A construct,
not a creature: built by the creatures pipeline, rigid parts.

**Silhouette.** 3.2 m, seated, a broad block shape with long arms ending in flat stone hands; the head a calm mask.

**Palette.** Laterite blocks `laterite`, crust `volcanic_stone`, cracks `ember_orange` emissive (the mountain's fire,
not glimmer). Phase 2: the crust parts (a `break_parts` swap, as the Drownbell's rim) and the cracks widen.

**Clips.** `idle`: a slow rising breath, the crust creaking. `attack`: a hand slam. `cast`: both hands rise, the cracks
flare. `hit`: a grind. `phase_change`: the crust bursts off. `ko`: she settles forward, the glow going out. Budget ≤ 16k.
