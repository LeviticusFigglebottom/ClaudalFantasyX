## The act in one paragraph
Pulled from the sea by the Hollowmere ship that waited at the Rim, the party learns in Dace's dry-dock why he never
came home and that he went into the storm on purpose, after Imra. Asha, who now knows the singer becomes the storm's
heart, chooses the Unbinding: not to sing herself in, and not to go home and sing nothing, but to end it, knowing
everything bound will go, the Kite and her friends' dead and Kerrid with it. The Kite rises; the world opens and is
seen changed; Pell turns around at last and is named again; the Eighth Verse is assembled from keth, Kirru song and
the Choir's stones. At Iskarra the prologue's vigil comes back, understood. Then the dive: six rooms of drowned memory,
six friends let go, the four founders who thank them as they fall, and the heart (act3_ending).

## Rules for every Act III sheet
1. **Light grammar** (bible §2): up = released, down/seaward = held. In Act III held light is everywhere until H09. No
   mote rises before the Letting except: a lifting done the Kirru way (a few motes, as in Ch10; Haluna in Ch18), a
   Vowbound's release in Ch20 (each one, once), and Nhoa's Swell.
2. **No gilt line in Act III** (N-04). The verse-sleeve is full (S7) from Ch17 and unravels only at H07.
3. **The Eighth Verse is never sung before H07.** Ysolde teaches its *words* only; Pell teaches the *tune* as the
   farewell-tide, wordless; the two meet only in Asha at the heart.
4. **Imra, Dace and Kerrid never share a frame** (act3_ending §0 rule 8) — and in Ch19 nothing names the three together.
5. **No Act IV hint, no sequel bait.** The ending is final (SPEC §3.5).
6. Banned-terms list and the voice guide hold. "Revenant" may be said in game text from Ch19 (glossary); never before.
