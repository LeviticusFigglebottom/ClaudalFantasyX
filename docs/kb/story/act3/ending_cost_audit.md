# Ending-cost audit (pillar 6): what each companion loses, and whether every scene earns it (2026-10-30)

The Lead asked for this after the Act III sheets. **Pillar 6:** breaking the cycle is right, but nobody keeps
everything. For each companion this lists what they lose by the end, the chain of scenes that earns it (Act I plant →
Act II turn → Act III choice → the payoff), a verdict, and a fix for any gap.

**Sources:** the character sheets (Wants, Needs, Act I–III, Ending); every beat sheet; the Act II scripts; the Act III
sheets in this folder; [act3_ending.md](../beats/act3_ending.md); vowbound.md.

**Verdicts:**
- **Earned:** the loss is planted, turned and chosen on screen.
- **Thin:** one link is optional, or told rather than shown.
- **Gap:** a link is missing.

**Fixes:**
- **Applied:** I made it in my own Act III sheets.
- **Proposed:** it touches someone else's doc.

## 1. Asha: her singing voice, and her mother again
**What she loses:** she lives but never sings or hums again (asha_velamir.md Ending; act3_ending E5). Imra is lost a
second time, after one scene of hands (E1).

**The earning chain:**
- **Act I:**
  - she sings her first verse at the Shell Shrine and Rhen knows it as his father's lullaby (Ch3, N-11);
  - the Velamir women hum the lullaby without the words (bible §5);
  - the Ebbing is danced, not sung (Ch9).
- **Act II:**
  - every binding is sung (Ch11, Ch12, Ch13, Ch16);
  - she hears what the singing costs at the Choir (Ch16 B05).
- **Act III:**
  - she chooses the Unbinding knowing the cost (Ch17 B08);
  - she learns the tune wordless (Ch18 K05);
  - the words, spoken (Ch19 I04);
  - the Letting (H07);
  - nothing comes (E5).

**The gap:** every time we hear Asha sing, she is singing **as a Cantor**: a verse, a binding, a rite. The voice she
loses has never been shown as *hers*. Losing it would read as losing her job, not herself, which undercuts E5.

**Fix (applied):** in Ch18 K05, after Pell teaches her the farewell-tide, Asha hums the Velamir lullaby back to him,
unasked: a trade of songs, her family's for his people's. It is the only time in the game she hums something just
because she wants to. Nobody says it's the last time. Rhen hears it from the shore's edge. E5's silence then lands on
*that*.
- If Narrative wants a second plant, use the optional Ch16 camp (`ch16_camp_coming_up`): she asks Rhen for the divers'
  lamps "coming up slow". That's already about her end, without naming it.

**Check:** E1 (Imra) is earned by Ch8 (`ch08_rhen_told_asha`: the figure humming), Ch16 ("She's in it. My mother."),
and Ch17 (the ledger tells of Imra on the stair).

## 2. Rhen: his father, for real
**What he loses:** Dace, after the goodbye he never had; "this time Rhen says it back" (E2).

**The earning chain:**
- **Act I:** the current-song in the storm (Ch1); he freezes before the figure in the storm and Kerrid hauls him back
  (Ch8 B17, `ch08_rhen_froze`). That is **the first time he doesn't answer**.
- **Act II:**
  - he admits he's Dace's son (Ch11);
  - he ties a knot for him, believing him dead (Ch12, optional);
  - Kerrid's half-story (Ch15).
- **Act III:**
  - the ledger, and its blank page (Ch17 B05);
  - the hitch (Ch19 I03);
  - the heart (H02);
  - he says it back (E2).

**Verdict: earned.** The "back" in "says it back" depends on Ch8 B17 being read as a goodbye he didn't answer.
**Check (proposed, Cinematics):** in E2, an insert that rhymes with Ch8 B17's framing (the same lens and the same
distance) so the player feels the answer arrive. If `ch12_rhen_tied_knot`, H02 may use "I carry the knot. You don't
have to." (the act2_ch12 flag table suggests it).

**Not lost, and right:** Kell's wick stays lit for the future (E7). Rhen keeps something, and it's the one thing the
pillar says somebody gets to keep: a small, ordinary flame.

## 3. Kerrid: himself
**What he loses:** his unrisen life, and the watch; he goes last (E3).

**The earning chain:**
- **Act I:** "I held a stair for your mother once." (Ch5); the stair (Ch8); "Stair's clear." heard by a child (Ch9,
  optional).
- **Act II:**
  - he stands, never sits (Ch10–14);
  - the confession (Ch15);
  - "a watch is for being relieved" (Ch15 camp, optional);
  - he holds the line (Ch16).
- **Act III:**
  - "first down the stair" (Ch17 B08, Ch20 U00);
  - the hitch, "Somebody should have it" (Ch19 I03);
  - Kahre's "It doesn't hurt, son" (Ch20 U02);
  - "Stair's clear." (E3).

**Verdict: earned**, and it is the game's cleanest arc. The risk was a martyr with no want of his own. The Ch15 camp
("I want to be relieved") and the Ch19 hitch give him one. **Note:** the Ch15 camp is optional, so Ch19 I03's "Somebody
should have it" must carry the want alone for players who skipped it. It does.

## 4. Liss: every machine her people own, the Kite, Tobin's lamp
**What she loses:** every bound light in Saltwright work goes dark at H09, the Kite included. She brings it down by hand
(E4). Tobin's light, caught in the cracked lamp (ADR 0204 §13), goes with everything else.

**The earning chain:**
- **Act I:** her salvage camp burned (Ch6); her gadgets glow with bound light (liss_varrow.md).
- **Act II:**
  - Rhen hid what he is (Ch11);
  - the lamp bench (Ch11);
  - the Archive: binding was her people's craft too (Ch13);
  - Tobin killed (Ch14);
  - "I want it to cost them" (Ch14 camp, optional);
  - the cracked lamp (Ch15).
- **Act III:**
  - the Kite flies on the held dead (Ch17 B10);
  - "Then I'd better build you something that gets you there" (Ch17 B08);
  - Virel names Dace's kite (Ch20 U03);
  - she can't mend the lamp (Ch20 R1);
  - E4.

**The gap:** her **Need** is to *accept* that her people's way is also a way of holding on (liss_varrow.md). As drafted,
the Unbinding simply *happens to* her machines. She never chooses to let go of anything; she only fails to mend a lamp.
Pillar 6 needs her to spend something on purpose.

**Fix (applied):** Ch20 U00 is the Kite's last full charge down the eye. Liss stops trying to mend Tobin's lamp and
seats it in the Kite's engine socket: Tobin's light is part of the charge that takes them down. She spends her
brother's held light to get his release. Her line, one: *"He volunteered. So nobody'd have to be chosen."* (the Ch14
camp line, turned). R1 changes to match: the empty padded box in her lap.

## 5. Pell: Ressa
**What he loses:** his sister's held light, the last of her (Ch20 U05).

**The earning chain:**
- **Act I:** "beautiful, and pointing the wrong way" (Ch9).
- **Act II:**
  - kept out (Ch10);
  - "I did not turn around" (Ch10);
  - he kneels before Nhoa (Ch11);
  - "Somebody should see it" (Ch11 camp, optional);
  - "I knew" (Ch15).
- **Act III:**
  - through the gate, named again (Ch18 K04);
  - he teaches the tune (K05);
  - he turns his back and sings (Ch20 U05).

**Verdict: earned**, the most complete arc. **Watch:** the Ch10 camp (optional) gives Pell's reason for watching
("Somebody should see it given"). In U05 he does the opposite *for* her. Players without the camp still have Ch10's
"I did not turn around".

## 6. Ysolde: Corran, and the tune
**What she loses:** Corran (Ch20 U06). She teaches Lamptongue "the words only, never the tune" (epilogue), so the song
she made possible is one she won't hear again.

**The earning chain:**
- **Act I:** she fixes the ink on Asha's wake-shell (Ch4); she doesn't cry at the Ebbing (Ch9).
- **Act II:**
  - the lie and the confession (Ch12);
  - the roll and Corran's line (Ch13);
  - Corran's house (Ch13, optional);
  - "I would want him let go" (Ch13 camp, optional).
- **Act III:**
  - the words begun (Ch17 B11);
  - the words whole (Ch19 I04);
  - Ethren (Ch20 U06).

**The gap:** her **Want** is to give Asha "a good death". The sheets never show it turning into wanting Asha's life. That
turn is what makes her the scholar who *makes the ending possible* rather than a technician.

**Fix (applied):** Ch19 I04 adds one line after the words, spoken: *"I came to give you a good death. I would like to
give you a long, dull life instead."* It's dry, as her voice is, and never jokey.

## 7. Toma: his inherited faith, and Ama
**What he loses:** the faith he was raised in (lost in Ch15, not at the end), and Hesk, the Saint whose count broke it
(Ch20 U04). What he keeps is the tending (toma_okeru.md Needs). The Ending's beach rite is "only a few motes left to
rise, and he tends them anyway".

**The earning chain:**
- **Act I:** the burning stopped by Sefir (Ch6); Ivo (Ch9).
- **Act II:**
  - Cloudloom (Ch12);
  - "the end of the road" (Ch12 camp, optional);
  - he refuses Hale (Ch14);
  - the confession (Ch15);
  - on the tally (Ch15, optional).
- **Act III:**
  - Haluna lifts its own (Ch18, optional, flagged);
  - he thanks Ama (Ch20 U04);
  - he holds the line at the Cage (U07);
  - the beach rite (Ending).

**Verdict: earned, but thin if Ch18's Haluna lifting is skipped.** Then the Ending's beach rite arrives with no rehearsal.
**Fix (proposed, Narrative, act3_ending):** if `ch18_haluna_lifted` is unset, the beach rite opens with Toma asking
the villagers to turn their backs (one line), so the rite is taught on screen.

## 8. Checks that cross people
- **Nobody keeps everything, and somebody keeps something:**
  - Rhen keeps Kell's wick (E7);
  - Liss keeps the knowing how (the epilogue's windmills);
  - Pell keeps his name;
  - Ysolde keeps the words;
  - Toma keeps the tending;
  - Asha keeps her life and the dark sea.

  No one ends with nothing, and no one ends whole. **Pass.**
- **Humour never undercuts the loss** (pillar 1). The ending's two jokes, Kerrid's "Your father slouched." and Liss's
  "He'd have hated the trim." (Ch18), are warmth between people, said *after* the loss is seen. **Pass.**
- **Every loss is shown, not told.** Each one has a seen act:
  - the voice: E5, the breath let go;
  - the Kite: E4, landed by hand;
  - Ressa: U05, a turned back;
  - Corran: U06, the ring fading;
  - Kerrid: E3, last off the wall;
  - Dace: E2, said back;
  - Tobin: U00 with this audit's fix, the lamp seated.

  **Pass after the Liss fix.**
- **Optional-only links** (a player can skip them): the Ch10–Ch16 camps, Ch18's lifting, and Ch15's tally. Every arc
  above still has a critical-path chain without them (checked per person). **Pass.**
