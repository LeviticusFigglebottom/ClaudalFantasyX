**The rules this chapter must keep:**
1. **The reveal is Kerrid's own confession,** at sea, by his choice. **Nobody performs a rite on him, nobody attacks, nobody exposes him.** No Stilling, no Cantor's gesture over him, no weapon drawn (ADR 0016; kerrid.md). He tells them before anyone asks.
2. **Every mote at sea is held.** The shoal's dead and the motes leaving Kerrid's hands are `glimmer_held` cyan and drift **sideways**, toward him and from him. Nothing rises and nothing turns gold (color script `ch15`; ADR 0022 (g): his light lifts only at H09).
3. **No lamp is struck at night.** No warm source on the barge from the start of the chapter to the Hollowmere dawn: no lamp, no fire, no flame. Liss has wrapped every bound light in her gear in sailcloth (open_sea_ch15.md: skin takes only moon and sea).
4. **Kerrid's facts are exact** (ADR 0022 and its sweep notes):
   - he **kept the wall-gate** and opened it for Imra at every dawn and dusk; he never walked beside her;
   - he **held Dace's line at the rings** for eight low tides; they **did not drink together**;
   - Imra and Dace **never met** before the stair;
   - **Imra went down the stair for a diver she'd never met**;
   - Kerrid **doesn't know what became of Dace** after the stair;
   - he has **no rank, title or fame**, and no promise to anyone.
5. **Kerrid's long speech is the only one he gets** (voice guide: earned once, Ch15). It is plain, a fisher-watch captain's words. He doesn't perform wisdom, and there are no vows.
6. **Asha voices the lantern for the first time here,** and only here (ADR 0022 sweep-G B3; decisions_log B2): her lantern-man is the twin of Rhen's lantern.
7. **Toma's faith breaks quietly.** He stops saying "Be level", and he never says it again in Act II. Nothing theatrical.

## 0. The chapter in one paragraph

The bridal barge drifts south out of Ansedra's lagoon through the night with no lamp, because wedding barges never carry one and nobody dares strike one now. Tobin lies under Merel's bridal cloth, and the little light that came off him at the court still clings under the cloth, held there by his sister's hand. Something followed the first wake in four hundred years out of the lagoon, and before midnight its fin comes cutting round the barge in the dark: the Wakefin, fought by sea-glow alone. Past midnight the barge drifts over a shoal where an old Rising's drowned are still held under the water. Their light comes up off the sea and drifts toward Kerrid, the way held light drifts toward the sea, and a few motes begin to leave his hands through his rope-burns. Before anyone can ask, he sits down on the deck, holds his hands still, and tells them. Corrow, 1001: the Cantor he opened his gate for and the diver whose line he held, strangers to each other until his stair; a diver drowning under the stair and a Cantor going down for him; the gate held open; the line through his hands; Dace reaching back and missing; the watch he never came off. He says Rhen's name for the first time. Asha touches her comb and remembers the man with the lantern. Toma asks about his breath in Ullar, and Kerrid says yes. Nobody does anything to him. At dawn Liss sings a Saltwright current-song into the fog, and Hollowmere answers. She has to tell her father. Among the people his order burned, with Tobin's body on the dock behind him, Toma confesses the camp he stood in the line for, and Liss doesn't forgive him, but she lets him carry her brother to the pyre-raft.

**Emotional line:** numb flight (the dark, the cloth) → fear in the dark (the Wakefin) → exhaustion → wonder turning to dread (the shoal's light comes to him) → **the confession**: grief, a debt, a name said for the first time → stillness; nobody moves to harm him → a sung chart in the fog and an answer → the worst errand (Liss to her father) → a faith breaking out loud, then quietly → being allowed to carry something.

**What the chapter must prove:**
- **SPEC §3.5's mandated reveal:** Kerrid is one of the dead, revealed in Act II, by his own word, among friends, in no fight (ADR 0016's Ch15 row);
- "Somebody's still below." and the Ch8 fishwife made legible;
- the two halves of 1001 placed in Rhen's and Asha's hands (and what they don't know: what happened to Dace);
- Hollowmere's first visit as grief among allies, before the voyage to the Rim;
- Toma's faith breaking (toma_okeru.md);
- Liss's grief, the rawest in the game.

## 2. Beat detail

### B01. Unlamped (C, 1.5 min)
- **U01:** the barge out on the open sea, small and red-hulled on black water under a three-quarter moon behind thin cloud. The sea-glow is the brightest thing in the frame (color script `ch15`: moon 0.25, sea-glow 0.12). Ansedra is a faint gold smear on the north horizon and then it's gone. The bells stopped an hour ago.
- **U02:** the bow's **empty lamp hook** (open_sea_ch15.md). Rhen looks at it, at the tinder pouch on his belt, and doesn't strike it. (Nobody tells him not to. Everybody knows.)
- **U03:** the stern canopy: **Tobin's bier under Merel's bridal cloth**, wedding-red, gone grey in the moonlight. Under the cloth's edge **a few motes of held light still cling** to him, cyan and barely moving (color script `ch14`/`ch15`: `glimmer_held`). They came off him on the court's slabs and haven't gone anywhere. Liss sits beside the bier with one hand flat on the cloth, and the motes stay near her hand. (bible §2: grief anchors. Nobody names it.) Liss's gear lights are wrapped in sailcloth; one glows faintly through the weave.
- Sets `ch15_started`. No dialogue.

### B02. The deck at night (P, 4 min)
- The barge is the zone: a 16 × 7.5 m deck, the canopy and bier at the stern, the empty hook at the bow, punt poles laid down along the rails (open_sea_ch15.md). No fights until B03, no shrine (no land), no shop.
- **Talk to each** (one exchange apiece, ~150 words in all; all optional except where noted):
  - **Asha** (sitting against the rail, not sleeping): she starts to ask Rhen how he is, and stops (voice guide: in Act II she starts to stop doing that). *"No. I'm not going to ask about your night. Mine's awful. Sit down."*
  - **Toma** (at the bow, shield across his knees): *"Merel's father served at the Bastion with my commander. I have eaten at his table."* (Long pause.) *"I did not know a man could stand on steps and watch that."*
  - **Ysolde** (hair down since the court, the pin gone): *"I have been doing sums all night, Master Sallow. I keep getting the same number."* (Corran's line in the roll; Ch13.)
  - **Pell** (at the rail, facing the water, crest flat): *"The sea is full here. Under."* (He can hear the shoal ahead. B06 pays it.)
  - **Kerrid** (standing at the stern behind the bier, looking back north): no line. He nods at Rhen. That's all.
  - **Liss** (sets `ch15_sat_with_liss` if the player sits with her): she is quietly narrating the barge's construction to herself (her tell): *"Lacquer over cedar. Pegged, not nailed. Ansedrans peg everything. Tobin said they'd peg the sea down if they could."* If Rhen sits: she doesn't look at him. *"Don't. Don't say anything kind."* (A rhyme: Rhen's own Act I line, "Don't be kind about it.") He doesn't. They sit. **This is the only free action in B02 that changes anything:** Ch15's supper (B14) reads it.

### B03. The fin (C, 1 min)
- **U05:** a sound first: something cutting the water, a long hiss. The sea-glow brightens in a line off the starboard bow, and the line moves.
- **U06:** **the fin** rises out of the black water, a tall swept blade 2 m high, with a lit seam of sea-glow along it, circling the barge (wakefin.md). Kerrid has his anchor-cleaver off his back. Pell, very quietly: *"It followed the ripples."* (The first wake in four hundred years drew it out of the lagoon: the cost of moving water, too.)
- `@music silence` → `wakefin`.

### B04. Boss: the Wakefin (P, ~7 min)
See §5.

### B05. After (C, 1 min)
- **U07:** the Wakefin rolled belly-up and drifting away, its wake fading. The barge rocks and settles. Nobody talks. Rhen sits down where he stands. Asha sleeps sitting up against Toma's shield. Ysolde closes her eyes. Liss hasn't moved from the bier. **Kerrid is at the stern, standing**, as he always is at night ("I don't sleep. I sit." is later; tonight he stands). Pell is at the rail, and his crest lifts slowly. He is looking down into the water ahead.
- `unlamped`, stripped to its bed. No dialogue.

### B06. The shoal (C, 1.5 min)
- **K01:** 01:00. The barge drifts over shallower water: in the moonlight the sea below goes from black to a deep, faintly lit green. A shoal. Under the water **there are lights**: hundreds of small held motes lying on the sand and in the weed, still, cyan. These are an old Rising's drowned (a village that was here once; Narrative may name it in the codex), never ebbed and never lifted, held where they fell.
- **K02:** the motes **come up**. Not upward into the sky: they rise to the surface and come off the water and **drift sideways across the deck**, low, the way held light drifts toward the sea (rule 2), except that they aren't drifting toward the sea. **They are drifting toward Kerrid.**
- **K03:** Kerrid at the stern sees them coming. He doesn't step back. The first motes reach him and **orbit him slowly**, the way the mourners' motes orbited the kneeling at the Ebbing (act1_ch09 B04). They recognise one of their own.
- **K04:** **his hands.** Close on his forearms (the left wrapped in tarred cloth, the right bare): the rope-burns are glowing, brighter than the faint leak of Ch10 (art/characters/kerrid.md: "up to 0.6"), and **a few motes are leaving them**, coming off the scars sideways and drifting out to join the shoal's. His light is starting to go with them. The others are waking: Asha first (she can hear the dead: "like someone humming in the next room"), then Rhen.
- `kerrid_at_sea` enters on K04, very low (a bed for a voice, as `iskarra_vigil`; KERRID_STAIR far below: act2_finals.md). No dialogue.

### B07. The confession (C, ~5 min): the reveal
**Staging:** before anyone says a word, **Kerrid sits down** on the deck by the bier. It is the first time anyone in the party has seen him sit (kerrid.md's clue list: "he never sits"). He rests his forearms on his knees and **holds his hands still**, palms up, and the motes leaving them slow. The party gathers without being asked: Asha nearest, Rhen opposite him, Toma standing, Ysolde, Liss by the bier with her hand still on the cloth, and Pell at the rail with his back to the water. **Nobody draws anything, says any rite, or reaches for him** (rule 1). The shoal's motes hang round all of them now, low and slow.

**The speech** runs ~350 words in short blocks of 1–4 lines, broken by the others (SPEC §3.6: no lore dump over four lines; the voice guide gives Kerrid this one long speech). His `@face` runs from neutral through `guilty` to `grief` (art/characters/kerrid.md). The facts are rule 4's, exactly. Proposed shape, for M4-A2P-006 to script:

1. **K05** — Kerrid, before anyone asks: *"I'll tell it before you ask. You've been very patient."* (The dry gallows note starts here: kerrid.md, post-reveal.)
2. **K06** — the watch: *"I kept the Fisher Stair at Corrow. Fourteen years. Captain of the watch. Fishers, all of us. No rank. The wall's the Order's. The stair's ours."*
3. **K07** — the gate and the Cantor (to Asha, who is very still): *"Eleven years ago a Cantor came for the Coilspire trial. The path to the headland goes out through my gate. I opened it for her every dawn. Every dusk. We said good morning. She laughed at the wrong times."*
4. **K08** — the diver (to Rhen, who doesn't know yet where this is going): *"Same eight days I'd hired a Low Reaches lamp-diver. To re-seat the rings at the stair foot, under low water. I held his line. Eight low tides. He slouched."*
5. **K09** — the strangers: *"They never met. She was a Cantor with a Stillguard escort. He was a wright with his marks under his sleeves. I was the only man in Corrow who knew them both."*
6. **K10** — the storm: *"On the eighth day the sea went out and came back early. He was under the stair foot at the last ring."* (Beat.) **"She heard a man drowning under my stair and went down for him. A diver she'd never met. Your father, Rhen."**
   - **The first time he says Rhen's name in the game** (voice guide: "should hit like a blow"). **K11:** Rhen's face. He doesn't joke. He doesn't speak.
7. **K12** — the gate, the stair: *"I held the gate past the hour. Went down after them. Got them both on the stair. She hummed to the children going up ahead of her. Kept them climbing."* (He doesn't say what she hummed. Rhen and Asha both know the tune: the player may realise it. Nobody says it.)
8. **K13** — the line: *"I was last on the hand-line when the sea came over. It ran through my hands."* He turns his palms a little in the light. *"I held it."*
9. **K14** — Dace's reach (to Rhen; `guilty`: gaze down, then makes himself look up): **"Your father reached back for me. He missed. I don't think he forgave himself."**
10. **K15** — after (plain): *"I woke at the next low tide, at the foot of the stair, with the drowned. Corrow had already lit my name. I'd stood on the wall twice while they Stilled one of us. I was gone before light."* ("Stilled" names the Order's rite as lore. It is said, never shown or performed: ADR 0016.)
11. **K16** — to Asha (`grief`): **"I got your mother up the stair. That's all I did. I got her up so she could walk to the Choir."**
    - Asha's hand goes to the **tortoiseshell comb** in her hair. **K17:** *"A man with a lantern like that brought me this. I was seven."* (Rule 6: the first time the lantern is voiced. She is looking at **Rhen's lantern**. Decisions_log B2: Dace's lanterns were a pair; hers was the twin.)
    - Rhen looks at his own lantern. He has nothing to say. (He doesn't know the comb's story; Ch17's ledger has it.)
12. **K18** — **Toma**, standing, very quietly, the Ch12 clue paid in two lines: **"Your breath. In Ullar."** Kerrid: **"Yes."**
    - Toma, after a long pause (if `ch08_fishwife_called` reached him through Marra at the Ebbing: act1_ch09 B01; on the critical path it did): *"The watch at Corrow tells it every Rising. The captain who held the stair. I served seven years on that wall and never knew his face."* Kerrid: *"Nobody did. That was the point of a watch."*
13. **The question nobody can answer:** Rhen, finally, hoarse: *"What happened to him? After?"* Kerrid: *"I don't know. I was in no state to ask."* (Dry, not a joke. Rule 4: he doesn't know. Rhen carries the half-story into Ch16: rhen_sallow.md.)
14. **"Somebody's still below."**, made legible (Rhen remembers the road; Kerrid looks south, past them, out to sea): *"She walked on to the Choir. A captain doesn't come off the wall while there's anyone below."* (ADR 0022 (e).) He doesn't say Imra is in the storm; he doesn't know what the Choir does (Ch16).

Sets `ch15_kerrid_told`. ~360 words, of which ~280 are Kerrid's.

**Optional lines, by flag** (each one line, if set):
- `ch05_heard_straw_cape_tale`: Rhen: *"The big man in the straw cape. The one who pulls them out."* Kerrid: *"Somebody has to."*
- `ch14_steps_held`: Kerrid, of the court: *"Held a stair again. At a wedding. Didn't expect that."*
- `ch08_fishwife_called`: Kerrid: *"Marra knew me. On the stair. I went down a step so she'd stop looking."*

### B08. Still below (E, 1.5 min)
- **K19:** nobody moves to do anything to him (rule 1). The silence holds. Then **Asha sits down beside him**, not as a Cantor, as a person, close enough that her shoulder touches his. She doesn't touch his hands. **The motes leaving his hands slow, and stop.** (Rendering: the emission from the scars eases back to Ch10's faint leak over ~6 s.) The shoal's motes drift on past the barge and away, still held, still sideways, and sink back to the shoal behind them.
- The player can **talk to each** (in-engine, one line apiece, ~70 words):
  - **Pell** (sets `ch15_pell_said_he_knew`): Liss, sharp, first words since the court: *"You knew?"* Pell: *"From the first day. It was his to say."*
  - **Ysolde:** *"Thank you for telling us, Kerrid."* (It's the first time she has used his name. She uses names exactly.)
  - **Toma:** he says nothing. When Kerrid looks at him, he nods once, as one watchman to another. No "Be level" (rule 7: it's gone).
  - **Rhen:** *"You knew who I was. In Pemakar."* Kerrid: *"Yes."* Rhen: *"You could've said."* Kerrid: *"You'd have asked me the end. I didn't have it."*
  - **Kerrid, last**, the line that opens his Act III voice: **"I have nothing left to hide. I may as well talk."** (kerrid.md.)
- **K20:** wide: the barge drifting on, the shoal behind it going dark, seven people round a bier, one of them dead and sitting down at last.

### B09. The current-song (C, 2 min)
- **H01:** 05:40, the open sea in thick grey fog. No horizon. The barge is lost in the white. Liss stands up from the bier for the first time since the court and goes to the bow.
- **H02:** she sings: a **Saltwright current-song**, a sung chart of the sea's currents (bible §7), unaccompanied, into the fog. Her voice is rough from the night. (It is a Hollowmere current-song, never the one in the storm: Narrative and Audio keep it distinct from `current_song`, Dace's, act1_ch08 §6.) **Rhen** turns at the first phrase: it's the same *family* of song as the one he heard in the storm. He doesn't say so. (A seed for Ch17; nothing more.)
- **H03:** a long silence. Then, out of the fog to the south, **an answer**: another voice, then many, singing the next phrase of the chart back, and then the dark shapes of lashed hulls coming out of the white, windmills turning on them. **Hollowmere answers** (`hollowmere_answers`, ~60 s: CURRENT_SONG). Liss doesn't cry. She keeps singing until the first hull is close enough to throw a line. Sets `ch15_hollowmere_answered`.
- No spoken dialogue.

### B10. The dock-master (C, 2 min)
- **H04:** Hollowmere at dawn, with the fog lifting to grey-rose and the first oil lamps lit on the hulls (color script `ch15`: "the Hollowmere dawn lifts to grey-rose with the first oil lamps"), the first warm light since the court. The barge is drawn into the dry-dock berths by the dock-master's own people.
- **H05:** **Liss's father**, the dock-master, keeper of the dry-dock berths (liss_varrow.md). A big, quiet, sun-dark man with line-marks to the shoulders and a dock-hook in his belt, at his hull-house door (`fathers_door`). He knows the barge is an Ansedran bridal barge. He knows who went to Ansedra to be married. He looks at the canopy, then at his daughter. (**Aldous Varrow**, approved by ADR 0204 §4. On screen he's mostly "the dock-master".)
- **H06:** Liss walks up the dock to him. **We don't hear it.** The camera stays on the barge, with the party, at the bier, at a distance: Liss talking, short; her father not moving; then his hand on the back of her head, pulling her face into his shoulder. Rule: **no dialogue is heard in this beat**. The player saw the death; the errand is hers. Sets `ch15_liss_told_father`.

### B11. Hollowmere (P, 5 min)

## 5. The boss: the Wakefin (B04)

**Lore justification (SPEC §6.7):** Undertow-spawn drawn by the first wake in four hundred years (act_outline; wakefin.md). It followed the bridal barge out of Ansedra's lagoon. The ripples that freed them called it.

**Arena:** `bridal_barge_night` (env-art, M3-ENV-720): the deck at y 0; the `BossAnchor` in the sea off the bow; open water to the horizon, the sea-glow, the stars; no lamp anywhere. Staged ids (M3-BPR-419): enemy `wakefin`, phase `wakefin_breaching`. The barge's floor must cut the body at the blade's root (wakefin.md: the arena needs a water surface at y 0, M3-CRE-626).

**The fight in the dark** (M4-A2P-062): the only light is the moon and the sea-glow. **Pillar 4 holds: the timeline shows everything.** The telegraphs are **sound and sea-glow**, never lamp light.

**Phases** (ADR 0204 §6 keeps the creatures-b lane's `close_circle`; Combat may cut it with sim data):
1. **Circling (100–50%).** Mostly the fin.
   - **The cut:** a telegraphed charge (one turn): a hiss off one side and the sea-glow brightening along its path. Then the fin sweeps the deck and strikes the **back** of the formation's side it came from. Pillar 4: the timeline names "The Cut", and the glow shows the side.
   - **The dive (`sound_dive`):** the fin goes under and the water goes dark, and the Wakefin gains **evasion up for a turn**, with a clear telegraph on the timeline and a ring of sea-glow marking where it will surface (ADR 0204 §3: evasion, **not** untargetable; pillar 4).
   - **One whole animal** (ADR 0204 §3): the fin is never a separate target and there are no deck adds; the Wakefin is one combatant.
   - **Role answer:** Rhen's precise strikes land when it surfaces, and turn manipulation moves the party's turns off the cut's.
2. **Close circle (`close_circle`, 50–25%).** It tightens its circle round the barge: the cut comes every other turn instead of every third, from alternating sides (the creatures-b lane's phase).
3. **Breaching (`wakefin_breaching`, below 25%).** It rises alongside to strike with the whole body, and **its spine wounds show** when it breaches (wakefin.md): a breach turn is the window, when Kerrid's armour break and big blows land.
   - It is also when it's most dangerous: the bill strikes a front-line hero hard.

**Rules:**
- `scripted: true` (no Escape: there's nowhere to go).
- **The bier is never hit** (staging: the canopy is behind the party line in the arena; no skill targets it).
- **Liss fights** (she isn't `unavailable`): the barge is her brother's bier, and she'll defend it.
- No random encounters on the barge before or after.

**Balance:** the Act II boss band (naive ≤ 3%, role_answer 55–80%, smart 70–90%, p90 ≤ 80) on the Ch15 loadout (M4-A2P-019).
**Music:** `wakefin` (two stems, rendered: M3-AUD-845); stem 2 on `close_circle`.
