# Act Outline: Prologue to Ending

Chapter-by-chapter beat sheet. Each chapter lists its **zones** (proposed `game/levels/<chapter>/<zone>/` names in brackets), its key beats, its boss, and its **optional emotional side scene** (SPEC §3.6). Colour-script cues follow SPEC §7.2.

**★ VERTICAL SLICE (M2)** is marked wherever it applies. The slice is **Chapters 2–3** at Haluna.

Zone count: 9 towns or hubs, 6 temples plus the Drowned Choir, and about 17 routes and dungeons. That's close to the SPEC §10.1 target and can be cut (see the cut notes at the end).

---

## Prologue: "The Last Time"
**Zone:** Iskarra Ruins, dusk [`prologue/iskarra_dusk`] (re-used in Ch19).
- Gold dusk light over drowned white towers. The party, all seven, sit around an oil-lamp fire on a broken plaza that juts above the water. Nobody speaks. Asha's verse-sleeve is fully embroidered.
- A short walk (a controls tutorial) across the plaza to the water's edge. The sea glows faintly with drowned motes.
- Rhen, in voice-over: **"Listen. This might be the last time I tell it."**
- Glimmer rises around him, and we cut to black and then back to Oruvel, *one month earlier*, with a title card.
- **Cinematics note:** this frame recurs at each act break as a short Rhen voice-over of one or two lines.

## ACT I: THE PILGRIMAGE
*Colour: warm, high noon, and festival.*

### Ch1 "Lamp-Diver"
**Zones:** Oruvel raft-town [`ch01/oruvel`], Oruvel Underway, a drowned-tower dive [`ch01/oruvel_underway`].
- Rhen on a salvage dive in a drowned tower, a tutorial dungeon in which the lantern lights the way. He surfaces to haggle and joke with the raft-town, which establishes his voice and the community that is about to die.
- The lintel-gulls cry. The Undertow rises out of a clear sky. Oruvel is torn apart.
- Rhen goes under, and deep in the dark water he hears his father's current-song. Then nothing.
- **Boss:** *Hullgnaw* (an Undertow-spawn eel grown through a ship's ribs), fought on a breaking raft. It's a tutorial for the turn-order timeline.
- **Side scene:** before the storm, an old diver, **Mother Kell**, gives Rhen a fresh wick "for luck." The wick returns in the post-credits scene.

### Ch2 "Salt on the Tongue" ★ VERTICAL SLICE
**Zones:** Haluna Beach and Village [`ch02/haluna`].
- **★ Cutscene A, "The Girl on the Beach":** Rhen wakes in the shallows at dawn. A young woman with a lantern-staff is kneeling over him. It's Asha. She smiles first, and Toma's spear is at Rhen's throat. Rhen's first words are a joke. Asha laughs, then notices the brass lantern on his shoulder and goes still for half a beat. *(Facial animation: the smile, the stillness, and the smile again.)*
- Haluna is preparing for Asha's departure. Rhen learns, in fragments and from overheard villagers, that she is the Cantor and leaves in two days. He doesn't yet understand what that means.
- **Town:** shops, an inn, a save shrine, and NPC state that changes after Ch3.
- **★ Side scene (optional), "Pila's Granddaughter":** Grandmother Osu shows Rhen the wake-shells in the shrine wall and tells him about her grandmother Pila, whose brother Teyo went as Cantor so she wouldn't have to. This pays off in the Ilun finale fight.

### Ch3 "The Shell Shrine" ★ VERTICAL SLICE
**Zones:** Saltgrass Causeway, a route [`ch03/saltgrass_causeway`]. Shell Shrine, a small temple [`ch03/shell_shrine`].
- **★ Route:** a tidal causeway of saltgrass and coral boulders, exposed at low tide with tidepools and wind, and random encounters (reef crabs, lantern-lure eels, and a palette-variant flier that teaches the player to use Rhen). Asha, Toma, and Rhen walk it together. Banter establishes all three voices.
- **★ Temple trial:** inside the shrine, rotate three shell-basins to redirect seawater through channels until the inner pool fills and the Vowbound chamber floods open (water-flow puzzle, §6.6). Hidden reward: a **net-float charm** for the player who drains the side pool.
- Asha enters the chamber alone, by rite. Rhen and Toma wait outside, and their first real conversation is about her ("Do you know what a Cantor is?" "...Someone who sings?").
- **★ Cutscene B, "The First Verse":** Asha emerges at sunset with Ilun's light still fading off her skin, the first band embroidered on her sleeve, and sings the first verse aloud for the first time. **It is the lullaby Dace hummed.** Rhen says "...where did you learn that?" before he can stop himself. *(Facial animation: Asha's exhausted joy and Rhen's slow recognition. Music: the first diegetic statement of the Hush theme.)*
- **★ Boss: Drownbell (two phases)** on the causeway as the tide comes in.
  - *Phase 1:* a bell-shaped Undertow-spawn (a jellyfish's hood over a crab's legs). Its **toll** delays the whole party on the timeline. Gimmick: break its rim (Rhen's precise strikes) to silence the toll.
  - *Phase 2:* the tide floods the causeway, and the bell submerges and drives spawn-polyps from below. Gimmick: the polyps heal it, and **summoning Ilun** (first use, full cinematic) clears the water field. This teaches the Vowbound mechanic.
- Return to Haluna at night. Rhen asks to come along as a guardian. Asha says yes before Toma can object.
- **Slice end card.** For M2 the slice ends here, with a Rhen voice-over: *"I said yes to a girl I'd known for two days. I didn't know yet what I was walking her toward."*

### Ch4 "The Pilgrim's Wake"
**Zones:** Haluna at night [`ch02/haluna` night variant]. Brightwater Crossing, the ferry deck at sea [`ch04/brightwater_ferry`].
- Haluna holds Asha's living funeral. Rhen finally understands that the Cantor doesn't come back, and leaves the feast angry. Asha finds him on the dock and comforts *him*. This is the first glimpse of her smiling to protect someone.
- Dawn ferry. **Ysolde** boards by Order appointment, cold and correct.
- **Boss:** *Spar-Wraith* (a spawn tangled in a dead ship's rigging) attacks the ferry. Ysolde's elemental magic is introduced.
- **Side scene:** Ysolde re-inks Asha's wake-shell by lamplight, alone.

### Ch5 "Terraces of Ash"
**Zones:** Pemakar Town [`ch05/pemakar`]. Ember Terrace temple [`ch05/ember_terrace`].
- Lanternfall festival: a thousand leaf-boats on the terrace channels, among the graves (pillar 2). Kerrid appears at the festival's edge, tells Asha "I travelled with your mother," and simply walks with them from then on.
- Temple trial: light glimmer channels in sequence up the stair-mountain. Asha binds **Kahre**.
- **Boss:** *Cinder Matron* (a magma-crusted temple guardian construct, two phases) breaks armour, which introduces Kerrid's niche.
- **Side scene:** a smith whose son drowned in Oruvel asks Rhen what the town looked like. Rhen tells him the truth, then a better version.

### Ch6 "Salt Edict"
**Zones:** Brine Shoals, a route of wrecks and mud-flats [`ch06/brine_shoals`].
- The party stumbles on an Order raid burning a Saltwright salvage camp. Toma freezes. The party shelters **Liss**, who escapes with a crate.
- **Boss:** *Stillguard Inquisitor and a raid-hound*. The party fights the Order's own for the first time, and Toma refuses to strike to kill.
- Liss joins on Toma's papers. She clocks Rhen's lantern as a Saltwright make immediately.
- **Side scene:** Liss buries a burned lamp at the tideline. She won't say whose name was on it.

### Ch7 "The Tidecup"
**Zones:** Tamsule port [`ch07/tamsule`]. The Tamsule Bell, the Reefball arena [`ch07/tamsule_bell`].
- Haluna's team is short a player. Toma captains, and Rhen dives. **The story Reefball match** (simplified, turn-paused, SPEC §6.10).
- **High Warden Sefir Aurane** blesses the Tidecup, warm and gracious. He tells Asha he knelt beside her mother. Toma is overjoyed. Liss notes aloud that the Bell is glimmer-tech, and nobody answers.
- **Side scene:** a Reefball veteran who lost his whole team in the last Rising asks Asha to bless the empty team benches.

### Ch8 "The Seawall"
**Zones:** Corrow Bastion and the seawall [`ch08/corrow`]. Coilspire temple [`ch08/coilspire`].
- Asha binds **Virel** at Coilspire (trial: route lightning through copper channels). Liss finds Wenna's scratched line-marks in the stone.
- The Undertow comes for Corrow. The Stillguard defend the wall with Liss's rigged Saltwright cannons. The Order uses them and thanks no one.
- **Set-piece:** the defence. The party holds a section of wall against waves of spawn. **Pell** is on the wall imitating the storm's sounds, luring spawn from fishers.
- **Boss:** *the Undertow's Reach*, a colossal limb of the storm (multi-phase, scripted escape).
- Rhen sees a figure inside the storm's wall and hears the current-song. He dives toward it. Kerrid drags him out.

### Ch9 "The Ebbing"
**Zones:** Corrow shoreline at night [`ch08/corrow` night variant].
- Hundreds of drowned. Asha performs her **first great Ebbing**, dancing on a raft as the motes follow her lantern-staff out to sea. It is the most beautiful sequence in Act I. (Rendering: motes visibly drift *down and seaward*.)
- Pell watches and says: *"Beautiful. And pointing the wrong way."* He joins.
- **Act break:** a Rhen voice-over at the Iskarra fire.
- **Side scene:** Toma finds an old Stillguard friend among the dead.

## ACT II: THE TRUTH
*Colour: overcast, storm, moonlight, and cold blue stone.*

### Ch10 "Fen of the Kirru"
**Zones:** Ossa Fen, mangrove channels [`ch10/ossa_fen`]. Reedhollow, the Kirru village [`ch10/reedhollow`].
- Pell isn't allowed in. The party witnesses a Kirru **farewell-tide** at dusk: the clutch sings and then turns its back, and the motes **lift**, straight up into the stars. Asha can't stop looking.
- **Boss:** *Mangrove Drowner* (spawn in the roots, two phases).
- **Side scene:** a Kirru child asks Asha why humans make their dead swim.

### Ch11 "Lowlight"
**Zones:** Sunken Vey, the drowned galleries [`ch11/sunken_vey`]. Lowlight temple, half underwater [`ch11/lowlight`].
- Liss finds Lit Age records showing the founders were *binders*. Rhen admits he is Dace's son and that he hid it. Liss is furious.
- Asha binds **Nhoa**, who is Ressa. Pell kneels before his sister and says nothing for a full scene.
- **Boss:** *the Lamp Engine*, a still-running Lit Age machine full of bound dead (two phases). Releasing its core makes a small upward lift, a foreshadowing.
- **Side scene:** Rhen and Liss repair a lamp together in silence. It's the start of their truce.

### Ch12 "The Cold Road"
**Zones:** Keth Pass, a mountain route [`ch12/keth_pass`]. Quellan, a highland town [`ch12/quellan`]. Cloudloom temple [`ch12/cloudloom`].
- The pass-cords. In the cold, Toma notices **Kerrid's breath doesn't fog**.
- Asha binds **Hesk**. Ama's frozen keth hang everywhere. Ysolde reads them and lies about what they say. That night she confesses to Asha: *"forty-seven voices, seven houses."*
- **Boss:** *Avalanche Ram* (a woolback grown monstrous on drowned glimmer from a meltwater lake).
- **Side scene:** an Ullari woman ties a knot for her son on the pass-cord and walks on. Rhen asks how she can. She says, "I carry the knot. He doesn't have to."

### Ch13 "Gold and Lacquer"
**Zones:** Ansedra, the holy city [`ch13/ansedra`]. Sanctum of the Level, the Holy temple [`ch13/sanctum`].
- A city of perfect stillness. Sefir hosts the party. Asha binds **Ethren**, and Ysolde stands before Corran with a Cantor beside her.
- The **peace wedding** is announced: Liss's brother **Tobin** will marry **Merel Dask**, a Warden's daughter. Liss is cautiously, painfully hopeful.
- **Side scene:** Ysolde shows Rhen the house she shared with Corran, now someone else's.

### Ch14 "The Peace Wedding"
**Zones:** the Lacquer Court [`ch13/ansedra_lacquer_court`].
- A gorgeous ceremony at dusk on still water. Sefir officiates. The Conclave springs the trap: Saltwright leaders are seized and the party is declared **heretics**. **Tobin is killed** shielding Merel.
- In the escape, the Stillguard perform a **Stilling** on Kerrid, and motes tear out of him. **The reveal.** He holds it off by will and tells them what he is.
- **Boss:** *Warden Malcor Venn* (Abiding, two phases). The second phase reveals that he is a revenant: his wounds don't bleed.
- No side scene; the chapter runs straight on.

### Ch15 "Heretics"
**Zones:** the Underreach, Ansedra's sunken under-city [`ch15/underreach`]. The Cistern [`ch15/cistern`].
- Fleeing below the city, they find the **Cistern**: the glimmer machines that hold Ansedra still, and the reservoir where "retired" Saints are consumed.
- Toma's faith breaks. Ysolde does the maths on Corran's fate. Toma confesses the camp burning to Liss. She lets him carry Tobin.
- **Boss:** *the Cistern Keeper*, a bound-dead construct (two phases).
- They escape by sea on a stolen barge.

### Ch16 "The Edge of the World"
**Zones:** the Pale Rim, white shallows [`ch16/pale_rim`]. The Drowned Choir [`ch16/drowned_choir`].
- Days of wading over white sand under a white sky. Kerrid finds the spot where he died.
- At the Choir, Asha binds **Kesh** and sees, through Kesh's memory, the founding and what the Hush truly does.
- **Sefir arrives** and explains reasonably. He offers Asha the chance to free her mother. Then he reveals that he wants her to sing *him* in. **Asha refuses.** Ysolde denounces him.
- **Boss:** *Sefir's Abiding guard*, then the storm wakes early as Imra's heart falters. Kerrid holds the line. The party is swept away.
- **Act break:** a Rhen voice-over.

## ACT III: THE UNBINDING
*Colour: dusk gold, then the glimmer abyss, then total darkness under stars.*

### Ch17 "Hollowmere"
**Zones:** the Hollowmere flotilla [`ch17/hollowmere`].
- Saltwright ships pulled the party from the sea. Tobin is mourned. Liss's father is broken.
- Dace's dry-dock and his unfinished ship. Rhen reads Dace's logs and finds his own name in every entry. Asha decides on the Unbinding. Ysolde begins reconstructing the Eighth Verse.
- **Side scene:** Rhen and Liss's father share a drink and two different memories of Dace.

### Ch18 "The Kite"
**Zones:** the Kite (a hub) [`ch18/kite`]. Reedhollow revisited.
- Maiden flight. **The world opens** (SPEC §6.6): revisit towns that have changed, Ultimate Weapon quests, and secret coordinates.
- **Required:** Pell returns to Reedhollow, is given a new name-shell, and teaches Asha the farewell-tide.
- **Optional flagged scene:** Toma leads Haluna in lifting its own dead the Kirru way.
- **Side scenes:** one per revisited town, showing how each town's grief has changed.

### Ch19 "Iskarra"
**Zones:** the Iskarra Ruins [`prologue/iskarra_dusk` full version]. Iskarra Lamp Spire, a dungeon [`ch19/iskarra_spire`].
- **The prologue catches up:** the dusk fire and the same shot, now understood. "Listen. This might be the last time I tell it."
- They climb the Lamp Spire to shut down the founders' anchor-engine, which opens a way into the storm's eye.
- **Boss:** *Warden Hesper Oan and Ilsabet Rue* (a revenant pair).
- Kerrid's last quiet night with Rhen.

### Ch20 "Undertow"
**Zones:** the Undertow layers, a multi-room dungeon of drowned memories [`ch20/undertow_layers`].
- They dive with the Kite into the eye. Each layer is a remembered place (an Oruvel market, a Corrow kitchen, a Reefball crowd) suspended in dark water.
- **Vowbound releases**, one per layer: Ilun, Kahre, Virel, Hesk, Nhoa, Ethren (see [vowbound.md](../vowbound.md)). Each leaves the roster for good.
- **Boss:** *the Cage*: the four bound founders, Orin Tesk, Halde Moor, Isse Varan, and Cael Imbrey, fought as one multi-part boss. They thank the party as they fall.

### Ch21 "The Heart"
**Zones:** the Heart of the Undertow [`ch21/heart`].
- Imra at the centre, and beside her, holding her light together for eleven years, **Dace Sallow**.
- **Kesh's release**, with Sefir watching.
- **Final boss: Sefir Aurane**, three phases: the Warden, the Undertow-heart, and **the Letting**. In the last phase Asha sings the Eighth Verse as a timeline countdown the party must protect.
- **The Great Unbinding.**

### Ending
- Everything lifts. Kerrid goes first: "Stand up straight. Your father slouched." Imra and Asha share a last scene of hands and silence. Dace and Rhen say goodbye, and this time Rhen says it back.
- The Kite goes dark, and Liss brings it down by hand.
- Asha tries to sing and nothing comes.
- **Final image:** Haluna dock at night, the sea completely dark, the stars reflected in it.
- **Post-credits:** Oruvel is rebuilt, and Mother Kell's wick is used to light the first new oil lamp.

## Cut Notes (for the Lead's scale fallback, SPEC §10.2)
If zones must be cut, cut in this order: Brightwater Crossing (merge into Ch4 as a cutscene), Keth Pass (fold into Quellan), the Brine Shoals route (move the raid to the Pemakar outskirts), then Ch19's Lamp Spire (shorten it to a single boss arena). **Never cut:** the slice, the Ebbing, the farewell-tide, the wedding, the Choir, any Vowbound release, or the ending.
