**The rules this chapter must keep:**
1. **The Choir truth is only what SPEC §3.5 gives it:** *what the Hush really does.* The singer becomes the storm's next heart (ADR 0016 (b): the founding itself is already known from Ch13's Archive). Kesh shows it **from inside**, and she shows the man who bound her in the stones.
2. **Sefir's temptation is to go home** (ADR 0016 (c)): "Leave the Eighth Verse in the stones. Go home to Haluna. Live." **He never asks anyone to sing him in.** He never says he means to take the heart himself ("I will see to the storm myself" hides it).
3. **Kerrid's line is said, never drawn** (ADR 0022 (h)). He draws no steel on Sefir and doesn't lead the refusal: Asha refuses, Ysolde denounces. He holds the line only once the storm wakes.
4. **No revenant is fought openly** (ADR 0016: first in Ch19). Sefir's Abiding guard are the High Warden's sworn guard, **living** men and women. Sefir himself doesn't fight. Nothing on screen says what Sefir is: the player knows from the Archive, and he never says it.
5. **The dead here are held.** The Rim's few motes and the storm's light are `glimmer_held` (color script `ch16`). Nothing lifts in this chapter. The act ends on held light; the Letting is Act III's.
6. **Kesh is bound but not called in Act II** (act2_finals.md: `kesh_summon` belongs to Act III's H03). She's in the roster from the act break, usable from Ch17.
7. **No Dace on screen.** Nothing at the Choir shows or names who is holding Imra's light (Ch21's). Rhen may hear the current-song in the waking storm, as in Ch1 and Ch8, and nothing more.

## 0. The chapter in one paragraph

A Hollowmere ship carries the party south until the water is too shallow to float it, and waits at the Pale Rim. Then they wade: days of ankle-deep and waist-deep water over white sand under a white sky, with no shadows and no horizon, marked only by old cairns, with things buried in the sand that wait to strike. Kerrid walks last in the line, as on a stair, behind Asha. At the end, at moonrise, the Drowned Choir: a ring of colossal stone singers standing in the sea, heads tilted up, mouths open, round a sunken amphitheatre. This is where every Cantor ends. Asha binds the last Saint, Kesh, the First Voice, bound in the stones by her own husband a thousand years ago. Through Kesh's memory Asha learns the one thing the Archive couldn't tell them: what the Hush does. The singer doesn't calm the storm. She becomes its next heart. And Asha's mother is the storm's heart right now. Then the High Warden arrives, gentle and reasonable, and offers her a gift: leave the Eighth Verse in the stones, go home to Haluna, live, and no Cantor will ever be named again. Ysolde asks who it takes, then. He smiles and doesn't answer. Asha refuses. Ysolde denounces him. Kerrid, who has just learned what he got Imra up his stair for, says it without shouting. Sefir's guard come for them on the dais, and in the fight the storm wakes early, because the heart in it is faltering. Kerrid holds the line while it comes, and the sea sweeps them all away.

**Emotional line:**
1. a voyage chosen;
2. the world going white (disorientation);
3. the watch walking last;
4. the ring of mouths at moonrise (dread);
5. the binding;
6. **the truth from inside** (horror, then grief);
7. Asha's mother, now;
8. a gentle offer (temptation);
9. "Then who does it take?";
10. refusal;
11. a fight;
12. the storm waking (terror);
13. a man holding a line;
14. the sea;
15. a voice in the dark, older.

**What the chapter must prove:**
- **SPEC §3.5's mandated beat:** "They reach the Drowned Choir and learn what the Hush really does." It's told by the *first* Cantor, who wants it ended (ADR 0016's Ch16 row);
- the seventh Vowbound bound (verse-sleeve **S7**: the seven verses complete; the Eighth is still in the stones);

## 2. Beat detail

### B01. The ship waits (C, 1 min)
- **W01:** day 1, 09:00. The Hollowmere ship (Ch15's lashed double hull, windmills turning) at anchor where the sea goes pale: water shading from blue-grey to white over sand. The ship's captain, one of the dock-master's people: *"This is as far as she floats. We'll wait. As long as it takes."* (act_outline: "A Hollowmere ship carries them as far as the shallows allow and waits at the Rim.")
- **W02:** the party goes over the side into knee-deep water. Liss leaves the padded box with Tobin's lamp on the ship (if act2_ch15 §10.1 stands): *"Keep it dry."* Kerrid is last over the side, as on a stair.

### B02. The white wade (P, 10 min)
- **The zone:** `ch16/pale_rim`: white sand shallows to the horizon under a white overcast noon, sandbars, old **cairns** at intervals (the greybox; `pale_rim_flats`). The lowest contrast in the game (color script `ch16`: 0.25): no shadows, and the horizon dissolves (pale_rim.md).
- **Structure:** three legs, one per day, each ending at a cairn where a short **montage cut** jumps a night (the party sleeping standing in the water at a cairn, or sitting on a sandbar: Kerrid standing watch, last).
- **Systems:**
  - random encounters (M4-C11-03: `glass_skate` ambushes, untargetable until it has acted once, so the lesson is to wait; `rim_crawler` swarms with no wall to hold, M4-A2P-021);
  - **no save shrine** (the Order builds none here); a Saltwright **slate-cairn** where travellers chalk their names is the save point at each day's end (plan §4.2);
  - **no shop**.
- **The line:** in the field the follower order is fixed for the chapter: Toma first, Pell, Asha, Ysolde, Liss, Rhen, **Kerrid last, behind Asha** (act_outline; ADR 0022 (h)). He walks last as on a stair.
- **Barks** (~120 words, one or two per day):
  1. Rhen, day 1: *"How do you know which way's forward?"* Pell: *"The water knows."* Rhen: *"Great. Ask it."*
  2. Ysolde, day 1, to Asha: *"Corran told me about this. He said it was the only time in his life he wasn't afraid. There was nothing left to look at."*
  3. Liss, day 2, counting cairns (her repair-narration, gentler now): *"Seven. Somebody stacked seven. Flat stones on the bottom. Somebody knew what they were doing."*
  4. Toma, day 2, without "Be level" (it's gone since Ch15): *"Every Cantor walked this. Every one of them. And their guardians walked back."*
  5. Asha, day 3, very quietly, to nobody: *"My mother walked here."* Kerrid, behind her, hears it and says nothing.
  6. Kerrid, day 3, post-reveal voice, dry, to Rhen: *"I've never been this far south. Dead eleven years and still finding new places."*
- Sets `ch16_reached_choir_edge` at the third cairn.

### B03. The ring of mouths (C, 1.5 min)
- **C01:** day 3, moonrise. The white sky has gone grey-blue, and the moon rises low in the east (color script `ch16` Choir variant: moonlit, stone faces cold). Out of the dusk ahead: **the Drowned Choir.** Ten colossal robed singers, 24 m tall, in weathered Lit Age white ceramic, stand in the sea in a ring, faces toward the centre, heads tilted up 25°, mouths deep open darks (pale_rim.md). They look past everything, up.
- **C02:** walking in between two singers, small, the moon between their heads. The sunken amphitheatre: a bowl of six broken seat-tiers, the lowest half-drowned, a flooded ring channel, and the singer's dais at its floor, dry. Held motes drift low over the channel, sideways (rule 5). The singers' open mouths make the wind into a very low chord, a sound you feel more than hear (Audio: `drowned_choir`).
- **C03:** Asha stops at the channel's edge. This is where every Cantor ends (bible §5.10). Her mother stood here.
  - Asha: *"This is it."* Nobody answers.

### B04. Kesh (C, 2 min)
- **C04:** Asha crosses to the dais. The others stay at the channel's edge, the guardians' place, as every party before them must have. Kerrid stands last behind them (ADR 0022 (h)).
- **C05:** she sings the six verses she has. The sea in the bowl answers: **Kesh rises** from the amphitheatre's sunken floor, a colossal woman of **standing seawater**, her robe a hundred open choir-mouths, a **Lamplighter's lamp-key** at her belt (the keepsake; vowbound.md §7). She is bound light inside water: still, held, cyan (color script: "Kesh pale cyan"). She is enormous and very old, and her face is a woman's, tired beyond telling.
- **C06 (the binding; M4-A2P-071):** **the seventh verse** comes out of the stones, not out of Kesh: the singers' mouths, all ten at once, sing it, a thousand years of it (Audio: `kesh_bound`). The verse-sleeve grows to **S7**: the seven verses of the Hush complete. Asha's sleeve is full.
- **C07:** Kesh bends down to Asha, with her face close, water running, and puts a hand made of the sea flat on Asha's forehead. *(This isn't a rite performed on Asha. It's how Kesh shows her: a memory given by touch, which Narrative may keep or replace with a look.)* The world goes white. Sets `ch16_kesh_bound` (rule 6: she's bound; `kesh_summon` waits for Act III).

### B05. What the Hush does (C, 3 min): the memory sequence
**Staging:** Kesh's memory, from inside, a thousand years ago. **A new cinematic look** (M4-A2P-071): desaturated toward white, soft edges, sound underwater, and no UI. The same seven verses the player has heard all game, heard now from the other side. ~40 words in all, Kesh's voice only.

- **K01:** Iskarra drowning, the First Rising: white towers in a black sea, a storm wider than the sky (bible §2). Brief, overwhelming.
- **K02:** the Choir as it was, new and white, with six Lamplighters in a ring on the dais, singing, and **Kesh in the middle**, young, holding a lamp-key, unafraid. Among the six, a tall man with silver hair stands at her shoulder: **Sefir**, young, mortal, in love (sefir_aurane.md). The player who has seen him in Ch13 knows the face at once. Nobody names him.
- **K03:** Kesh sings, and the storm comes down to her. She walks out into it, into the sea, singing.
- **K04 (the truth, from inside):** **inside the storm.** Darkness lit by motes, millions, held, and Kesh at the centre of them with her arms out, and the light **closing round her**, holding to her, her own light becoming the thing that holds it all together. The storm goes quiet around her, not calmed but **held in place, by her**. *She is its heart.* (bible §2; ADR 0016: the singer becomes the storm's next heart.) Kesh's voice, the only words: *"It doesn't sleep. It holds on. To us."*
- **K05:** time passing inside: forty years in a few seconds. Then another voice singing far above, a second Cantor at the Choir, and **Kesh is pushed out**: the new singer's light takes the heart, and Kesh's is shoved out of the storm, spent, drifting up out of the sea at the Choir, **lifting**: for one moment her light starts to go up (the only rising light in the chapter, and it's a memory: rule 5 holds for the present).
- **K06:** **and a hand closes on it.** Sefir, older now (forty years: still mortal-looking), on the dais, weeping, catches her rising light in both hands and **won't let it go**. He presses it down into the dais's stones, singing something wrong, and it holds. She is bound in the stones. He stays kneeling on the dais with his hands on the stone.
  - Kesh's voice: *"He couldn't let me go."*
- **K07:** and in the same moment, a detail the player must see but nobody explains: as he binds her, a thread of her light runs **into his hands**, and his face, which was grey with grief, goes smooth and young again (bible §2: "drawing on a displaced heart keeps his own lamp burning. The Abiding begin that night"). **Rule 4:** it's shown, never said. The player from the Archive knows whose hand signed those pages.
- **K08:** white. Then, from the stones, a thousand years of waiting, Kesh's voice: *"Every one of them since. Every one."* (Sixty-one Cantors. The heart, pushed out and caught, each time.)
- Sets `ch16_learned_the_hush`.

### B06. "She's in it." (C, 1 min)
- **C08:** back on the dais at moonrise. Asha is on her knees. Kesh has sunk back into the bowl, a shape under the water. The others have run across the channel to her: Toma first.
- **C09:** Asha, looking up at the dark south sky, past the singers, where the storm sleeps beyond the edge of the world (asha_velamir.md: she learns her mother is the storm's heart *right now*):
  - Asha: *"She's in it. My mother. She's holding it. Right now."* (Beat.) *"And it's me next."*
  - Nobody can say anything. Rhen kneels down in the water beside her. He doesn't joke (Act III's "stops joking" starts here). Kerrid, behind them, looks south too, out to sea, the way he looked on the Corrow road (act1_ch09 D02): *somebody's still below* has a meaning now that he didn't know.
- `@music silence`. ~20 words.

### B07. The High Warden (C, 4 min)
- **C10:** across the white shallows, in the moonlight, a white barge comes **without a wake**, poled by four veiled guards: the High Warden's barge (act1_ch06: "his white barge is poled through the smoke and leaves no wake"). It grounds at the channel's edge. **Sefir** steps off it into ankle-deep water, unhurried, alone except for his guard: eight of them in veiled gold lacquer, who stay at the barge (rule 4: living sworn guards).
- **C11:** Sefir crosses to the dais. He is gentle, and he looks at Asha on her knees with real sorrow.
  - Sefir: *"You have seen it, then. I am sorry. There is no kind way to learn it, and I have tried to find one for a very long time."*
- **He explains, reasonably** (the voice guide's "complete paragraphs in miniature"; this is his one long speech, earned: voice guide; broken by the others every 2–3 lines, SPEC §3.6):
  - *"The storm is the dead, Lady Cantor. All of them who were never let go. It wants to come apart and drown everything. Somebody has to hold it together. A Cantor does. That is all the Hush has ever been."*
  - Toma: *"You told us it was mercy."*
  - Sefir: *"It is. It is the most merciful thing anyone has ever built. Thirteen years of life for hundreds of thousands, for one."*
- **C12: the temptation** (ADR 0016 (c); act_outline; **fixed**): **"Leave the Eighth Verse in the stones, Asha. Go home to Haluna. Live. No Cantor will ever be named again. Your mother sleeps, and is never spent. I will see to the storm myself."**
  - It is the Long Stillness offered as a gift: nobody pays, nobody is lost, and nothing ends (act_outline). He means every word of the gift. He says nothing about the price (rule 2).
- **C13:** **Ysolde** asks the only question (**fixed**): **"Then who does it take?"**
  - **Sefir smiles, and doesn't answer.** (act_outline.) Hold on the smile.
- **C14: Asha refuses** (her refusals are very short: voice guide; **fixed**): **"No."** (Beat.) **"No, High Warden. I won't."**
  - If `ch10_asha_answered_child`: one more line, her own words for the Kirru child's question: *"A child at Reedhollow asked me why we make our dead swim. I'm going to find out how to stop."*
- **C15: Ysolde denounces him** (her rage comes out quieter: voice guide; reads `ch13_read_cantor_roll`, `ch12_ysolde_confessed`): **"Forty-seven voices. Seven houses. Do the sum, High Warden. I already have."** (Beat.) *"Kept, Sanctum. To be spent at the next Calm. In your hand."* (If `ch12_ysolde_lied`: *"I lied once this year, for your sake. I am done."*)
  - Sefir, to her, kindly, his tell (he answers grief with logic): *"You loved him. So did I, in my way. He is kept."*
- **C16: Kerrid** (ADR 0022 (h); **fixed; rule 3**). He has been standing last behind them all. He doesn't come forward and doesn't touch his cleaver. He looks at Sefir across the dais and says it without shouting: **"I carried her up so you could do this."**
  - Sefir looks at him properly for the first time in the game, a long look at a dead fisher-watch captain whose face he never knew (sefir_aurane.md). *"Captain."* (He knows the cord. It's the only time Sefir acknowledges what Kerrid is, and he does it with a word of rank Kerrid never had in the Order. Kerrid doesn't answer.)
- **C17:** Sefir, to Asha, one last time, sorrowful: *"Then I am sorry for what comes next. I would have given you a long life."* He steps back toward his barge and lifts one hand, and his guard come off the barge into the water.
- **C18:** Asha stands up. Sets `ch16_refused_sefir`. ~230 words, of which Sefir's ~120.

### B10. Swept away (C, 1 min)

### B11. Act break (C, 1 min)

Then the cut to **P04**: the Prologue's plaza at Iskarra at dusk, the vigil frame (act1_ch09 B10's P02, deeper again): the seven apart, Rhen beside Asha, the burner's flame lower still. ## 5. The fights

### 5a. Boss: Sefir's Abiding guard (B08)
**Lore justification (SPEC §6.7):** the High Warden's own sworn guard, Stillguard elite in veiled gold lacquer, the "Abiding guard" in the Order's own word (they guard the Abiding's seat; they don't know what the Abiding are). They're **living** (rule 4; ADR 0016: no revenant is fought openly before Ch19). Sefir doesn't fight. He stands at his barge and watches.

**Arena:** `drowned_choir_ring` (env-art, M3-ENV-722): the dais at y 0, the flooded ring channel, ten singers at r 44, the moon rising between two of them. No `BossAnchor`: the guard is a group (pale_rim.md). It has no baseline yet (the inventory), so it needs its first approved render.

**The fight** (two waves in one battle; act2_finals.md: two stems, stem 2 is the storm):
1. **The Veiled** (four guards): disciplined, paired. Two **Bulwark** casters and two halberdiers who Slow ("settle") a target, the Order's craft of stillness as tactics, an echo of Ethren's kit turned against the party. Ysolde's magic breaks the casters; Toma's counters punish the halberds.
2. **The Captain of the Veil** (one, joins at the first wave's half): a duellist who reads the timeline. He **delays** the hero who just acted (pushes them back) and parries physical blows. Rhen's turn manipulation, and Pell's learned skills, are the answer.
- **Under the fight, the storm stirs:** every few rounds the singers' chord deepens and the held motes over the channel all drift south at once (a field cue, telegraphed on the timeline as "The Choir shudders"), a warning of B09.

**Balance:** the Act II boss band (naive ≤ 3%, role_answer 55–80%, smart 70–90%, p90 ≤ 80) on the Ch16 loadout (M4-A2P-019). `scripted: true` (no Escape).
**Music:** `abiding_guard` stem 1 (not yet written, M4-A2P-092).
