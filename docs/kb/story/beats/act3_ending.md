# Act III Ending Beat Sheet: Ch21 "The Heart" and the Ending

Owner: Narrative Director (M1-NAR-05). Reviewer: Cinematics (SPEC §12.3: does the staging serve the beat?).
Sources: [act outline Ch21 and Ending](act_outline.md), [N-16, N-17](../decisions_log.md) (M1-NAR-08), [bible §10](../bible.md) (the locked ending shape), [Vowbound §7 Kesh](../vowbound.md), [Sefir](../characters/sefir_aurane.md), [color script `ch21`, `ending_unbinding`, `ending_dock`, `post_credits`](../../art/color_script.md), [decisions_log](../decisions_log.md) N-01, N-04, N-11, N-14.

Written for the review-sweep-B finding that Ch21 and the Ending stack Kesh's release, a three-phase final boss, the Letting countdown, three goodbyes, the Kite landing, and Asha's failed song. Without a sheet, those would blur into one montage.

---

## 0. Rules for this sequence

1. **One goodbye per scene.** Each scene below says goodbye to exactly one person (or to one thing), or to nobody. No scene shares a goodbye, and no goodbye is cut against another.
2. **A rest beat between goodbyes.** Between the three family goodbyes (Imra, Dace, then Kerrid last: [ADR 0020](../../decisions/0020-kerrid-restaging.md) (e)) there is a short in-engine rest with no dialogue, so the player can breathe and each loss stays its own.
3. **The light tells the time.** After the Letting every mote rises (`glimmer_lift`, slow upward spirals). Every bound light in the world goes out in the same instant at H09, **the Kite's lamps and engines included** (SPEC §3.5, [N-16](../decisions_log.md)): the Kite flies the whole Ending dark, gliding on sail alone. The countdown needs no UI, and it is carried by three things: the sea-glow stepping down scene by scene (rule 4), the dead rising off the sea more thinly in each scene, and the Kite's slow descent toward the dark water. **Nothing is released after H09** (rule 7): those three are an ascent, not a release.
4. **Light grammar is never broken here.** Up = released. Nothing drifts down or seaward after H09. The `sea_glow` steps down across E1–E5: **E1 0.15 → E2 0.11 → E3 0.08 → E4 0.07 → E5 0.06**, eased linearly through the rests between them and **held at 0.06 through E5** (color script `ending_unbinding`). E5 holds at 0.06 rather than 0.04 so that it still reads as glow (about 1.45× on/off, against 1.3× at 0.04) and the cut to E6's 0.0 is itself visible ([ADR 0020](../../decisions/0020-kerrid-restaging.md); Rendering to confirm on the mood frame). It becomes exactly 0.0 only on the cut to E6. The first fully dark sea in a thousand years is the final image's, and no earlier scene may show it.
5. **No gilt line** in Act III ([N-04](../decisions_log.md)). Asha's verse-sleeve unravels into rising motes during the Letting (art `asha.md` state S8), so it is gone in every Ending scene.
6. **SPEC §3.5 is locked:** the Undertow ends forever; every glimmer-light and lingering soul is released in the same instant (that instant is H09, and everything after it is ascent, not release: rule 7), including Kerrid and Asha's mother (one last scene); Asha lives without her singing voice; Rhen loses his father for real and gets to say goodbye; the final image is Asha on a dock at night with the sea dark and the stars in it; post-credits, the first new oil lamp in a rebuilt city.
7. **Released at H09, rising after (SPEC §3.5's "in the same instant").** Every glimmer-light and every lingering soul in Maresh is **released** in the single instant of H09. Every bound thing lets go at once, and nothing is still held after it. Everything after H09 is the **ascent** of motes that were already released then. The dead still coming up out of the deep water in E1–E5 were freed at H09 and are rising through the sea to the surface. `sea_glow` from H09 on is those released motes, still in the water column on their way up. It is not bound or held light, and it does not stand for anyone still kept. Imra, Dace and Kerrid were released at H09 too, and their scenes are the time it takes them to rise. Kerrid, one of the unrisen, comes apart slowly from his wounds outward, so he is the last of the three to be gone; his "Stair's clear." is a report, not a release. No scene after H09 releases anything, and no staging or line may imply that something lets go later.
8. **Imra, Dace and Kerrid never share a frame** (review-sweep-G S11, [ADR 0022](../../decisions/0022-dace-imra-strangers-sweep-f.md) append note). The inspiring game closes on its three dead together, and ours must not. From H02 through E3 no shot, wide or close, in focus or not, holds all three of them. **H02:** the wide holds Imra, Dace and Rhen walking in; Kerrid's hand on Rhen's shoulder is seen only in Rhen's single. **E1:** the tight two-shot is Imra and Asha; Dace and Kerrid are out of frame. **E2:** Imra is gone; the look between Dace and Kerrid is a shot and a reverse (Dace's face with Kerrid off-frame, then Kerrid alone at the rail), never a three-shot and never a two-shot of the two of them. **E3:** Kerrid is framed with Rhen only. No line or caption groups the three either. **Scope (review-sweep-H S3):** the rule is guaranteed by authoring in every **C and E beat** (H02, H03's C parts, H05, H08, H09, E1–E3, and the authored intro and outro cameras of each P beat). In the **player-driven cameras** (the H01 walk, the fights in H03 (P), H04, H06 and H07, and R1's free camera) Kerrid may be in the active party and the camera is the player's, so the centre is **drawn as light, not figures**: from the battle floor and the R1 save point, Imra and Dace read only as the two close lamps of H01, a pair of light columns with no silhouette, face or hands, and their figures resolve only when an authored camera goes in close. **H05:** every shot that holds Imra and Dace (Sefir's reach past Dace into her light) keeps Kerrid off-frame. The party's reaction is a reverse looking away from the centre, with the lamps behind the camera, so Kerrid can be in it.

**Modes** (as in the [slice sheet](vertical_slice.md)): **P** playable, **C** letterboxed cutscene, **E** in-engine (no letterbox, minimal input). Minutes are a first-time player's.

## 2. Scene detail

### Ch21 "The Heart"

**H01. The last layer opens (P, 4 min).** After the Cage falls (Ch20), the last remembered room dissolves and the party walks a floor of held light toward the centre. *Staging intent:* everything in the frame drifts down and inward, toward the heart, and the party are the only things moving forward. One rail, no encounters: the walk is the threat. Far ahead, two lights stand close together. *Music:* `heart_approach`, a low drone with the Hush theme's first phrase played backwards, very slow.

**H02. Two lamps (C, 2.5 min).** Imra at the centre, her light holding the storm together, and Dace beside her, his hands in her light, keeping it whole. Dace can't turn or speak while he holds her. Rhen sees his father. *He walks, he doesn't run* (an answer to his Ch8 freeze). He stops an arm's length away because Kerrid's hand is on his shoulder. Sefir is already there, waiting, unhurried. **No goodbye here:** this is the withheld one, and the player should feel it held back. *Music:* `heart_approach` thins to `@music silence` on Sefir's first word. *Staging:* wide, then Rhen's face, then Dace's back (we don't see Dace's face until E2). The wide holds Imra, Dace and Rhen; Kerrid's hand on Rhen's shoulder is only in Rhen's single, so the three dead never share a frame (§0 rule 8).

**H03. Kesh (C 1 → P 6 → C 1.5).** Kesh has been in Asha's roster since Ch16 (`ch16_kesh_bound`). Asha calls her out of the sleeve one last time, and she is released as the Ch20 six were (act3_threads T-01, accepted by the Lead 2026-10-30; ADR 0204 §16). Sefir watches. The fight is as written in [vowbound.md §7](../vowbound.md): she fights at full strength because she wants them strong enough. **Goodbye: Kesh.** She lifts and doesn't look at him. *Staging intent:* in the release cutscene the camera stays on **Sefir's face**, which doesn't move, while her light rises out of the top of frame. We never cut to her. *Music:* `kesh_first_voice` (boss, all seven verse phrases in turn, one per element phase), then `release_sting`, the Vowbound release sting from Ch20, last and longest.

**R1. Seven bands (E, 1 min).** A save point (a dark Choir-stone). The party stands in silence. Asha looks at her full sleeve for the last time. One optional line per party member if the player talks to them, each under 10 words, none of them a goodbye. *Music:* `heart_rest`, the drone only.

**H04. Phase 1, the Warden (P, 6 min).** Sefir buffs, delays, and "settles" the timeline (freezes turn order: [sefir_aurane.md](../characters/sefir_aurane.md) battle notes). He talks during the fight, warmly and reasonably. *Staging:* he never moves his feet. *Music:* `sefir_warden`, a hummed congregation over strings, beautiful and airless (the Order hums).

**H05. He takes the heart (C, 1 min).** Sefir reaches past Dace into Imra's light and begins drawing the heart into himself. Dace holds on. *Staging:* the held motes reverse direction around Sefir, inward and down, the wrong way, faster. *Framing (§0 rule 8):* Sefir, Dace and Imra share the frame; Kerrid is off-frame in every shot that holds the two lamps. The party's reaction is a reverse looking out from the centre, with the lamps behind camera. *Music:* `sefir_warden` breaks into `sefir_heart`.

**H06. Phase 2, the Undertow-heart (P, 7 min).** He draws on the party's Vowbound-adjacent glimmer (none is left to summon, since every Vowbound has been released). *Music:* `sefir_heart`, the storm's own note under rock-fusion percussion.

**H07. Phase 3, the Letting (P, 5 min).** Asha steps off the battle timeline and sings the Eighth Verse. Her verse is a countdown icon the party must protect. *Staging intent:* her voice is the music, and the score follows her. Over the countdown the palette rotates from cool held to warm released (color script `ch21` phase 3). The sleeve unravels from the cuff upward over the last 6 s. The verse ends on *tash* (let go). *Music:* `the_letting`, diegetic, Asha's voice leading. **Original melody** (SPEC §2): the first verse's phrase turned upward.

**H08. Sefir lets go (C, 2 min).** **Goodbye: Sefir's, to Kesh.** Kesh, already lifting, doesn't take his hand. He finally watches her go, and he lets her. Then he lifts too, and his last expression is relief. No speech longer than two lines. *Staging:* his reflection in the water ripples for the first time in the game (his revenant tell, undone). *Music:* `the_letting` thinning to a single held note.

**H09. The Great Unbinding (C, 2 min).** The last *tash*. Every mote in Maresh lifts at once. Six cutaways, each 3 to 4 s, no people in close-up and no goodbyes: Iskarra's lamp-columns go dark; a Hollowmere tide-wheel stops; the Tamsule Bell's light leaves it; the Sanctum's mirror basin goes dark and ripples for the first time in four hundred years; Lowlight's conduits empty upward; the storm wall comes apart into rising light. Then the Kite, in a last wide shot: its lamps go dark in the same instant as everything else, its engines stop, and it rides the storm's last updraft out into open night on sail alone, unlit. *Music:* `the_letting`'s last phrase, then `unbinding_swell`: full choir and orchestra for the only time in the game, and the Hush theme finally resolves upward.

### Ending

Staged on the Kite's dark deck as it glides in a long, slow, descending spiral over the open sea at night. Liss holds it aloft on sail alone, while the sea gives up its dead in slow spirals (color script `ending_unbinding`). The dead who were held come up with the light. Imra and Dace come up out of the heart's light onto the deck, and Kerrid is already aboard, each framed apart (§0 rule 8). **Each farewell scene is lit only by the rising motes of the person leaving.** The Kite's lamps have been dark since H09. Other dead rise far off the rail and stay out of the key, and the sea-glow below is a background value that never lights a face.

**The order is Imra, Dace, then Kerrid last** ([ADR 0020](../../decisions/0020-kerrid-restaging.md) (e)). Imra must go before Dace, because his hands are only free once she has gone. Kerrid goes last by the Corrow stair-watch's law, *first down the stair, last up it*: the captain comes off the wall after everyone else. Imra and Dace were strangers until the night of the Corrow storm, when she went down Kerrid's stair into the sea for him ([ADR 0022](../../decisions/0022-dace-imra-strangers-sweep-f.md)). Nothing here stages them as a pair who travelled together.

**E1. Imra (C, 2.5 min). Goodbye: Imra.** Imra, free of the heart, kneels in front of Asha. Hands and silence: almost no words (two lines at most, Imra's). Imra hums the first phrase of the **Velamir lullaby** ([N-11](../decisions_log.md)), the one she hummed every night, once, for her daughter. Asha doesn't try to join her. She listens, and holds on. (The failed song is E5's, and it must not be spent here.) *Staging intent:* a tight two-shot, mostly hands. Imra's face is lit by her own lift and Asha's by her mother's. We hold on their joined hands after Imra's light has gone out of them. *Music:* `goodbye_imra`. The score drops out completely, and Imra's unaccompanied hum is the cue. A single piano note enters only after she is gone.

**R2. Rest: hands (E, 0.75 min).** Asha's hands, still held out. Ysolde comes and takes them, the mother-in-all-but-name beat (asha_velamir.md), wordless. *Music:* `ending_lift_bed`, the drone and a faint high shimmer, no melody.

**E2. Dace (C, 3 min). Goodbye: Dace.** With Imra gone, Dace's hands are free for the first time in eleven years, and he turns around. We see his face for the first time. He knows Rhen at once. **Dace and Kerrid: one look, no words** ([ADR 0022](../../decisions/0022-dace-imra-strangers-sweep-f.md) (i)): past Rhen's shoulder he sees Kerrid at the rail, and for one beat the reach he missed on the Corrow stair is in his face. Kerrid shakes his head once. Then Dace looks only at his son for the rest of the scene. (It is a look, not a goodbye, so rule 1 holds.) The scene is the voice guide's Rhen with the jokes gone: Dace says goodbye, and **this time Rhen says it back**. That is the payoff of his whole arc, so it must be said aloud, plainly, and not cut away from. Dace sees his old lantern on Rhen's shoulder, the first of his pair, the one he left on its hook at Oruvel (ADR 0022 sweep-H note), still the oil burner he fitted and not glimmer, and is glad (N-02). (This is the lantern's own oil fitting. Kell's wick is never fitted to it, on screen or in a line: it stays unburned for E7.) The lantern is **unlit** (Rhen put it out on the Kite's dark deck), so the farewell stays keyed only by Dace's own motes. *Staging intent:* a single slow push-in on the pair (a same-axis move, so a blend is allowed), then one reverse at the line "Goodbye, Dad." The look is the only other cut: from Dace's face, with Kerrid off-frame past Rhen's shoulder, to a reverse single of Kerrid alone at the rail for the shake of the head, and back to the push-in. Never a three-shot (§0 rule 8). **The rhyme with Ch8 B17** (ending_cost_audit §2, accepted by the Lead 2026-10-30): at Corrow the figure in the storm *turned away* (W22) and Rhen froze in close-up with no word (W23). Here Dace's turn *toward* him repeats W22's move on the same screen axis, mirrored, and the reverse at "Goodbye, Dad." uses W23's lens and distance on Rhen exactly. The close-up where he froze becomes the one where he speaks. The current-song is in the room as it was in W22, and this time it doesn't leave until after the line. *Music:* `goodbye_dace`. Dace's current-song from Ch1 on plucked strings, and inside it, for four bars, the lullaby tune, the thing that tied two families together without either child knowing.

**R3. Rest: where his father stood (E, 0.5 min).** No dialogue. Rhen stands where Dace stood. Kerrid comes and stands at his shoulder, not touching, the way his hand held Rhen back in H02. The player can only turn the camera. *Music:* `ending_lift_bed`.

**E3. Kerrid (C, 2 min). Goodbye: Kerrid, last off the wall.** He has been coming apart since H09, slowly, from the rope-burns on his hands outward (released then, like everyone: §0 rule 7). His motes are **`glimmer_lift`** throughout: his rope-burns' held cyan made color rule 1's single 1.5 s lerp to lift at H09, so no held light shows in the last farewell ([ADR 0022](../../decisions/0022-dace-imra-strangers-sweep-f.md) (g)). He looks over the rail at the sea once, the way a captain checks a stair, and reports the stair-watch's all-clear: "Stair's clear." Then, to Rhen, a small thing: "Stand up straight. Your father slouched." It lands harder because the player has just seen Dace. *Staging intent:* a dry, plain goodbye, with gallows humour and no speech (voice guide). He fixes Rhen's collar the way he grabbed it at Corrow. As his motes lift, his own light throws **his shadow** across the deck and onto Rhen's feet, and for a moment the shadow is clearer than the man. He is there, for a moment, on the way out. The camera stays at Rhen's height. *Music:* `goodbye_kerrid`, a solo low string with a gamelan pulse, the walking rhythm of the pilgrimage, slowing to a stop. As the last farewell, its stop is the end of the walking.

**R4. Rest: the empty place (E, 0.75 min).** No dialogue. Rhen sits where Kerrid stood. Toma sits beside him, not touching. The deck is empty of the dead. Only a few motes still rise off the sea, far below and much nearer than before. The player can only turn the camera. *Music:* the bed thins to nothing.

**E4. The Kite comes down (E, 2 min). No person's goodbye:** this is the cost to the Saltwrights. The Kite has been gliding dark since H09, and now the glide runs out. Liss, who has already said goodbye to Wenna (Ch20), brings it down onto the water by hand, with ropes and the sails Dace modelled on Virel. One player input: hold to take the strain with her. *Staging intent:* sweat, rope, and darkness, and a hard landing that everyone survives. Liss doesn't cry until it's down. *Music:* `@music silence`. Only wind, rope creak, and the hull meeting water.

**E5. Nothing comes (C, 1.5 min). Goodbye: her voice.** On the dark, floating Kite, Asha tries to sing, properly, for the first time since the Letting. She draws breath and **nothing comes**. Rhen doesn't comfort her with words. He sits beside her. The `sea_glow` is at its last faint 0.06 here (held there, not 0.04, so the glow still reads and the cut to E6's 0.0 is visible): the very last of the risen dead are leaving the water around them, and the sea is nearly dark, but **not yet**. Its first full dark is held back for the cut to E6. *Music:* `silence`. This is the quietest scene in the game.

**E6. Final image: the dark sea (C, 1.5 min).** Haluna's dock at night (color script `ending_dock`; the H10 dock, with the camera on the planks looking out to sea, which is the opposite direction to H10). Asha at the end of the dock, alive, with a **second reach-band** on her forearm for the voyage she wasn't meant to come back from (N-01). The sea is completely dark, and the stars are reflected in it. **She draws breath for the tune, lets it go unsung, and smiles** ([N-14](../decisions_log.md)). She never sings or hums again, and the smile isn't for anyone else this time. Rhen's older voice-over returns from the dock: one or two lines, gentle, still a little funny (rhen_sallow.md Ending), ≤ 25 words each. *Staging intent:* one locked shot, **front three-quarter on her face** ([N-17](../decisions_log.md), [N-19](../decisions_log.md)). The camera sits on the dock planks about 4 m shoreward of her, at her eye height (about 1.5 m), on a 50 mm lens, looking out to sea. **Blocking ([ADR 0020](../../decisions/0020-kerrid-restaging.md)):** the shot opens with her standing **across the dock end, side-on to the lens**, looking out over the dark water to **frame left**, with her left shoulder toward camera. (She does not face straight out to sea: from a shoreward camera that would be her back.) As she draws breath she turns about 50° to her left, toward the shore, to look back at Haluna, at home, and holds there: her nose line is then **about 40° off the lens axis**, toward camera, so the camera is forward of her shoulder line and her whole face reads in front three-quarter. Behind her is the open sea, black, with the stars reflected in it. The breath, the unsung tune and the smile play to that angle. No cut, no push. The stars do the work. *Music:* `ending_dock`, solo piano: the Hush theme's first phrase, which resolves at last. The instrument carries it, never a voice.

**CR. Credits (7 min).** *Music:* `credits_suite`, a suite of the game's themes, ending on the lullaby phrase on piano.
- **The beach rite** (a credits vignette, in-engine, ~20 s; toma_okeru.md Ending; ending_cost_audit §7, accepted by the Lead 2026-10-30): dawn after the Unbinding, Haluna's beach, the village with their backs to the water and Toma among them. Only a few motes are left to rise, and he tends them anyway. **If `ch18_haluna_lifted` is set,** no line: they already know how. **If it is unset** (the player skipped Ch18's lifting), the vignette opens with Toma teaching it, one line under the music: *"Backs to the water. Like this. They go easier when nobody watches."* Then the same shot. The sea-glow here is the last of it, before E6's dark (§0 rule 4).

**E7. Post-credits: the first lamp (C, 1 min).** Oruvel rebuilt, pre-dawn blue hour. Someone uses **Mother Kell's wick** (Ch1) to light the first new oil lamp. It is the only warm light in frame (color script `post_credits`). No dialogue. *Music:* `post_credits_lamp`, a single low metallophone phrase from `haluna_dawn` and one struck match.

## 3. Requests to other roles

- **Cinematics (reviewer):** E1–E3 are each a single scene with one goodbye and a rest beat after it. Please check that the rests read as rests and not as dead air. They depend on the camera being free in E-mode.
- **Audio:** the cue ids above are proposals: `heart_approach`, `heart_rest`, `kesh_first_voice`, `release_sting` (shared with Ch20), `sefir_warden`, `sefir_heart`, `the_letting`, `unbinding_swell`, `goodbye_imra` (mostly silence plus a hummed phrase), `goodbye_dace`, `goodbye_kerrid` (now the last farewell, ADR 0020), `ending_lift_bed`, `ending_dock`, `credits_suite`, `post_credits_lamp`. Every melody is original (SPEC §2). `the_letting` and Imra's hum both need a voice decision (SPEC §16 Q4/Q5).
- **Combat:** H07's countdown (the Letting as a protected timeline entry); H06 runs with zero Vowbound available.
- **Art and Rendering:** the Kite's lamps going dark in H09 with everything else (§0 rule 3; there is no dimming curve); the stepped `sea_glow` (§0 rule 4), held at 0.06 through E5 and 0.0 only from the cut to E6 (please confirm on the mood frame that E5 → E6 reads as a step to dark); Kerrid's shadow from his own rising light in E3; the farewells keyed only by the leaving person's motes; Sefir's reflection rippling in H08; the E6 front three-quarter frame on the dock (Asha turned back toward Haluna, the sea behind her).
- **Systems:** E4 needs one hold input inside an in-engine scene.
