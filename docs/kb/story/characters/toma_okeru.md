# Toma Okeru

**Role:** Asha's sworn guardian and a former Stillguard. **Age:** 31. **From:** Haluna.
**Battle niche:** tank and defender who counterattacks.

## Background
Toma is a Haluna fisher's son who joined the Stillguard at sixteen because it was the most good a strong boy could do. He served eight years at Corrow and Ansedra. He was a believer, sincere and disciplined, and well liked. When Asha was named Cantor, Haluna asked him to come home and take the guardian oath. He set down his Stillguard rank to do it. Before that, though, he was on the line at a Saltwright camp burning when he was twenty-two. He did what he was told. He has never told anyone, and he still dreams about a girl's line-marks under a brine-brand.

He played Reefball for Haluna as a boy. He hasn't played since he took the Stillguard oath.

## Want / Need
- **Wants:** to bring Asha to the Drowned Choir safely and with honour, as his faith says.
- **Needs:** a faith that doesn't need anyone to die. His faith has to break and be rebuilt into something truer: *the Still Water was never the god; the tending was.*

## Arc Beats
- **Act I:** steady and courteous, and the most devout in the party. He corrects Rhen's ignorance of rites with patience instead of contempt. At the Brine Shoals (Ch6) he freezes at the burning, and then the High Warden himself stops it: his faith is handed back to him in the same hour, and he is overjoyed. At the Tidecup (Ch7) he watches from the galleries beside Asha while Rhen dives for Oruvel ([ADR 0017](../../decisions/0017-act-one-restaging.md)). At Corrow (Ch8) he reunites with old Stillguard comrades and holds the Fisher Stair alongside them. When Liss joins (Ch6) he is the most uncomfortable and the most polite.
- **Act II:** cracks start at the Tamsule Bell's hypocrisy and widen at Cloudloom. At the wedding (Ch14) he is ordered by a former commander to stand aside. He doesn't. In the Lamp Archive (Ch13) he watches Ysolde read the *spent* column. At Hollowmere (Ch15), among the people his order burned, with Tobin's body on the deck behind him, his faith breaks completely ([ADR 0016](../../decisions/0016-act-two-restaging.md)). He confesses the camp burning to Liss. She doesn't forgive him, but she lets him carry her brother's body.
- **Act III:** he becomes the party's conscience in a new shape. He organises the Haluna villagers to lift their own dead in the Kirru way before the finale (an optional-but-flagged scene). He holds the line against the Cage-Wardens in the dive.
- **Ending:** he leads the first open lifting rite on Haluna's beach after the Unbinding. Only a few motes are left to rise, and he tends them anyway.

## Silhouette and Costume
- The broadest silhouette in the party, with a wide stance.
- **Asymmetry:** one massive **red-lacquered shell pauldron** on the left shoulder, a holdover from his Stillguard armour. He has chiselled the Level emblem off it, leaving a bright scar in the lacquer. The right shoulder is bare.
- A wide indigo sash, wrapped trousers, and bare forearms with dense Haluna reach-bands. A pale band of skin on his wrist where an Order prayer-cord used to be.
- **Signature props:** a great round **tide-shield** of layered turtle-shell and lacquer on his back, and a hooked boarding spear.
- Hair: cropped close, with a short beard.
- Palette: red lacquer, indigo, bronze skin, and bone.

## Battle Tie-in
Toma *takes the hit and answers it.* His skills draw aggro (Provoke), raise Protect, counter physical attacks, and cover allies under the shield. A defender who returns force mirrors a believer who keeps the faith by acting on it. **Suggested Swell input:** a *hold-and-release brace*: hold through incoming hits on the timeline and release on the counter.

## Relationships
- **Asha:** devoted, protective, and occasionally overbearing. The hardest thing he does is let her choose.
- **Rhen:** begins as rivals, becomes brothers. Rhen makes him laugh, and Toma makes Rhen stand still.
- **Liss:** guilt and slowly earned respect. Their Act II scene is one of the game's emotional peaks.
- **Kerrid:** Toma knew Kerrid by reputation: every Stillguard cadet learns how the captain of the Corrow wall held the Fisher Stair in 1001 and died at its foot ([ADR 0020](../../decisions/0020-kerrid-restaging.md)). Toma is the first to suspect what Kerrid is and the last to say it.
- **Ysolde:** a fellow believer at first. As both their faiths fall, they become the two people who understand exactly what it costs.
