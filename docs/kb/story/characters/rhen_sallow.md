# Rhen Sallow

**Role:** protagonist and narrator. **Age:** 19. **From:** Oruvel raft-town, Low Reaches.
**Battle niche:** fast striker with speed and turn manipulation.

## Background
Rhen is a lamp-diver: he goes down into drowned Lit Age towers with an oil lantern and a tether, cuts out pre-Edict brass and glass, and sells it to anyone who pays. That includes Order-licensed dealers and, quietly, smugglers. His mother, an Oruvel net-mender, died of fever when he was six. His father, **Dace Sallow**, was a Saltwright who married into Oruvel and hid his line-marks under long sleeves. Dace taught Rhen to dive, to read currents by song, and a lullaby tune Rhen never knew the source of. It is the first verse of the Hush, kept as a lullaby by the Velamir women of Haluna. Dace heard a girl hum it on Haluna's dock when he was a young wright trading along the Low Reaches, years before Rhen was born, and never learned her name. It was Imra, long before she was named. He hummed it to Rhen every night after ([decisions_log N-11](../decisions_log.md), [ADR 0022](../../decisions/0022-dace-imra-strangers-sweep-f.md)).

When Rhen was eight, Dace went up the coast for a season's wall-work at Corrow and never came back. The one message that reached Oruvel was to **Mother Kell**, the old diver two hulls along: he was "helping a friend on a long trip", and would she keep the boy. She did. Kell raised Rhen from eight to nineteen, taught him to haggle, and never once said a hard word about his father. Oruvel said Dace drowned in the Undertow. (What happened: on the Corrow stair a Cantor he had never met went down into the sea for him, and the stair's captain died holding it for them both. On the climb the sea tore Dace's sleeves away, and Imra's Stillguard escort saw his line-marks. By morning the Bastion was asking the low town for the marked diver, and a hunted Saltwright who went home would bring the Order to the raft-town and to his son. So he didn't go home. He carried her comb to her daughter, began a hull at Hollowmere among his own people to follow her, and sailed into the storm after her when it went down to sleep. He never walked her pilgrimage. Rhen learns all of this from Dace's logs in Ch17. [ADR 0022](../../decisions/0022-dace-imra-strangers-sweep-f.md) and its review-sweep-G append note.) Rhen decided his father had *chosen* to leave, because being abandoned hurt less than having lost him. He has been not-thinking about it ever since. He wears his father's diving lantern on his shoulder and tells people it was a good trade.

He doesn't know he is half Saltwright by blood, or rather he knows and never says it.

## Want / Need
- **Wants:** to keep moving. Get paid, make people laugh, and never stand still long enough for anything to catch up.
- **Needs:** to stop running from loss. He has to stand in front of the thing he lost, his father, and say goodbye properly.

## Arc Beats
- **Prologue:** at Iskarra at dusk, steady and quiet. He walks among the six others, keeping their separate vigils, and sits down beside Asha to light his lantern ([ADR 0017](../../decisions/0017-act-one-restaging.md)). "Everyone I'm going to tell you about is still here tonight. Remember that, later." ([ADR 0020](../../decisions/0020-kerrid-restaging.md) (g)) We meet the man he becomes before the boy he was.
- **Act I:** brash, curious, and a joke for every fear. The Undertow takes Oruvel in Ch1 and he hears his father's current-song inside it. He washes up at Haluna and joins Asha's guardians half on impulse and half because her song is his father's lullaby. He learns what a Cantor is in pieces and gets angry when it lands (Ch4, Pilgrim's Wake). On the crossing he boards a dead trader to bring its name-board home, the first thing he has ever salvaged for nothing. At the Tidecup (Ch7) he dives for Oruvel's side and has to tell its captain, Mother Kell's grandson, that Kell, who raised him, couldn't swim. At Corrow (Ch8) he sees a figure in the storm and freezes. The figure turns away, and Kerrid pulls him off the wall's edge.
- **Act II:** Liss outs his Saltwright blood (Ch6 seeds it, Ch11 he admits it). In Ch15 Kerrid tells him that Asha's mother went down the Corrow stair into the sea for his father, a stranger. Kerrid doesn't know what became of Dace after the stair, and Rhen has to carry the half-story into the Choir.
- **Act III:** he stops joking in the moments that matter. At Hollowmere (Ch17) he reads Dace's logs in the unfinished hull that becomes the Kite and finds his own name in every entry. The logs tell him why Dace never came home (the Order had his marks from the stair, and a marked man brings the Order with him) and that Dace went into the Undertow on purpose, after Imra, to repay the debt. His father didn't abandon him: he stayed away to keep the Order off his son. Then he paid a stranger's debt and chose someone else's daughter's mother over ever coming home. Rhen has to hold both truths at once. Inside the storm he finds Dace still holding Imra's heart together. He doesn't ask his father to come back. He says goodbye.
- **Ending:** the narrator voice returns, now from the dock: older and gentler, still funny.

## Silhouette and Costume
- A lean, quick build with a forward-leaning stance.
- **Asymmetry:** a short sun-faded sea-jacket with only the **left sleeve**, which runs long into a fingerless diving gauntlet. The right arm is bare to the shoulder and carries a faint, half-finished reach-band.
- **Signature prop:** Dace's brass-and-salt-glass **diving lantern**, clipped to a strap on his right shoulder blade. It reads instantly in silhouette as a bulb over one shoulder.
- A coil of tether-rope on his left hip, wrapped sandals, and calf wraps.
- Hair: short and wind-ruffled, dark at the roots and sun-bleached copper at the tips.
- Weapon: twin **reef-hooks** (short hooked blades), worn crossed at the small of his back.
- Palette: sun-faded teal and sand with brass accents.

## Battle Tie-in
Rhen's moves are diver's moves: in, strike, out before the current turns. His skills shorten his own delay or push enemies back on the timeline ("Undercut," "Kick Off the Wall"). He is the elegant answer to fliers and fast evasive enemies. **Suggested Swell input:** a *timing ring* shaped like a lantern flicker, where you hit the light at its brightest.

## Relationships
- **Asha:** her song is his father's lullaby. He is drawn to her first by that mystery, then by her. The love story stays slow and nearly unspoken until Act III.
- **Liss:** a foil and sparring partner. She salvages for meaning and he salvages for money. She sees the Saltwright in him before he will.
- **Kerrid:** he resents the man at first, who knew his father and won't talk about him, then comes to trust him as a father-shaped figure. In Ch15 Kerrid tells him Dace was his hired diver, that he held Dace's line at the stair foot for eight low tides, and that Dace reached back down the Fisher Stair for him and missed. In the ending Kerrid goes last, after Dace ([ADR 0020](../../decisions/0020-kerrid-restaging.md)): the first goodbye Rhen says after he has learned how.
- **Toma:** an easy big-brother friendship that begins as rivalry over who protects Asha.
- **Dace (offscreen father):** the absence his whole arc bends around.
- **Mother Kell:** the old Oruvel diver who kept him from eight, at Dace's one message. She gives him the fresh wick before the storm (Ch1) and drowns in it. Her grandson Tavi captains Oruvel's Reefball side (Ch7).
