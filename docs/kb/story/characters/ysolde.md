# Ysolde Ysel

**Role:** Asha's appointed Lamptongue tutor and a black mage. **Age:** 47. **From:** Ansedra.
**Battle niche:** elemental magic.

## Background
Ysolde was born to a minor Ansedran Order family and was the best Lamptongue scholar of her year. She married **Corran Ysel**, a Tender and a hearer, for love, which surprised everyone. When Corran was named the 60th Cantor, she walked his pilgrimage as his tutor and his wife. She watched him sing at the Drowned Choir twenty-four years ago.

When Imra sang in 1001, the Order told Ysolde that Corran had been "raised to Sainthood" as the Vowbound **Ethren** in the Sanctum of the Level. Ysolde has visited his temple every year since. She believes he is at peace, that his sacrifice gave thirteen years of life to hundreds of thousands, and that the pilgrimage is the most merciful thing the world has. She chose to tutor Asha in order to make Asha's road as gentle as Corran's was not.

She is cold because warmth would make it unbearable.

## Want / Need
- **Wants:** to give Asha a good death: well-taught, unafraid, and meaningful.
- **Needs:** to let Corran go, both his memory and his Saint, and to turn her grief into protecting the living.

## Arc Beats
- **Act I:** joins at the ferry after Haluna (Ch4) by Order appointment. She is exacting, correct, and witheringly polite. She stands apart at the Pilgrim's Wake, then quietly fixes the ink on Asha's wake-shell. At Corrow she is magnificent in battle and, in private, the only one who does not cry at the Ebbing.
- **Act II:** at Cloudloom, Ama Quillo's keth contradict scripture. Ysolde can read them, and she lies to the party about what they say. That night she confesses the lie to Asha. At the Sanctum (Ch13) she lets Asha bind Ethren, and she stands before her husband's spirit for the first time with a Cantor beside her. In the Lamp Archive (Ch13) she reads the Cantor roll and does the maths: Corran is *kept, Sanctum*, and the margin says *to be spent at the next Calm* ([ADR 0016](../../decisions/0016-act-two-restaging.md)). **She becomes the pilgrimage's fiercest critic**, coldly and precisely, and her anger frightens even Sefir's Wardens. She denounces Sefir to his face at the Choir.
- **Act III:** she reconstructs the Eighth Verse with Asha: the words from the Choir stones, the tune read through Pell's farewell-tide, and its place from Ama's question-knot in the keth (act3_threads T-04). She is the scholar who makes the ending possible.
- **Ending:** she releases Ethren in the dive, the hardest Vowbound fight in the game for her. She says goodbye to Corran. In the epilogue she is teaching Lamptongue in Haluna, the words only, never the tune.

## Silhouette and Costume
- Tall and very straight, with a long vertical silhouette.
- **Mourning dress:** Maresh mourning colour is deep indigo-black. A long split robe with a high, stiff, flared collar framing the head, and a single streak of salt-white ash in her hair at the left temple, which she keeps deliberately (an Ansedran widow's custom).
- **Asymmetry:** **long lacquered ink-guards** (curved metal nail-sheaths) on the fingers of her left hand only. These are scholar's tools for marking Lamptongue on lacquer, and she casts with that hand.
- **Signature prop:** a **folding lacquer book**, accordion-pleated black panels painted in gold script, which she unfolds into a fan-arc to cast.
- Hair: pinned high with a single silver **Level-pin**. In Act II she removes it, and her hair comes partly down.
- Palette: indigo-black, salt-white, and gold script.

## Battle Tie-in
She commands all elements with precision and casts from a distance. She is the elegant answer to magical and elemental enemies. Her late skills read and exploit weaknesses ("Correct Reading"). **Suggested Swell input:** a *glyph trace*: trace a Lamptongue glyph on the folding book in a short time window (Combat may substitute a dice/tile mechanic).

## Relationships
- **Asha:** a pupil, then a daughter in all but name. Asha refuses to fear her, and that disarms her.
- **Corran (Ethren):** her husband, the hinge of her arc.
- **Toma:** a fellow believer who falls alongside her.
- **Rhen:** irritation, then wry fondness. He is the only one who makes her laugh, which she resents.
- **Sefir:** her spiritual father for decades. His betrayal is personal.
