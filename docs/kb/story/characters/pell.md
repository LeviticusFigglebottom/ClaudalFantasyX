# Pell

**Role:** a Kirru outcast. **Age:** about 120 (Kirru middle age). **From:** Reedhollow, Ossa Fen.
**Battle niche:** learns enemy abilities by listening to them.

## Background
Pell and **Ressa** were clutch-siblings, hatched the same season and inseparable for fifty years. Ressa was a hearer, the only Kirru in history known to hear glimmer, and she came to believe she could end the Undertow from inside it. Against her clutch's wishes she petitioned the Order. To everyone's astonishment, the Order made her the 56th Cantor, a political display of the faith's mercy.

When Ressa went into the storm about seventy years ago, Pell would not sing the farewell-tide. He stood at the tideline facing the water, watching, which anchors the dead. When he refused to turn around, the elders scraped his name-shell blank and sent him away.

He has followed the Undertow ever since, alone, cataloguing it the Kirru way: by listening to it and imitating what he hears. Recently Kirru who die near the sea have stopped lifting, and he knows, without proof, that it is because of her.

## Want / Need
- **Wants:** to see the Undertow end, so that Ressa, and now his people, can lift.
- **Needs:** to turn around. The thing he refused to do seventy years ago is the thing the world needs: to let go, and to sing the farewell-tide for her.

## Arc Beats
- **Act I:** first seen at Corrow (Ch8) on the seawall during the attack, imitating the storm's own sounds to lure spawn away from fishers. He joins after the Ebbing (Ch9). He tells Asha that her dance is "beautiful, and pointing the wrong way." He doesn't explain.
- **Act II:** at Reedhollow (Ch10) he isn't permitted to enter. The party sees the farewell-tide from a distance while Pell stands with his back to them. At Lowlight (Ch11), Ressa is the Vowbound **Nhoa**. He did not know she had survived as a Saint. He kneels in front of her and doesn't speak for a full scene.
- **Act III:** he teaches Asha the farewell-tide, the melody that holds the Eighth Verse. He returns to Reedhollow, and his name is carved again on a new shell when he tells the elders he will turn around at last.
- **Ending:** in the dive, Pell fights Nhoa and, as she lifts, **turns his back to her**. It is the most loving thing he can do. In the epilogue he lives at Reedhollow and sings at every tide.

## Silhouette and Costume
- **Unmistakable:** 2.3 m tall, long-necked, and lean. A narrow otter-like face with whiskers and a crest of fine heron feathers that rises when he is alert. Long webbed hands. Sleek dark fur, pale at the throat.
- **Costume:** a woven **reed mantle** over one shoulder (right), pinned with a bone toggle. Beaded cord wraps on the forearms. Otherwise very little, because the Kirru dislike clothing.
- **Signature detail:** a **name-shell** on a cord at his throat, scraped blank. In Act III it is replaced by a freshly carved one (a costume state change).
- **Weapon:** a long, light **fishing-staff**, a pole with a barbed bone tip and a counterweight, held like a wading bird's leg.
- Palette: wet dark brown, pale cream, reed gold, and bone. *Never* comic. Animate him with stillness and economy.

## Battle Tie-in
The Kirru learn by listening and imitating. Pell learns enemy abilities by *hearing* them used on him or nearby (a "Listen" stance that costs a turn of rank). He is the party's answer to enemies with exotic gimmicks: he turns a boss's tool against it. **Suggested Swell input:** a *tile/call-and-response* mechanic: repeat back a shown sequence of shell-tiles.

## Relationships
- **Asha:** he sees in her what he saw in Ressa, and this time he wants to be the one who lets go correctly.
- **Liss:** an unexpected friendship between two people who take things apart to understand them.
- **Kerrid:** a deep, wordless understanding between two who are outside life. Pell knows what Kerrid is from the first day (Kirru can smell anchored motes) and says nothing.
- **Ressa (Nhoa):** his sister, the reason for everything.
- **The Kirru elders:** exile and eventual return.
