# Old Kerrid

**Role:** a silent, scarred wandering swordsman, and secretly one of the **unrisen**. **Apparent age:** 44 (dead for eleven years). **From:** Corrow; formerly a Stillguard captain.
**Battle niche:** heavy hitter who breaks armour.

## Background
Kerrid was the most decorated Stillguard captain of his generation. Eleven years ago he guarded the Cantor **Imra Velamir**, alongside her friend **Dace Sallow**, a Saltwright. At the Drowned Choir, Kerrid understood too late what the Hush does. He drew steel on Sefir Aurane to stop it and was killed.

As he died, Imra was already singing. He had promised her two things: *watch over Asha*, and *if Dace does something foolish, bring his boy up right*. His motes would not lift. He woke on the white sand of the Pale Rim three days later, unrisen.

He has spent eleven years walking the edges of the world. He watched Asha from a distance. He went to Oruvel twice and couldn't bring himself to speak to Rhen. He is now fraying: his memory has gaps, and his wounds leak light.

## Want / Need
- **Wants:** to keep his promises before he comes apart.
- **Needs:** to accept that a kept promise ends something. He has to let himself be finished.

## Arc Beats
- **Act I:** joins at Pemakar (Ch5) unasked. He says only, "I travelled with your mother," to Asha, and nothing to Rhen. He is silent and capable. **Clues to plant:** he never eats; he declines inns; his breath never fogs in Ullar later; animals shy from him; he never stands near the Ebbing. At Corrow he hauls Rhen out of the storm (Ch8).
- **Act II:** in Ullar (Ch12) Toma notices the breath. Adrift on the unlamped bridal barge after the wedding (Ch15), the barge passes over a shoal of held dead, who drift toward him as held light drifts toward the sea, and a few of his own motes begin to leave his hands. **The reveal is his own confession**, not an exposure: nobody performs a rite on him and nobody attacks. He sits, holds his hands still, and finally speaks at length: what happened at the Choir, Imra, and Dace ([ADR 0016](../../decisions/0016-act-two-restaging.md)). After this he speaks more freely ("I have nothing left to hide. I may as well talk."). At the Choir (Ch16) he holds the line while the party escapes the waking storm.
- **Act III:** he is warmer, dryer, and almost funny. He teaches Rhen the last of what Dace taught *him*. He knows the Unbinding will lift him, and he volunteers to lead them into the storm.
- **Ending:** he lifts in the Great Unbinding, one of the first to go. His last line is to Rhen, and it is a small thing: "Stand up straight. Your father slouched."

## Silhouette and Costume
- Tall and heavy, with a hunched-forward weight like a man walking into wind.
- **Silhouette key:** a long, heavy **rain-cape of layered woven palm fibre**, a shaggy cascading form like a haystack on the shoulders, bound across the chest with old rope. It makes him look larger and older than he is.
- **Asymmetry:** a cloth **mask over the lower face** only on the left side, hiding a jaw scar that never healed (in Act II we learn it is a death-wound). A Stillguard captain's knotted cord hangs from the right hip, faded and deliberately kept.
- **Signature weapon:** an **anchor-cleaver**, a massive hooked blade forged from a ship's anchor fluke, carried bare across his back.
- Eyes: one clouded. In dim light his scars glow faintly gold (a subtle shader cue from Act I onward).
- Palette: dry straw, iron, faded Stillguard red, and bone.
- **IP note:** no hat, no coat worn off one arm, no eyewear, no gourd. Keep him clear of existing archetypes in this genre.

## Battle Tie-in
Kerrid breaks things: Armor Break, Power Break, and heavy high-rank blows that cost a lot of time but end fights. As one of the dead, he can later learn a skill that ignores the Zombie status and one that **trades his own HP for allies' Regen** ("Spend the Light"). **Suggested Swell input:** a *weighted swing*, a single long charge you release at the right moment, more power the longer you dare.

## Relationships
- **Asha:** a promise he keeps, and the daughter of the woman he failed.
- **Rhen:** a promise he nearly broke. Their relationship is the game's quiet spine.
- **Toma:** a former Stillguard who idolised him and now sees what the Order did to its best.
- **Sefir:** his killer. Their confrontation at the Choir is fury with no shouting.
- **Imra and Dace:** his closest friends, both in the storm.
