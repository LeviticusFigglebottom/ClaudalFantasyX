# Old Kerrid

**Role:** a silent, scarred wandering swordsman, and secretly one of the **unrisen**. **Apparent age:** 44 (dead for eleven years). **From:** Corrow; formerly the Stillguard captain of the Corrow wall.
**Battle niche:** heavy hitter who breaks armour.

## Background
*(Re-staged in [ADR 0020](../../decisions/0020-kerrid-restaging.md).)* Kerrid was the most decorated Stillguard captain of his generation, and he held the Corrow wall. Eleven years ago, in 1001 AL, the Cantor **Imra Velamir** came to Corrow for the Coilspire trial with her guardian **Dace Sallow**, a Saltwright. They were Kerrid's guests for eight days. He and Dace drank together, and Dace slouched.

Then the Undertow came for Corrow. Imra went down into the low town against her escort's orders to bring people up, because she could hear the drowning, and Dace went after her. Kerrid held the Fisher Stair's wall-gate open past the hour the rule allows, went down the stair himself, and got them both up. He was the last man on the stair, holding the hand-line for the last climbers, when the sea came over it. The line ran through his hands and burned them to the bone, and he held it. Dace reached back down for him and missed. The sea took Kerrid off the bottom step.

The Corrow stair-law is *first down the stair, last up it*: a captain doesn't come off the wall while anyone is still below. Kerrid never came off. His motes would not lift. He woke at the next low tide at the foot of the stair, among the drowned, unrisen. Corrow had already lit his name. He had performed the Stilling twice as a captain and knew what the Order would do with him, so he was gone before anyone saw him.

Imra walked on to the Choir and was spent. Dace, who had already lost one man off a stair, went into the storm after her. Kerrid has never been to the Pale Rim.

For eleven years he has walked the coasts. Wherever the sea takes someone (a swamped boat, a diver who doesn't surface, a child off a reef), he goes into the water. The Low Reaches tell an anonymous fisher's tale about **a big man in a straw cape** who pulls the drowning out and is gone before anyone can thank him. When a new Cantor was named, he started walking toward Corrow, because the storm always breaks at Coilspire in a Rising, and he meant to be at the stair. He never watched over Asha and never went looking for Rhen. He is now fraying: his memory has gaps, and his wounds leak light.

## Want / Need
- **Wants:** to be at the stair when the sea comes, and to get the last ones up before he comes apart.
- **Needs:** to accept that no wall and no stair will ever get everyone up, and to come off the wall. He has to let the watch end, and let himself be finished.

## Arc Beats
- **Act I:** at Pemakar (Ch5) he meets the pilgrimage on his way to Corrow. He sees Imra's face in Asha's, hears the name Sallow, and walks with them because they are going where he is going. He says only, "I held a stair for your mother once," to Asha, and nothing to Rhen. He is silent and capable. Nobody hails him, and Toma doesn't connect him with the dead captain until the Cold Road ([ADR 0017](../../decisions/0017-act-one-restaging.md)). At the Brine Shoals (Ch6) he keeps his back to Sefir's barge: the Order knows the dead Hero of Corrow's face. **Clues to plant:** he never eats; he declines inns; his breath never fogs in Ullar later; animals shy from him; he never stands near the Ebbing. At Corrow (Ch8) he works the **foot** of the Fisher Stair, waist-deep, pushing the last climbers up. An old fishwife on the stair stares at him and says "Captain?" He says nothing and goes down a step. He comes up the stair last, which is why he is there to haul Rhen off the wall's edge by the collar. He is gone from Corrow before the Ebbing. The player doesn't yet know this is where he died.
- **Act II:** in Ullar (Ch12) Toma notices the breath. Every Stillguard cadet learns the Fisher Stair of 1001, so Toma knows whose story it is. Adrift on the unlamped bridal barge after the wedding (Ch15), the barge passes over a shoal of held dead, who drift toward him as held light drifts toward the sea, and a few of his own motes begin to leave his hands, from the rope-burns. **The reveal is his own confession**, not an exposure: nobody performs a rite on him and nobody attacks. He sits, holds his hands still, and finally speaks at length about Corrow: eight days as Imra and Dace's host, the gate held open, the line through his hands, Dace's reach, and the watch he never came off ([ADR 0016](../../decisions/0016-act-two-restaging.md), [ADR 0020](../../decisions/0020-kerrid-restaging.md)). To Asha: "I got your mother up the stair. That's all I did. I got her up so she could walk to the Choir." To Rhen: "Your father reached back for me. He missed. I don't think he forgave himself." After this he speaks more freely ("I have nothing left to hide. I may as well talk."). On the Pale Rim (Ch16) he walks last in the line, as on a stair, toward the place he once sent Imra. At the Choir he learns from Kesh's memory what he saved her for. He faces Sefir with no shouting: "I carried her up so you could do this." Then he holds the line while the party escapes the waking storm, first down and last up.
- **Act III:** he is warmer, dryer, and almost funny. He teaches Rhen the last of what Dace taught *him* in eight days of drinking. He knows the Unbinding will lift him and that it is the first thing that will ever get everyone up. He leads them into the storm: first down the stair.
- **Ending:** he lifts in the Great Unbinding and is the **last** of the three to go, after Imra and Dace, because the captain comes off the wall last. Everyone was released at H09; he is simply the last of them to rise. He reports the Stillguard all-clear, "Stair's clear.", and then says a small thing to Rhen: "Stand up straight. Your father slouched."

## Silhouette and Costume
- Tall and heavy, with a hunched-forward weight like a man walking into wind.
- **Silhouette key:** a long, heavy **rain-cape of layered woven palm fibre**, a shaggy cascading form like a haystack on the shoulders, bound across the chest with old rope. It makes him look larger and older than he is.
- **Face:** bare and sea-weathered, with **nothing covered**: broad, with deep squint-lines, a nose broken flat years before he died, grey-black hair cropped like a soldier's, and a short beard. **Both eyes are clear** and grey.
- **Scars (his death-wounds):** rope-burns spiralling from palm to elbow on both arms, from the stair's hand-line. They never healed. In dim light they glow faintly gold (a subtle shader cue from Act I onward).
- **Asymmetry:** his **left forearm is wrapped** from knuckle to elbow in tarred hauling-cloth, because that hand took the rope's full run. The right forearm is bare and shows the paler spiral scar. A Stillguard captain's knotted cord hangs from the right hip, faded and deliberately kept.
- **Signature weapon:** an **anchor-cleaver**, a massive hooked blade forged from a ship's anchor fluke, carried bare across his back.
- Palette: dry straw, iron, faded Stillguard red, and bone, with tar-black on the wrapped arm.
- **IP note:** no hat, no coat worn off one arm, no eyewear, no gourd, **no mask or high collar over the face, and no eye effect** ([ADR 0020](../../decisions/0020-kerrid-restaging.md)). Keep him clear of existing archetypes in this genre.

## Battle Tie-in
Kerrid breaks things: Armor Break, Power Break, and heavy high-rank blows that cost a lot of time but end fights. As one of the dead, he can later learn a skill that ignores the Zombie status and one that **trades his own HP for allies' Regen** ("Spend the Light"). **Suggested Swell input:** a *weighted swing*, a single long charge you release at the right moment, more power the longer you dare.

## Relationships
- **Asha:** the daughter of the woman he got up the stair so that the Order could spend her.
- **Rhen:** the son of the man who reached back for him. Their relationship is the game's quiet spine.
- **Toma:** a former Stillguard who learned the Fisher Stair of 1001 as a cadet and idolised its captain, and who now sees what the Order did to its best.
- **Sefir:** the head of the Order that lit Kerrid's name as the Hero of Corrow and would Still him on sight. Sefir didn't kill him. Their confrontation at the Choir is fury with no shouting, and it is for Imra.
- **Imra and Dace:** his guests for eight days and the last two he got up the stair. Both are in the storm.
