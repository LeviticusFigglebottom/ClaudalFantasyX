# 0200 — Act I kits dress the greybox zones; the greybox keeps the gameplay

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Roles affected:** cinematics (act1-chapters), environment (env-art), systems (GreyboxZone), rendering
- **Author role:** cinematics (act1-chapters)

## Context
Ch4–Ch9 are greybox zones built from layout files (ADR 0035, `GreyboxZone`): boxes for the look *and* the
collision, plus spawns, triggers, marks, actors, shots and the flag-driven `states`. env-art has now built the Act I
kits (M3-ENV-01..11) as procedural `.glb` pieces with kit previews under `game/levels/_tests/<kit>_kit/`, and handed
each over with a swap-in note: copy the placements into the zone, never reference `_tests/` from a shipped scene.

The greybox's gameplay is tested end to end (`test_chNN_thread.gd`) and tuned (encounter rates, trigger sizes, the
ferry that is never attacked). The kits are art: sloped hards, channel beds, wreck hulls. The field controller has
no step-up (ADR 0035 Watch), so kit geometry can't simply become the walkable floor.

## Options
1. **Rebuild each zone around the kit** (kit collision, re-placed triggers and marks). Most faithful, but it
   re-opens every chapter's tuned and tested gameplay, and kit pieces export no collision proxies.
2. **Promote a per-kit dresser** (as Haluna's `HalunaDresser` / `haluna_dressing.tscn`). One script per zone, owned
   by Environment, and the story blocks (`Barge`, `Lamp`, `Smoke`) still need wiring to the states.
3. **A generic dressing child over the greybox.** One `ZoneKitDressing` node (a `HalunaDresser` subclass, so the
   same LOD chain and material-slot mapping) reads a per-zone dressing layout, places the kit, hides the greybox
   meshes and leaves their collision, triggers and states alone.

## Decision
Option 3.
- **`game/levels/act1/zone_kit_dressing.gd` (`ZoneKitDressing`)**, a child named `Dressing` in each zone's `.tscn`,
  with the zone's `Water` (`WaterSurface`) and `Tide` beside it when the kit has water.
- **The dressing layout** `game/levels/chNN/<zone>/<zone>_dressing.json` is generated by a script under
  `tools/act1/` from env-art's kit (bounds, preview placements), in the greybox zone's own frame. A grade the
  preview uses (Environment `.tres`, LUT) is copied next to the zone by the same script.
- **Story pieces** name their greybox block (`"block": "Barge"`) and follow its visibility, so `hide_block`,
  `show_block` and the `states` rows drive the kit. Decor off the route takes a box collider (`"collide": true`).
- **The greybox stays the gameplay:** its collision, triggers, marks and spawns are unchanged unless the kit
  demands a move (a spawn that would sit in water; a water strip the player mustn't walk on is a new solid
  block). Kit pieces sit near y = 0 (a raised hard is sunk) so the flat greybox floor still matches the ground.
- **Story blocks no kit has yet** stay visible greybox (`greybox.keep`) and go to env-art as a request task.
- **The zone's sun** takes the colour script's frame for the kit (the kit's grade expects it), and the dressing
  writes `hymn_sun_direction` from the zone's sun so the sky follows the story's light rows.
- **Shots:** each dressed zone gets a rig under `game/levels/_tests/act1_zones/` (`Act1ZoneRig`) and two
  registered baselines.

## Consequences
- Easier: every chapter's thread test still passes unchanged; a kit revision is one generator re-run; the kit
  never needs collision proxies.
- Harder: collision is coarser than the art (boxes, flat floors). Where the kit's shape matters to play (stairs,
  the Seawall's walk at y 12, the Tidecup galleries), that zone's layout must move its floor blocks to the kit's
  heights; the dressing layout's generator documents the anchors it used.
- Watch: a dressing piece must never cover a trigger or a mark (the thread tests can't see that; the shot rig
  can). Copied grades drift from the kit preview when env-art changes it: re-run the generator after a kit merge.
