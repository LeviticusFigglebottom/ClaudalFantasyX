## Done
- **M1-QA-10 → review (reviewer: lead).** The intermittent `asha_face_stylized_compare` failure (`Shader vertex input location (0) not provided…`, `pipeline.is_null()`, then `Attempted to free invalid ID: 0` and `Aborted`) is a **Godot 4.6.3 engine race**. It is not in our content.
  - `RenderingDevice::render_pipeline_create()` (worker threads) reads `vertex_formats` outside the RD lock, through `has()` and the non-const `operator[]`. `vertex_format_create()` (render thread) inserts into it under the lock. A lookup during a rehash default-inserts an empty layout, so validation reports location 0 missing. The waited ubershader pipeline stays `RID()`, which gives the invalid-ID free at exit. The empty entry's null driver id crashes `vertex_format_free()` at RD shutdown, which gives `Aborted`. Upstream 4.7 and master fixed this with a locked `getptr()`; the 4.6 branch did not.
  - Exonerated: the M1-RND-24 mouth-bounce pass, `CharacterShaderTiers` copies, and every mesh and material. The mask/placeholder paths in `SceneShaderForwardClustered` and `ShaderRD` were checked and can't produce it.
  - Proven with `tools/dev/rd_vertex_format_race/run.sh forced`: an isolated local-RD project plus a gdb script on the Ubuntu debuginfod symbols that forces the interleaving. It gives the exact message (`i` = 0, empty attribute vector), then SIGSEGV in `vertex_format_free` at RD shutdown. `run.sh plain` (no forcing) is clean.
- **Rate:** 0 in 90 lavapipe processes under load (stylized ×20 warm, ×20 cold, and the asha block in manifest order ×5 with a fresh `user://`). The only hit is the Lead's batch-3 gate.
