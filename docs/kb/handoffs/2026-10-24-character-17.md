## How to drive and blend (the contract)
- **Cycles:** move the root at `speed_mps`. To move at another ground speed, set the playback speed scale to
  ground speed / speed_mps. Rhen's shorter cycle gives him the quicker cadence; Toma's is the longest.
- **Exact joins, no crossfade needed:**
  - a start's first frame is `idle`'s first frame, and its last frame is the cycle's first frame (the left heel strike);
  - a stop's first frame is the cycle's first frame, and its last frame is `idle`'s first frame;
  - turns, `idle_breathe` and `idle_shift` begin and end on `idle`'s first frame.
  So the clean sequence is: idle (at its loop point) → start → cycle → (at the cycle's loop point, `foot_l_down`) →
  stop → idle.
- **Entering mid-cycle:** to start a stop or switch away from mid-cycle, crossfade (0.15 to 0.25 s). The feet will
  then slide a little for the length of the crossfade.
- **Starts and stops:** move the root by `root_travel_m[i]` at key i, interpolating between keys (the speed rises or
  falls smoothly). Turns: yaw the root by `root_yaw_deg[i]`.
  - If the root doesn't follow the curve, the planted feet will slide. The body never turns or travels on its own.
- Walk and run start with the left foot (the right foot pushes or stays down), and the stops end feet together at the
  idle's width.

## Known issues
- Clip keys are at 15 fps (the library's global FPS). The run's planted phase is only about 4 keys, so Godot's
  linear interpolation between keys can make the planted foot slip by a few millimetres between keys.
- Toma's walk now keeps his idle stance width (about 0.58 m between the ankles). The one-step walk_start and walk_stop
  can't change foot width, because the foot that stays down would have to slide. That makes his walk read broad; the
  run tracks narrower (its starts and stops step both feet).
- Rhen's and Toma's trouser hems ride the shins and can't take a deeply flexed ankle (the rig probe's skin-through-
  garment check). A planted foot whose shin leans past `STANCE_DORSI` (Rhen 6°, Toma 10°, Asha 25°) rolls onto its
  ball early, so their heels lift sooner than a natural walk.
- Rhen's right arm swings back less (`back_r` 0.35) so that it clears his hip pouch.
- Toma's skin-through-garment Body count sits exactly at its ratchet (200 at run@9). Any new Toma clip that bends the
  ankle will trip it.
