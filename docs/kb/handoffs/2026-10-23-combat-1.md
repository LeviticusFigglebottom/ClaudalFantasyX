## Done
- **Item KO prediction (CMB-07 AC1).** New `BattleEngine.item_leaves_standing(item, c)` (static) runs the item's effects in order on `BattleCombatant.sim_copy()` (new; HP, MP, statuses, affinities, equipment wards; never the timeline) and reports whether `c` is still `is_alive`. Item effects use no RNG, so it is exact. `item_downs(item, c)` = standing now and not after. `preview_item` now sets `FALLS` from `item_downs`, so a heal item that KOs a Zombie (ally or self) drops it from the order, and so does a self-petrify. The revive branch only adds `revived` for HP 0.
- **Defeat prediction (CMB-07 AC1 and the UI-04 latent bug).** `item_defeats_party` now asks "does anyone in the active party stand after the item?", using `item_leaves_standing` on hit targets and `is_alive` on the rest. A group revive that fells the last Zombie but revives a fallen ally is no longer a defeat. A heal that KOs the last Zombie is one. If every foe would be down, it returns false (victory first, as in `_check_outcome`).
- **delays_taken on rejoin (CMB-07 AC2).** It resets to 0 on a revive or petrify cure (`_resolve_item_on`). On a swap, the entrant takes a living leaver's count with its slot, so a swap can't launder diminishing returns, and starts at 0 when it replaces a fallen member. formulas.md § Time says so.
- **Ease ceiling on smart (CMB-07 AC3).** A win-rate ceiling can't work: smart is at 100.0%. targets.json has a new optional `mean_turns_min`: smart ≥ 16 mean turns (`sandbox_reef`, measured 17.9 / 18.1 at 1000 / 200 seeds) and ≥ 14.5 (`sandbox_reef_tier1`, measured 16.1 / 16.3). `sim.py` folds it into `in_band` and the Band column. balance_targets.md explains why turns and not KOs. `balance_report.md` was regenerated, and every number is unchanged bit-for-bit.
- **UI-04 sweep-E docs and nits.** economy.md: the petrify-cure rejoin now has the speed term (`_rejoin_time`; speed is read after all effects); "the last *living* active member"; the heal-KO is now predicted; the group-revive case; a fresh delay count on rejoin. formulas.md: the speed term in both petrify lines, plus a preview/defeat paragraph. Stale comments are fixed at battle_engine.gd `item_can_target` and item_battle_action.gd:8 (Progression's file: comment only).
