## Done
- **`BattleVowbound`** (`game/battle/battle_vowbound.gd`, Node-free, no random rolls). The engine owns one as `engine.vowbound`; `engine.summon(id)` and `engine.dismiss()` drive it.
  - **The call:** the whole active party stands down and the Vowbound takes the turn. The caller pays a `call_rank` delay when the party returns; the others keep their ticks.
  - **Dismissal** spends the Vowbound's turn.
  - **A fall is no defeat.** The party returns, and that Vowbound cannot be called again this battle.
  - **Victory while it is out** gives the field back first.
  - **Refused while it is out:** swap, escape and items.
  - **Its own Swell** fills from the HP it loses, and its finisher is `unleash()`.
  - **Signals:** `called` and `departed`.
