## Done
- **The game now applies GraphicsSettings.** New `game/core/graphics_runtime.gd` (`GraphicsRuntime`) owns the game's settings and applies them at three points:
  - **Boot.** `boot.gd` `_ready` calls `GraphicsRuntime.boot(get_viewport())`. It loads `user://settings.cfg` (falling back to the default preset), applies the Viewport and RenderingServer operations, and arms the per-scene hook.
  - **Zone loads.** `scene_router.gd` gained `attach_graphics_hook(node, stats)`, a small additive change in a Systems-owned file. `_swap` calls it before `change_scene_to_node`. On the new root's `ready` signal it applies the per-scene part:
    - the Environment gates and the `sky_detail` uniforms
    - the `vegetation_*` and `gi_voxel` groups
    - `apply_tiers` (shader families)
    - the water, weathering, Undertow, glimmer and character tiers

    The hook does nothing until boot has armed it, so tests, captures and gpu_bench render as before.
  - **Settings menu.** `GraphicsRuntime.select_preset(id, vp)` and `set_override(key, value, vp)` save the change and apply it at once. No graphics menu exists yet, so I filed **M1-UI-10** for UI.
- **Tests.** `tests/integration/test_graphics_runtime.gd`, 6 tests:
  - boot with Low saved, and boot with no saved file
  - Low on a fixture loaded through the router hook (sky, gates, three vegetation groups plus the foliage family, water, weathering, Undertow)
  - the hook disarmed before boot
  - menu select and override, saved to the file
  - the real Haluna zone (sky uniforms, water, weathering), with its shared resources restored afterwards
- **RND-12 S2**, measured with the new `tools/gpu_bench/sky_sun_probe.gd` on lavapipe:
  - Haluna's sun is **Light Only**, so `env_sky_cumulus` never sees it: `LIGHT0_ENABLED` is false and the clouds are lit from the zenith.
  - Turning the sun therefore changes neither the sky nor its radiance. That explains RND-12's "no `--spin-sun` cost".
  - With Light and Sky, the Incremental radiance does follow the sun, converging within about 64 frames.
  - The GPU checks are in graphics_settings.md, "Sky radiance and a turning sun".
- **Docs.** graphics_settings.md has two new sections: "Applying in the game (M1-ARC-15)" and "Sky radiance and a turning sun".

## Known issues
- On a non-default preset, the swapped shader variants (water, weathering, foliage and characters on Low) are not prewarmed. ShaderPrewarm stages the authored materials, so the swapped variants compile on first draw, under the fade.
- Characters spawned after a zone load must call `CharacterShaderTiers.apply_to_tree` themselves.
- **Pre-existing:** instancing `haluna.tscn` on the main thread and freeing it leaks resources at exit. It prints `p_elem->_root != this` errors, 38 resources in use and 3 dummy-shader RIDs.
  - I reproduced it on the base commit with a bare load-and-free test, without this change.
  - `test_graphics_runtime`'s Haluna test therefore prints these errors at exit. `godot_log_check` does not flag them.
  - Run alone, `test_haluna_zone.gd` doesn't show them; I did not find out why.
