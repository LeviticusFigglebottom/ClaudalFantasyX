## Asks for the battle track (choreo.json / BattleChoreo)
- **Clip length vs strike_sec.** BattleChoreo puts the contact at approach + contact_at × strike_sec, but plays a hero
  clip only at the battle speed. So these clips land later than the beat:
  - attack 0.33 s against melee 0.165 s;
  - cast 0.8 s against 0.4 s;
  - finisher 0.868 s against 0.341 s.

  Either play a hero strike clip at `speed × clip_length / strike_sec`, or, better, read the `contact` marker and move
  the contact to it, as `_move_contact` already does for a creature's `contact_time`. A hero-side
  `BattleClips.contact_sec` would return `anim.get_marker_time(&"contact")` when `anim.has_marker(&"contact")`.
- **One `finisher` clip, many contact_at values.** It plays for finisher 0.62 (Toma's breakwater), dive 0.5 (Rhen's
  lamp dive), verse 0.42 (Asha's verse), brace 0.82 (Toma's return tide), chant 0.8 and glyph 0.85. The clips use 0.62,
  so the marker approach above fixes all of them at once. Otherwise each technique needs its own clip name.
- **The same applies to `cast`.** It plays for cast 0.8 and support 0.7; the clip uses 0.8.
- **`ko`.** The clip ends kneeling on one knee, leaning on the weapon (or a hand, for Rhen), and holds there; it does
  not lie flat. `_beat_ko` lets the stage show a model's own KO. On a revive, `on_ko_state` only stands up a
  procedural KO, so please confirm that the stand-in's `set_ko(false)` puts a hero model back on `idle`.
