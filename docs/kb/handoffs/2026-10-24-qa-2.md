## Done
- **The shrine_* hang is a Godot 4.6.3 engine deadlock in `ShaderRD`. It is not our code, and not audio or the bake.** `version_get_shader()` holds a shader version's mutex across a plain (non-collaborative) wait for that version's compile group. Pool pipeline compiles (`ShaderData::_create_pipeline` -> `_get_shader_variant`) need the same mutex. When they fill every worker before the group's tasks start, nothing can move. I symbolised the Lead's hung process offline: its load base came from the plt call sites, and the frames resolve against the debuginfod symbols QA-10 already downloaded. That gives exactly this cycle, with all four workers on the mutex (`docs/kb/qa/shrine_hang_bt_symbolised.txt`).
- **Trigger:** Forward+'s advanced shader group is turned on lazily. The shrine is the only non-TAA VoxelGI shot, so it turns the group on with the whole hall loaded, and on lavapipe the main thread then waits up to 8.5 s at a time holding version mutexes (gdb trace).
- **Mitigation:** the shot key `"warm_shader_groups": true` (in `capture.gd`, on the five shrine shots). One empty TAA render through `force_draw(false, 0.0)` before the scene loads turns the group on first. The trace confirms the order.
- **capture.sh hang guard:** `HYMN_SHOT_TIMEOUT` (default 600 s) per shot process, killing only that process group. A hung shot is logged as `CAPTURE PROCESS EXIT 124 (hung: killed after N s): <id>` and fails the gate. Tests are in `tools/tests/test_capture_hang_guard.py`. I took M2-NIT-04's capture.sh and `test_capture_sh.py` from lead/batch4 (a91a6774) as is, so that merge is the same text.
- Filed M2-RND-14 (rendering): turn the group on at boot whatever the AA setting. That is the game-side exposure for TAA-off players.
- Docs: a new "Engine deadlock" section in `docs/kb/qa/screenshots.md`, and the M2-QA-02 notes.
