fig-choked side gallery), and `ch11/lowlight` (the half-drowned temple: the atrium's lamp bench, the Engine hall, the
well-shaft's two flights down past the waterline, the ledge over the drowned sanctum).

it. There is no swimming field mode. Narrative can overrule; M4-C11-01 asks.
