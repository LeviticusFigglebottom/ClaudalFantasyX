# Ch13 "Gold and Lacquer": draft (pre-gate)

**Zones:**
- `ch13/ansedra`: the still city: moorings, gold lamp-post causeways, bowl-domes doubled in the mirror lagoon, Sefir's
  house, Corran's old house.
- `ch13/sanctum`: the still basin lit from beneath.
- `ch13/lamp_archive`: the reading room between stacks.

**Greybox frame:**

  (46, -40).

**No fights in Ch13.** Its pacing is the city, the hospitality and three Archive days. Every scene ends on a walk the
player takes, never a cut straight into the next one.
