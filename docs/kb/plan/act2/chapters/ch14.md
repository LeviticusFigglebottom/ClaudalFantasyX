# Ch14 "The Peace Wedding": draft (pre-gate)

M4's named set piece (SPEC §10.2). There's no side scene: the chapter runs straight on.

**Zones:**
- `ch13/ansedra_lacquer_court`: the colonnade, the dais, the galleries, the Conclave's gallery, the water-steps, the
  bridal barge, the water-gate.
- **The lagoon at night**, for the escape. Plan default: `ch13/ansedra` under a `variants` night row on
  `ch14_steps_held` (window light across the watching city), with a `LagoonBarge` spawn on the bridal barge's deck.
  The barge is a moving set (a stage actor the escape scenes carry), so no second zone is built.

**Greybox frame:** lacquer_court has CourtEntry (13, -2.5) and Barge (0, 7); the `barge` touch at (0, 12) goes to
ch15/open_sea @ Deck (exists; it becomes the last step below).
