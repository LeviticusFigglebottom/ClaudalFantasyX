## Tuning log

### 2026-10-22: M1 sandbox softened (M1-CMB-05, combat-stream-2)

The sandbox was harsh: a naive party never won and wiped in about two rounds. The target is naive 5–15% with the role-answer swap at 85% or more. Enemy damage cuts alone barely moved naive, because naive spends the fight on the Reef Crab (the first foe, and the bulkiest) while the Gull Harrier's rank-2 Dive Rake (AGI 24) does most of the damage. So the fix trims the crab's bulk and the harrier's bite, with a light cut to the other two attackers.

What the numbers say:
- Naive is now in band, and a naive win still takes about 17 actions with roughly two members down.
- **Lowest HP and naive are coupled.** Any change that lets naive survive the crab also lets focus fire finish the two fragile attackers first, so lowest HP lands near 80% whenever naive is 5–15%. The swap reward is now the 14-point gap from lowest HP to role answer (it was 28). If playtests say swapping feels optional, widen the gap by giving the eel or harrier a trait only its answer handles cleanly (for example a physical-resist eel), not by raising damage again.
- The Crab's DEF 40 still halves Asha's Attack and keeps Toma's Shell Breaker the best opener (40 → 0 DEF is +40% damage for everyone after it).
- Follow-ups: rerun with a full Lattice petal (`BattleSim.run_one(..., party_modifier)`), as the M1-PRG-01 review asked; add per-skill usage to the report to watch Kick Off's tempo.
