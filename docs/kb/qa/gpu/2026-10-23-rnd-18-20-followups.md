## RND-18 S1: the deep lift is gated by the crest SSS

`water_surface_body.gdshaderinc`: `deep_lit = col_deep * (1 + scatter_view_lift * lift_gate * (1 - view_keep))`,
with `lift_gate = use_sss ? clamp(sss_strength / SSS_LIFT_TUNED_STRENGTH, 0, 1) : 0`. `SSS_LIFT_TUNED_STRENGTH` is
1.4, the default `sss_strength`, which is what the lift was tuned against. No material sets `sss_strength`, so every
SSS sea is unchanged. The storm sea (`storm_ocean_material.tres`, `use_sss` off) no longer brightens from above.

## RND-20 S1: bias 4.5 in the zones (measured; not applied)

The review asked for the stage's 4.5 to reach the game, because Haluna's sun is at 1.0. `shadow_normal_bias` is
measured in shadow texels, so I rendered Asha's smile, neutral and q34 shots with the stage's two lights given
Haluna's cascade settings: PSSM 4 splits, `directional_shadow_max_distance` 400, splits 0.04/0.12/0.35, blur 1.2,
bias 0.03. Commands: `capture_preset.tscn -- --preset=high --light-set=...,shadow_normal_bias=<b>`.

| Variant | Smile corner | Nose shadow | RMSE vs stage (smile / neutral / q34) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stage (8 m, bias 4.5) | clean | full | – |
| Haluna cascade, 1.0 | clean, no stripe | faint | 0.018 / 0.018 / 0.013 |
| Haluna cascade, 2.5 | clean | fainter | 0.019 / 0.020 / 0.013 |
| Haluna cascade, 4.5 | clean | gone | 0.021 / 0.022 / 0.015 |

- At face range, Haluna's first cascade (16 m) has texels about 10–20× the stage's. A bias of 1.0 is already a larger
  world offset than the stage's 4.5. Raising it only erases the face's remaining self-shadow (and would lift scenery
  shadows off their contacts).
- **So the Haluna sun stays at 1.0.** No edit to Environment's or First Playable's scenes. This also answers
  M2-FP-02 review S1 (2.5–3.0 on the Haluna sun).
- The real gap is that a 400 m zone cascade can't resolve a face's own shadows at dialogue range. That belongs to the
  **M2 dialogue rig**: a character key light with a few metres of shadow range and bias 4.5, as on the stage.
- The rule is now in character_shading.md, "Shadows on characters".
- **Limit:** this emulates the zone's cascades with the stage's light directions, not Haluna's sun angle. A shot of
  Asha in Cutscene A on the GPU would confirm it once the zone has one.
