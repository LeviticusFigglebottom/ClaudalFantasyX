| Ch8 day mood, no look | slate sky and fog, neutral key | `#525E62` · 0.474 / 0.016 / 223° |
| **Ch8 day mood + `day_storm`** (the shot) | **a slate wall on the sea, cyan rents pouring light** | `#4A5960` · 0.456 / 0.022 / 226° |

Reading:
- **Most of the daylight read comes from the lighting.** The colour script's Ch8 key, fill, slate sky and fog move the lit flanks from warm to slate-blue on their own (hue 97° → 223°).
- The `day_storm` look adds the storm's half: a cooler, slightly darker body, and a sun that reaches the lower wall, so the wall shows lit flanks and banding instead of the dusk tuning's shadowed lower body.
- **Framing matters as much as colour.** At 11 km the camera sits at the storm's bound radius and sees the anvil's unlit underside, so it read as night. At 22 km it reads as a tower. At **14 km** the wall spans most of the frame and rises past its top, which is the bible's "kilometres wide".
