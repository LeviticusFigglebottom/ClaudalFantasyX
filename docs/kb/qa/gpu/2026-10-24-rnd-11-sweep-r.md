# M2-RND-11: review-sweep-R fixes (2026-10-24)

Machine: RX 9070 XT, Godot 4.6.3, editor binary. Branch `gpu/m2-rnd-11-sweep-r`, cut from the integration branch.

## 1. First-run tests
`ShaderPrewarm.marker_current(preset, registry)` is the key logic of `needs_first_run`, without its headless guard.
A new test, `test_a_first_run_stages_every_wanted_entry_harvests_its_scenes_and_writes_its_key`, uses a
temporary registry and a tmp marker. It shows:
- a later boot stages only the resident entry;
- a first run stages every wanted entry and harvests `first_run_scenes`: 3, against 1;
- `_finish` writes the marker with `first_run_key("high")`;
- the marker then matches for High but not for Low.

## 2. Warm compute pipelines at exit
`UndertowBodyEffect.release_warm()` frees every warm shader, and with it its pipeline. The resident holder
calls it as the tree frees it. A twin warm-up creates the holder, and an effect still holding a released
shader doesn't free it again.
- **Before**, a windowed boot with `--quit-after 600` left `1 RID of type "Compute"` and `1 RID of type "Shader"`
  leaked.
- **After:** 0 leak lines and 0 ERROR lines over 3 runs. The headless smoke boot shows 0 ERROR lines.
- The release runs directly, not through `call_on_render_thread`. A call queued from the holder's predelete
  never ran at exit, and it kept scripts alive: hundreds of leaked instances. With the project's single-safe
  threading the main thread renders.

## 4. Ultra's reflection, and the sky dome
- Ultra's tier now has `compute_body`. Its full-resolution body runs through the compute effect, so the sea
  reflects the body's own pixels: the wounds glint in the chop as on High, which the impostor never did.
- It's also about 1 ms faster than the spatial body at 1440p (the storm's own: Day 15.2 against 16.2 ms, Eye
  16.4 against 17.7 ms).
- **Found:** Ultra's storm costs 15–18 ms at native 1440p. That's far over the ~1 ms the other tiers meet;
  Ultra has no render scale. It needs its own budget decision; filed for the Lead below.
- The sky dome's missing overcast is recorded in undertow.md's open items.

## 5. M2-ENV-05 lead: why Haluna's second-launch entry is slow
Haluna Beach entry (SceneRouter `last_swap_stats`), second launch, 2560×1440. Everything below is hidden under
the fade.

| Run | High entry | instantiate | prewarm | Low entry | Launch (High / Low) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| First launch (first-run warm-up) | 1535 ms | 1097 | 6 | 1471 ms | 5.1 / 6.7 s |
| Second launch (today) | 3097 | 1930 | 260 | 3498 | 2.0 / 2.5 s |
| + hold Haluna's PackedScene | 2709 | 1873 | 261 | 3302 | 2.6 / 3.1 s |
| + harvest Haluna's SceneState at boot | 2752 | 1906 | 262 | 3228 | 2.8 / 3.2 s |
| + hold Haluna's 17 registry entries | 2290 | 1628 | 5 | 3016 | 2.7 / 3.0 s |
| + those and the character materials (Asha's 11) | **1591** | 933 | 5 | **1885** | 3.4 / 4.2 s |
| + every wanted registry entry | 1493 | 957 | 5 | 1706 | 4.1 / 5.0 s |

**What it is.** A first run holds every registry entry it stages, not only the resident ones (a
`resident_only` boot holds what it builds). So after it, Haluna's set materials and the cast's character
materials are loaded and compiled for the session. The entry's worker-thread instantiate (1.1 s) and its
prewarm (6 ms) find them ready. On a second launch only the resident entries are held, so the instantiate
loads and compiles them again (1.9–2.2 s) and the entry's harvest reloads them (260 ms).

- It isn't the scene's other resources: holding Haluna's PackedScene, or harvesting it, changes little.
- **The lead for M2-ENV-05:** make Haluna's 17 registry entries resident, plus the character materials, which
  `gpu/hero-materials-resident` already does for Asha's 11. That brings a second launch's Haluna entry to
  1.6 s (High) and 1.9 s (Low), under 3 s. The cost is 1.3–1.7 s more boot behind the card, with its worst
  hidden frame at 0.38–0.53 s.
- Measured with temporary hooks, not committed. The registry change itself is m2-slice/Environment's call.
