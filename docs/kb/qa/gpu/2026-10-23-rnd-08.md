## Findings and fixes (`game/shaders/water/water_surface_body.gdshaderinc`, `light()`)

**1. SSS paints saturated green bands across the sea (sun low, behind the camera): reproduced and fixed.**
- **Cause.** `SPECULAR_LIGHT += col_sss * … * (back + 0.25 * wrap)`. The sharp `back` term is view-dependent and is 0 with the sun behind, but the soft `0.25 * wrap` term was **view-independent**. Masked by `v_crest`, the crests of the 62 m swell, it painted bands. Cyan `col_sss` times a warm low sun comes out green.
- **Fix.** Scale the wrap term by `toward_light = clamp(0.5 − 0.5·dot(VIEW, LIGHT), 0, 1)`: 1 looking into the sun, ½ side-lit, 0 with the sun straight behind the viewer.
- **Measured** (undertow scene, Horizon camera, sun 5° up straight behind, SSS forced on): the anti-solar disc mean goes **0.305 → 0.102**, and with SSS off it's also 0.102, so a sun behind the viewer now adds no SSS at all. The bands are gone (frame RMSE 0.0117 before → after).
- **Back-lit look unchanged:** water scene, Horizon camera, sun 5° up straight ahead: RMSE **0.0007**, max 9 levels.

**2. SSS crests read as large pale patches from high cameras at noon (Art, M1-ART-04): same cause, reduced.**
- The view-independent wrap term lit every crest whatever the view.
- With the noon sun (74°) and the Lagoon camera the broad pale-cyan sheets on the mid-ground swell are now much dimmer (frame RMSE 0.029). Caustics, the shallows colour and the glint are unchanged.
- **Art to confirm with their own high-camera views.** I don't know exactly which camera M1-ART-04 used.

**3. NaN at the anti-solar pixel blooming into a white disc: fixed defensively; not reproducible on this GPU.**
- **Cause (by inspection).** The glint's Schlick term `pow(1.0 − vdh, 5.0)` used `vdh = max(dot(VIEW, hv), 0.0)` with no upper clamp. At the anti-solar pixel VIEW = LIGHT = hv, and rounding can push `vdh` to 1 + ε. GLSL `pow()` of a negative base is undefined: NaN on lavapipe and some drivers. One NaN pixel spreads through glow and TAA into a disc. `hv = normalize(LIGHT + VIEW)` is also undefined when LIGHT = −VIEW.
- **Fix.** `vdh = clamp(dot(VIEW, hv), 0.0, 1.0)` and `hv *= inversesqrt(max(dot(hv, hv), ε))`.
- The fragment's Fresnel `pow(1 − ndv, 5)` already clamped `ndv`. The RX 9070 XT doesn't produce the NaN even with the old code: the anti-solar disc peaked at 0.36, with no disc in the water, undertow or lagoon views at 5° behind. **lavapipe, where the Undertow stream saw it, should confirm** on the Lead's next run.
- Regression test: `test_light_is_nan_safe_and_sss_is_view_dependent` (source-level, like `test_low_tier_keeps_sea_glow`).
