## 1. Nose and mouth-corner shadows with the key on

**No artefacts:** no acne, no striations, no terminator stair-steps at the mouth corners, at High or Ultra. The nose
now throws a real shadow, which the zone cascade couldn't resolve. **But on H05, as built, most of Asha's face
reads dark and blotchy.** Diagnosis:

| Test | Result | So |
|---|---|---|
| The key's shadow off | The face is cleanly lit | The darkening is the key's shadow, not its light |
| Hair and braid not casting (`--no-cast=hair`) | Unchanged | Not the hair cards |
| Toma not casting | Unchanged | Not another actor |
| Cast-only casters (`shadow_caster_mask` = layer 20) | Unchanged | Not the scenery |
| 32-bit shadow depth | Unchanged | Not depth precision |
| `shadow_bias` 0.3 | Half the face comes back into light | The occluder is within ~30 cm of the face, toward the light |
| The sun's elevation | **74°** (Haluna, mid-day) | ... |

**Cause:** the sun is almost overhead. A vertical face takes it at a grazing angle (N·L ≈ 0.28), and her own brow
and nose throw long shadows down it. This is the key doing exactly what it was built to do, copying the sun. The
key-off face only looks better because the 400 m cascade can't resolve those shadows at all. The blotches are the
skin's albedo and occlusion under ambient light only.

**Recommendation (Cinematics, shot data; no code change):** lower the key on Haluna's close shots with the
existing override. `key_pitch_deg` **30** (about 44° elevation) models the face well: a clean nose shadow, lit
cheeks, and clean mouth corners (`crop_h05_pitch.png`, second). `key_pitch_deg` 40 is also fine and slightly
flatter. Set H05 and H06 to 30. Any noon zone's close shots will need the same; worth a default rule in
cutscenes.md: on close shots, a key above ~55° elevation is pitched down to ~45°.

H06 (over Rhen): Asha is small in frame, and key on and off are nearly indistinguishable. No faults.

## 3. Haluna's zone sun at shadow_normal_bias 1.0 (M2-FP-11)

**Confirmed.** At runtime `HalunaMood/Sun` has `shadow_normal_bias` 1.0 and `shadow_bias` 0.03 (printed by the
probe), and `face_key_shadow_normal_bias` is 0, so it isn't overridden. **No face regression:** H05 with the key
off puts the sun at bias 1.0 on a dialogue-range face, and it's clean, with no acne or striations (key-off panels
in `crop_h05_diag.png`). H01 is a wide shot with no readable faces.

## A conflict to fix: render layer 20 is taken twice

`sequencer.json` `key_light.layer` is **20**, and so is `UndertowHalfRes.BODY_LAYER`. The Undertow's half-res body
lives on layer 20, and UndertowHalfRes clears layer 20 from the game camera's `cull_mask` every frame (undertow.md,
"Gotchas"). A close shot in a zone with a storm (Ch1, Ch8, Ch20–21) would move the cast to layer 20, and the
cast would **vanish** from the camera. Haluna has no storm, so this check isn't affected.
- **Proposed fix:** move the key light's cast to another free layer in sequencer.json (pick one in a registry first).
- Add a layer registry to `docs/kb/tech/` so the two can't collide again.
- This is the Lead's call (it crosses Cinematics and Rendering). Rendering's layer 20 is documented in
  undertow.md and was there first.
