# M1-RND-12: Haluna sky cost, quality tiers and fixes, measured on the GPU (2026-10-23)

Machine: AMD RX 9070 XT, Godot 4.6.3-stable Forward+/Vulkan, Windows 10. Branch `gpu/rnd-12-sky`.
Tool: `tools/gpu_bench/gpu_bench.tscn --variants=sky` on `game/levels/_tests/haluna_mood/haluna_mood.tscn`, cameras
H01, H04 and H07. Each row is the GPU median over 300 frames after a 120-frame, 3 s warm-up. **Cloud cost** is the
`sky` variant minus `no_clouds` (the same frame with `cloud_max_distance_m` 0, so the march returns at once). Load
while measuring: CPU 4–5 %, memory 59–60 %, GPU 3D ~1 % idle (nothing else running). Benchmarks ran one at a time.

## Before: no tiers, hashed noise

The sky ran the same march on every preset: up to 192 iterations, each lit sample making three density calls with a
hashed 3-octave fbm2 (12 PCG2D) and a hashed 4-octave fbm3 (32 PCG3D).

| | Low 1080p | Medium 1080p | High 1080p | Ultra 1080p | Low 1440p | Medium 1440p | High 1440p | Ultra 1440p |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Cloud cost, H01 | 2.40 | 2.77 | 2.37 | 3.55 | 3.69 | 4.33 | 3.71 | 5.72 |
| Cloud cost, H04 | 2.66 | 2.88 | 2.71 | 3.55 | 3.72 | 4.47 | 3.73 | 5.54 |
| Cloud cost, H07 | 2.46 | 2.85 | 2.46 | 3.50 | 3.65 | 4.22 | 3.68 | 5.58 |

Projected (RTX 3060 = 2.3–2.8×, GTX 1060 = 4.2–5.0×): **High 1440p on the recommended tier 8.5–10.4 ms; Low 1080p on
the minimum tier 10–13 ms**, for the clouds alone. The reviewer's "several ms" (review-sweep-H R1) was an underestimate.

Where it went (H01, High 1440p, the default 64-step march):

- The **coarse march of empty sky was 0.69 ms on its own** (coverage 0, so no cloud was ever found). The 2D height field runs
  on every coarse step, so its hashing dominated.
- The erosion fbm3 was about 1.5 ms before baking, and 0.3 ms after.
- The radiance cubemap does not re-render per frame while the sun and clouds are still. With 4 or 24 cubemap steps the
  frame cost was the same.

## What changed

1. **Baked lattices instead of hashes.** The shader still computes the same value noise; it just stops hashing.
   - The height field reads `assets/vfx/sky/cloud_lattice2.png` (new, `assets_src/vfx/sky/bake_cloud_lattice2.py`). It is
     the PCG2D hash of every signed cell within ±256, at 512² and 8 bits.
     - One `textureGather` per octave returns the four corners, and the shader interpolates them itself (quintic).
       Hardware bilinear only has 8 sub-texel bits, which would terrace the dome walls the field extrudes.
     - The cloud layout is the approved one: the sky matches the hashed field to a mean of 0.39 and a p99 of 3 levels.
   - The erosion fbm3 reads the Undertow's 64³ PCG3D lattice (`undertow_noise3d.png`) with one trilinear fetch per
     octave.
     - It wraps every 64 cells, so the billows are new ones of the same statistics. The cloud masses and silhouettes
       stay; the surface detail differs.
2. **Quality tiers, `sky_detail`** (a player setting like `vegetation_detail`; `levels.sky_detail` in graphics_presets.json).
   GraphicsSettings sets these uniforms on the Environment's sky material, and only those its shader declares:

   | Tier | `cloud_steps` | `cloud_erosion_octaves` | `cloud_light_detail` | `cloud_cubemap_steps` |
   |---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
   | low | 32 | 2 | 0 | 16 |
   | medium | 32 | 3 | 0 | 24 |
   | high | 40 | 4 | 0 | 24 |
   | ultra (= shader defaults, what the capture rig renders) | 64 | 4 | 1 | 24 |

   - `cloud_light_detail` 0 means the two sun samples skip the erosion; shadows don't need the billows.
   - Candidates were compared on crops of the H01 tower and the right-hand cumulus.
     - Below 32 steps the silhouettes step and the faces band. 24 steps showed both, and was rejected; a test keeps every
       tier at 32 or more.
     - High at 48 steps cost 0.64 ms for no visible gain over 40.
3. **Trapezoid integration** of each step's optical depth (`0.5 (d + d_prev)`). The opacity a step adds then varies
   smoothly with where the samples land against the cloud surface, so 32–40 steps no longer draw contour bands.
4. **R3, the iteration budget.** The budget is now `steps × (1 + refine/2)`, which is 192 at the defaults as before. A
   ray switches to "finish the rest" only when even coarse steps could no longer reach `t1`. It then marches the
   remainder at the step that does, instead of silently cutting off far cloud.
   - A first version tested `reach > dt_fine`, which is true from the first iteration (3 iterations per coarse step
     against a refine of 4). It never refined, and striped every tower; that's now covered in the shader comment.
   - Capping each step's opacity was also tried, and dropped: coarse steps in thin cloud drew wood-grain contours.
5. **R4, compositing.** The half-res pass returns premultiplied light, and the full pass composites
   `sky × (1 − a) + light`. The `mix()` multiplied cloud light by alpha a second time, which darkened thin edges and far
   haze quadratically.
   - Visible result: the darker blue rim around every cloud is gone, and thin cloud and the far deck are brighter.
   - `palette_check` still passes 19/19 on the new GPU captures (lit tops, chromatic undersides, zenith, grain).
6. **Far fade (the sweep-H nit).** Cloud alpha fades to 0 over the last 25 % of `cloud_max_distance_m`
   (`cloud_far_fade`), so the deck no longer ends in a cut.
7. **R2, the radiance re-render.** The radiance cubemap runs its own cheaper march (`cloud_cubemap_steps`, at most 2
   erosion octaves), and the Haluna Sky's `process_mode` is Incremental (time-sliced).

   Measured with `--drift-sky=2` (the cloud offset moves every frame), H01 High 1440p:

   | | GPU median | GPU p99 | frame p99 |
   |---|---:|---:|---:|
   | Before, static | 6.00 | 6.43 | 7.31 |
   | **Before, drifting clouds** | 6.01 | **11.36** | **17.10** |
   | After, static (Incremental) | 2.66 | 2.72 | 3.37 |
   | After, drifting, Automatic | 2.76 | 3.16 | 3.90 |
   | After, drifting, Incremental | 2.75 | 3.13 | 3.75 |
   | After, drifting, Realtime | 3.12 | 3.20 | 3.83 |

   - Before, a drifting sky spiked by about 5 ms and hit 17 ms frames. Now a drift costs ≤ 0.45 ms in any mode.
   - Turning the sun (`--spin-sun`) showed no measurable re-render cost in any mode. Either Godot's light-change check
     didn't fire for this rotation, or the cheap cubemap march hides it.
8. **R6, the net.** `kit_net` is now alpha hash (`transparency` 3), not alpha scissor at 0.4.
   - In the far mips the twine's ~0.56 coverage becomes a dither, which every preset's temporal AA (TAA or FSR2)
     resolves. On the GPU at High, the far panel reads as a see-through net, not a tannin sheet.
   - **In the capture rig, which has no TAA, the hashed net shows as a fine stipple** (H04). The AD should judge that
     when re-approving the baselines.
9. **R5.** The visible-sky lift is recorded as a per-zone exception in color_script.md (Conventions) and water.md
   (Gotchas). The zone README says what the sky costs and that its lattices must be bound.
10. **palette_check.** The zenith checks need 90 % of their rect selected (`min_fraction` 0.9). The underside hue is
    measured on cloud pixels only: within ΔE_ok 0.06 of a `cumulus_shadow` column, C ≤ 0.045, L ≥ 0.6, in hue range
    235–265° (sweep G S1).
    - `select` gained `"near"` / `"near_max_de"` to do this.
    - New tests inject the reviewer's two defects into the H01 baseline: a 90 % grey zenith and cyan undersides. Both
      now fail.

## After: tiers and baked noise

| | Low 1080p | Medium 1080p | High 1080p | Ultra 1080p | Low 1440p | Medium 1440p | High 1440p | Ultra 1440p |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Cloud cost, H01 | 0.23 | 0.27 | 0.30 | 0.79 | 0.31 | 0.37 | 0.42 | 1.19 |
| Cloud cost, H04 | 0.24 | 0.27 | 0.33 | 0.75 | 0.29 | 0.36 | 0.39 | 1.14 |
| Cloud cost, H07 | 0.21 | 0.22 | 0.28 | 0.76 | 0.28 | 0.34 | 0.39 | 1.12 |
| Whole frame, H01 | 1.13 (3.29) | 1.51 (4.00) | 1.79 (3.85) | 3.30 (6.04) | 1.63 (5.00) | 2.20 (6.15) | **2.67 (5.96)** | 4.88 (9.40) |

(Whole-frame figures in brackets are before.) The cost follows the window resolution, not `render_scale`: the
half-res sky pass is sized from the window.

**Projected:**

- **High 1440p, recommended tier (RTX 3060): 0.9–1.2 ms**, down from 8.5–10.4 ms.
- **Low 1080p, minimum tier (GTX 1060): 0.9–1.2 ms**, down from 10–13 ms.

Both are now rows in the graphics_settings.md budget tables. High is inside the M1-RND-12 budget, Low fits the
minimum tier's 30–60 fps envelope, and Ultra (1.1–1.2 ms here) is the screenshot and trailer preset.

## Baseline drift (lavapipe baselines not overwritten)

GPU capture rig (`tools/gpu_bench/capture_gpu.sh`, scene defaults = Ultra), before → after, linear RMSE:

| Shot | RMSE | max abs (8-bit) | pixels > 8 levels |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| haluna_h01_village_from_sea | 0.05177 | 195 | 20.9% |
| haluna_h02_tidewall_lane | 0.02211 | 115 | 4.1% |
| haluna_h03_lintel_gull | 0.03927 | 69 | 15.9% |
| haluna_h04_dock | 0.06047 | 166 | 18.4% |
| haluna_h07_chapel_court | 0.03859 | 124 | 7.7% |

**All five `haluna_*` baselines will drift** past the 0.008 gate. The causes are the R4 compositing, the new billows,
the far fade and R3's no-longer-truncated far cloud, plus the hashed net in H04. They need a Lead re-capture and an
Art Director re-approval. `palette_check` passes 19/19 on the GPU captures before and after. No other shot uses this
sky or `kit_net`.

## Not done here

- **S7, the floating cloud specks**, is an Art Director item, not in RND-12. Also not done: the net's drape (S6), and
  the extruded-column look of the height field, which is a signature-sky problem.
- **R5's longer-term suggestion**, moving the lift into `sky_energy` and re-validating `lagoon_clear`, is left to the
  signature sky.
- **Erosion that reproduces the hashed billows exactly** would need a signed 3D lattice large enough for the erosion's
  reach (hundreds of cells), so the billows are new ones instead.
