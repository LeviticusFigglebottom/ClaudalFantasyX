# M2-RND-01 start: the Undertow's "dark water" half (sweep-G findings on `undertow_day_ch08`), 2026-10-23

Machine: AMD RX 9070 XT, Godot 4.6.3-stable Forward+/Vulkan. Branch `gpu/m2-rnd-01-undertow`.
Tool: `tools/gpu_bench/gpu_bench.tscn` on `game/levels/_tests/undertow/undertow_test.tscn`. The figures are the GPU
median over 300 frames after a 120-frame, 3 s warm-up, whole frame. Load while measuring: CPU 4–5 %, memory ~60 %,
GPU otherwise idle. Benchmarks ran one at a time.

## The findings (review-sweep-G, Environment, on `undertow_day_ch08`)

Measured against bible §175, "a wall of dark water and cloud lit from within by glimmer", the shot missed:

1. **The base.** The wall sat on a flat, calm horizon line. There was no spray curtain and no lifted water joining
   sea and cloud.
2. **The sea in front.** It showed no influence from the storm at 14 km: no swell, and no darkening toward it.
3. **The flanks.** The two flank masses read as separate cumulus beside the main body, not as one wall.

## What changed

### 1. The base: a wall of dark water, with spray and scud
- **Skirt** (shared envelope, `undertow_common.gdshaderinc`, so the Low shells follow it too). The wall's foot
  flares out by `skirt_reach_m` (3 km) at sea level and closes at `skirt_height_m` (2.2 km). Its envelope is
  `skirt_density` 0.7, so the cloud noise tears it into rags instead of a solid band.
- **Water-dark base** (body). Below `water_wall_height_m` (1.4 km) the albedo turns toward `col_water_wall`, the
  zone sea's deep colour that the storm copies from its `water`, at `water_wall_mix` 0.55. The foot reads as lifted
  water, not more cloud.
- **Scud** (body). Below `scud_height_m` (2.6 km) the cloud noise is squashed vertically by `scud_stretch` 3, so the
  low wall and skirt tear into horizontal rags between sea and cloud base.
- **The spray curtain now shows.**
  - It was there before: two cylinders, the outer at 6.6 km radius, 650 m tall. But the body raymarch ignores depth
    and draws after the spray, so the body covered it wherever the two overlapped, which was the whole wall.
  - The outer curtain now draws after the body (`render_priority.spray_outer` 3; the motes move to 4), and only its
    near half (`spray_front_only`, `FRONT_FACING`); its far half is behind the wall.
  - It stands just outside the skirt's foot (10.6 km radius), is 560 m tall, and is shaded by the wall
    (`spray.sun` 0.35).
  - It's a sea-hugging mist plus torn sheets wider than they are tall. A row of equal puffs read as smoke.

### 2. The sea in front answers the storm
- A new **`storm`** uniform group in the shared water body (`water_surface_body.gdshaderinc`, every tier).
  `UndertowStorm` writes undertow.json **`sea_response`** onto its zone `water`'s material each frame, centred on
  the storm. It clears `storm_reach_m` when the storm leaves the tree.
- Within `reach_m` (32 km) the sea darkens toward the storm (`darken` 0.25 on the body and in-scatter). It also
  loses some of its reflected sky (`reflection_dim` 0.35), because the storm fills that sky.
- It turns choppier (`chop` +1.2× detail normals, which also raises crest foam) and rougher (`roughness` +0.12).
- **Wind streaks** run toward the storm: foam lines 0.6 m wide and 70 m long along the inflow, broken by a patch
  field. Where they shrink below a few pixels they fade to their mean coverage (`fwidth`), so they never alias into
  bars.
- `darken`, `chop`, `roughness` and `streak_amount` scale with `storm_intensity`, so a sleeping storm leaves the sea
  alone.
- **The storm's own funnel sea** dims its sky reflection the same way (`sea.reflection_dim` 0.35, half at the rim,
  all of it under the storm). Its rim no longer reads as a pale calm band against the darkened zone sea.
- **No storm, no change.** With `storm_reach_m` 0 (the default) every new term is an exact no-op, so the water shots
  don't move.

### 3. One wall
- **`band_join_m`** (5 km) fills the spiral bands' troughs near the wall, so the flank masses grow out of it.
- With the skirt under them, the flanks and the main body stand on one continuous foot.

## Looks

- **Day:** one slate wall standing in a darkened, wind-streaked sea. A grey spray mist and scud join it to the sea,
  and the flank masses are part of the wall.
- **Dusk, Night and Eye:** they pick up the same foot (the shape data is storm-wide), and their skies and wounds are
  unchanged. Dusk's left flank ends in horizontal scud rags.

## Half-resolution body with reprojection (M2-RND-01, criterion 1), 2026-10-23

Design and gotchas: undertow.md, "Half-resolution body".
- **Pipeline:** a SubViewport renders the body's layer at `body_scale` (Medium and High 0.5, Ultra 1). The
  composite is on the body's proxy, with a tent filter and an analytic capped-cylinder occlusion test. The game
  view's TAA/FSR2 does the temporal reprojection, as it did for the full-res body.
- **Cost, High 1440p** (the GPU time includes the SubViewport; `gpu_bench` now sums SubViewports):

  | Camera | Full res | Half res |
  |---|---:|---:|
  | Day | 4.94 ms | 2.27 ms |
  | Eye | 5.41 ms | 2.68 ms |
  | Night | 5.06 ms | 2.25 ms |
  | Horizon | 3.97 ms | 1.94 ms |

  The storm drops to about half its cost, and the M1-RND-10 6 ms cap now has more than 3 ms of headroom for the
  quality items.
- **Quality:**
  - **Glow dither on High:** 0.0092, against 0.0291 at full resolution (FSR2) and 0.0079 for native TAA.
  - **Pan** (Ultra, 0.15° a frame): reads like full res.
  - **Eye shot:** matches full res. The first occlusion test ignored the top cap and dropped the whole body; that's
    fixed.
- **Tried and rejected:**
  - SubViewport TAA: it destroys the alpha (black sky).
  - A full-screen composite quad: the same result as the proxy mesh. Godot writes no motion vectors for
    transparent materials, so TAA reprojects the storm from camera motion either way.
  - No tent filter: TAA's clamp kept the 2×2 jitter blocks as stipple.
- **Drift** (GPU capture_preset, High, storm held): undertow_day_ch08 0.0094, undertow_eye 0.0092,
  undertow_horizon_dusk 0.0084, undertow_night 0.0141; undertow_horizon_low unchanged. The lavapipe baselines will
  drift (the capture rig renders the High tier); re-approve them.
- **Known limit:** opaque geometry inside the wall's foot cylinder isn't occluded. The next step is a
  CompositorEffect compute march with the game view's depth.
