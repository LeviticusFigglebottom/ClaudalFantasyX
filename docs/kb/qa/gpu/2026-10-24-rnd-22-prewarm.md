# M1-RND-22: the prewarm hitch floor (2026-10-24)

Machine: RX 9070 XT, Godot 4.6.3 Forward+/Vulkan, Windows 10. Branch `gpu/m1-rnd-22-prewarm`, cut from
`origin/claude/undertow-lead-handoff-423ixt`. One measurement at a time.

## What the floor was (diagnosed, not as RND-15 assumed)

RND-15 put the ~230–310 ms first prewarm frame down to "stage set-up". I timed `ShaderPrewarm.start()` and each
staged entry (`tools/gpu_bench/prewarm_bench.tscn`, now printing `PREWARM_START_MS`):
- **The stage set-up is not the floor.** A stage kept alive from boot changed nothing: 563 against 559 ms on the
  Undertow scene, 295–316 against 316 ms on Asha's. That idea was measured and dropped.
- **The incoming scene's material harvest is not the floor for battle scenes either:** ~15 ms on asha_stage.
  It is the floor for big zones (Haluna).
- **The floor was what the first batch staged.** It sits inside `start()`, which runs in the router's frame:
  - **`prewarm_test_material.tres`, 118 ms.** A *test* material, registered for every transition
    (`"scenes": []`), was compiled at every scene change.
  - **Other presets' tier variants:** 75 ms each for `character_mouth_low` and `_medium` on a High game, and the
    same for every other per-tier shader. The registry's own notes said "prewarm only the active tier once
    ShaderPrewarm filters by tier".
  - **Shader variant compiles happen on first draw, not on load.** Loading 14–16 shaders takes 45–75 ms. The
    100–220 ms is the first material that draws them. So preloading resources alone does nothing; they must be
    drawn once.

## What changed

1. **The test material** left the production registry. Tests use their own registries.
2. **Tier filtering.** An entry with `"preset_tier": {"system", "tier"}` is staged only when the active preset
   selects that tier. The systems are character (`CharacterShaderTiers.tier_for_preset`), undertow (the preset
   id), and water, vegetation and weathering (the preset's `tiers`). 30 entries are tagged; default-tier materials
   stay untagged, since they also prewarm their real mesh formats.
3. **A boot warm-up of the resident shaders.** Every registry `.gdshader` entry is `"resident": true`.
   - `ShaderPrewarm.spawn_resident()` loads the preset's resident shaders, holds them for the session (a held
     Shader keeps its compiled variants), and stages them once under the tree root.
   - Boot starts it (`GraphicsRuntime.warm_resident_shaders`), and so does a preset change, but only once boot
     has armed GraphicsRuntime, so tests and captures are unaffected. Transitions then only build pipelines.
4. **The FSR2 stage** is at least `fsr2_min_size` (256) px. At 64 px, FSR2 logged
   `FFX_ERROR_INVALID_ARGUMENT` every frame of every High transition; that was pre-existing, and is now 0 with no
   cost change.
5. **Router timing (review-sweep-J S1 / sweep-N S3).** Without `split_swap`, `frame_ms[0]` starts after the
   prewarm wait (`SceneRouter.frame0_start`, tested).

**Tried and reverted:**
- The harvest on a worker thread, and threaded registry loads (`ResourceLoader.load_threaded_request`). Both
  **crashed Godot** (segfault) on Haluna in 3 of 6 runs, and the base never does. The threaded loads did take
  ~33% off the Undertow scene's worst frame. Not worth a crash.
- The persistent boot stage: no gain.

## Measured: the worst frame of the transition's prewarm, ms

Pre-scene: the Haluna zone, then the incoming scene (Haluna itself is entered from `haluna_mood`). Cold means
this project's `shader_cache` and `vulkan` were cleared first. "Before" is the integration branch (3f5eb36).

| Incoming scene | Preset | Before, cold | Before, warm | **After, cold** | **After, warm** |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| asha_stage (the battle stand-in) | Low | 366 | 308 | **41** | **34** |
| asha_stage | High | 371 | 310 | **46** | **36** |
| asha_stage | Ultra | 368 | 314 | **45** | **36** |
| undertow_test (storm scene) | Low | 972 | 541 | 254 | 151 |
| undertow_test | High | 709 | 550 | 185 | 142 |

All 18 "after" runs completed (no crash), each with 13–61 prewarm frames, well within the router's 120-frame wait.

**The boot warm-up** (once per session, under the boot screen): 1.3–3.1 s in total, worst frame 0.5–1.7 s,
14–16 shaders held.

## Verdict against SPEC §4.2 ("No hitch over 50 ms during battle transitions or dialogue")

- **Battle transitions: met** on Low, High and Ultra, cold and warm (34–46 ms).
- **Dialogue:** cutscene cuts play inside a zone already warmed. Their override pipelines use
  `spawn_with_state` (M1-RND-16).
- **Zone entries and the storm scene:** 142–312 ms remain. These happen behind the full fade, so they're
  loading-screen frames, not the battle transitions or dialogue the SPEC names.
  - The Haluna floor is the synchronous harvest of a 60-material zone.
  - The storm's floor is its runtime-built materials' first pipelines.
  - ADR 0029 proposes how §4.2 reads for them, and the two follow-ups: the export shader baker, and a
    scene-state harvest instead of an instance.
