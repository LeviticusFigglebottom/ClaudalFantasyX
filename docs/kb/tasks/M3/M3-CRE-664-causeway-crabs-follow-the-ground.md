## Goal
The Act I final-quality audit (creatures, 2026-10-29; `tools/creatures/wildlife_audit.gd`) found the causeway's two
crab areas (`path_head` y 0.11, `path_pool` y 0.18) standing on ground that varies by up to 0.30 m: the crabs walk at a
flat y, so some float and some sink. Every other zone's crabs now follow the ground: `game/data/world/wildlife/<zone>.json`

`ch03_saltgrass_causeway.json` is GENERATED by `tools/causeway/build_causeway_gameplay.py` (`build_wildlife`), so the
key belongs in the generator, not a hand edit.
