## Goal
Writing the Kerrid and Pell art sheets (docs/kb/art/characters/kerrid.md and pell.md) turned up one guardrail risk and a set of open story questions. The character builds (M3-CHR-505, M3-CHR-506) wait on the answers.

**1. Echo check (SPEC §2, CLAUDE.md §2; pillar 1).** In outline, Pell is:
- a tall non-human who learns enemy abilities;
- carries a long pole;
- bears a visible mark of shame from his own people;
- and returns to them in the end.

That is close to a well-known beast-folk guardian in the inspiring series. The art sheet avoids every visual cue: no horn, no mane, no blue fur, no tattoos, no levelled spear, no jumps. The staff is in his left hand, and the Kirru heron idiom drives his movement. The arc itself is Narrative's to check. Decide whether it is far enough from that figure, and if not, change one or more beats. Record the decision in the decisions log, or in an ADR if it changes canon. If any term needs it, add it to tools/banned_terms.txt.

**2. Kerrid questions:**
- Does "no breath" in his idle stand (the bible says the unrisen don't breathe, but he speaks)?
- No tears?
- Is the anchor-shackle ring as the cleaver's pommel welcome?
- Is a 6% skin desaturation right, or should it be 0?

**3. Pell questions:**
- No tail?
- No lower garment?
- Is a spiral of notches acceptable as the name carving?
- No tears?
- Bible §8 says the Kirru keep no keepsakes, but vowbound.md §5 calls Nhoa's name-shell "the keepsake". Which holds?
- Fur: bible §8 says dark fur on the back and shoulders, and pell.md says sleek dark fur. The sheet reconciles this as a dark saddle with mid-brown fur on the front. Confirm.

**4. Stale sources** (for a sweep):
- ADR 0020's body still carries text that ADR 0022 corrects: the rope-burns as a gold cue, the "Stillguard" cord and stair-law, and "gone from Corrow before the Ebbing".
- Glossary rows: "unrisen" says "around an unkept promise", while the bible says anything unfinished. The sweep-E rows still say "Stillguard". Mother Kell's rows 195 and 254 still say she is a diver.
