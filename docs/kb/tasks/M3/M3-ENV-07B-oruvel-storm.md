## Swap-in note (m2-slice, Combat)
Everything is in the layout (`game/levels/_tests/oruvel_kit/oruvel_layout.json`, generated by
`assets_src/environments/kits/oruvel/ov_preview_layout.py`); copy the data, never reference the preview folder.
- **The storm swap** (`states.storm`): `hide` lists the day placements to hide (piece, at, y), `pieces` what to place
  instead (HalunaDresser format: at, y, yaw_deg, tilt_deg, materials). Every broken piece sits in its intact piece's
  frame, so the zone's existing blocks can swap one for one: `raft_deck` -> `raft_deck_broken` (the torn strip is its
  +X side: turn it 180 deg to tear the -X side, as rc is), `plank_bridge` -> `plank_bridge_broken` (the C1-13 "bridge
  goes" beat: the second mooring's bridge is the one in the state), `hull_cabin` -> `hull_capsized`. `storm_debris` and
  `reef_edge_far` (300 m south, `lod1_from_m` 600) are additions. The zone's C1-13 run triggers, the lashing cuts
  (`Ch01OruvelZone` "lashings") and when each piece swaps are m2-slice's.
- **Hullgnaw's split** (`rigs.net_pen_split`): hide `net_pen` and place the three sections at their hinges (in the
  layout's world frame: A (27.5, 0, -16.8), B (22.2, 0, -6.2), C (32.8, 0, -6.2); net_pen frame A (0, 0, -5.3),
  B (-5.3, 0, 5.3), C (5.3, 0, 5.3)); with no turn they compose the intact pen, so phase 1 can already stand on the
  three pieces. **The phase change drives one number**, amount a 0 -> 1 (tween it over the lashings' snap, ~1.5 s), and
  the swell clock t (seconds, from the phase change): per section origin = hinge + a * drift, basis = Basis(UP, a *
  yaw_deg) * Basis.from_euler(Vector3(a * (tilt_deg[0] + swell), 0, a * tilt_deg[1])), swell = pitch_deg * sin(TAU *
  t / period_s + phase). Reference implementation: `OruvelPreview.section_transform(section, a, t)` (tested against
  the state's pose at a 1, t 0). Open: A drifts 1.5 m north and pitches its broken inner edge 12 deg toward the hole;
  B and C drift out south-west / south-east with their long arms going under. Walk collision for the sections (no `-col`
  proxies yet) and where Rhen and Hullgnaw stand on them are Combat's and m2-slice's; the pool between them stays open
  water for Hullgnaw's coils.
- **Kell's beacon** (`storm_lights`): one oil-flame omni (#FFB466, energy 3, range 9 m) in the chandlery's roof lantern
  at (-38.5, 5.1, -15.8), shadowless, `light_volumetric_fog_energy = 0`; lit from the drill (C1-12) to O18.
