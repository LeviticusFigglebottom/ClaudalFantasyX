## Goal
The first build (M3-CRE-621) reads as the sheet says at battle distance, but viewed close: the roots are smooth,
even tubes radiating from the top like legs (a real mangrove's prop-roots are knuckled, taper toward the mud and
arch down from the trunk), so the risen stance reads spidery (README rule 1 risk); and the core is a glossy,
featureless sphere.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Roots: knuckles every 0.4–0.7 m, taper, bark segments, lean outward from a visible trunk base
- [ ] Core: folds and blisters, the wounds in the folds
- [ ] Risen: four to six roots plant, the rest hang and trail (no even spider ring)
- [ ] Blender verify identical; PC render of creature_mangrove_drowner and _risen, viewed and approved
