**Shot list and my read of each image (lavapipe, 960 x 540):**
- **T01 (registered, `tamsule_t01_bell_from_the_mouth`)**: the establishing view from the river mouth. The green
  bell reads as old bronze, not Order gold; the drop's lower part hangs cyan below the mouth; the accreted gantry
  frames it; the pennant lines fall to the galleries; thatched port rows left and right; a watch-boat in the water.
  Weakest point: the galleries read as plain boxes at 150 m, and the port rows are uniform.
- T02: from the west gallery's second tier, the drop and the glass waist above, the column falling between two
  legs, the east galleries and their cloths across the bottom. Reads as "the seats".
- **T03 (registered, `tamsule_t03_oruvel_moorings`)**: the best frame. Oruvel's sea-blue and bone posts receding
  across the foreground, the pale pool rim, the column falling from the drop, the bell's mouth and conduits
  overhead, legs and pennants framing, the galleries beyond. Reads as the side scene's empty places.
- T04: on the east jetty, frontal and ritual: the float-post and woven float centre, the column just right, legs
  framing symmetrically. Good; the prow-boards show only at the bottom edge.
- T05: straight up the column into the drop: the rim ring, the glass bays and the radiating conduits. A strong
  graphic set piece; it reads a little like a wheel.
- T06: the gantry head at 85 mm: silvered and black trunk lengths, lashings, red splints, hewn beams, the yoke cap
  and finial, the green crown and ports. Gold is now thin bands (the first pass had drum-sized gold pucks).
- T07: the waist at 100 mm: verdigris with salt bloom over the ribs, glass bays with the drop behind. The patina
  reads; worn-bronze edges are subtle.
- T08: a gilded board in the right foreground, the gallery's boarded screen and its own boards, the bell above.
- Fixed during iteration (each seen, then changed): the bell read as a gold observatory dome (patina mask never
  reached threshold on the sparse detail map: rewritten), the galleries hid the drop from outside (stack raised 6 m,
  7 tiers to 5), the gantry read as steel pipe (hewn beams, timber lengths, thinner gold), the low tie crossed every
  under-Bell view (removed), the gallery outsides were dark undersides and then ochre boxes (a grey boarded band),
  T02 and T07 sat under shade-cloths, T04 missed the prow (framed numerically, then on the jetty).

**Open issues (honest).** Lavapipe only (no GPU look, no SDFGI). No crowd (characters are not the kit's); the stands
read empty. The drop is a placeholder until M3-RND-26. The port's shophouses are two variants with scale jitter
and read uniform at range; the galleries are plain at 150 m. The colour script's LUT hue pushes (boards warm, drop
cyan) are not in the generated LUT. The preview uses depth fog, not the frame row's volumetric 0.003. The prow-boards
of the captains' platforms are small in T04.
