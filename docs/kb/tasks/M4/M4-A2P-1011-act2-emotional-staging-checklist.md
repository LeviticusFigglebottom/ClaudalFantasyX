## Goal
To slice-polish, from the Lead's Act II emotional-beats pass (pillars 1 and 6).

**The lines need no change.** No Act II line undercuts sorrow. Every laugh and smile sits *before* a loss (Tobin's joy in
Ch13, the lamp-bearers), never after it. Tobin's dying "Your knots are terrible." pays off Ch13's knots and is his
tenderness, not a release.

Story has lengthened its own holds to the direction notes:
- V00: 5 → 9 s;
- Pell's kneel (N05): 4 → 7 s;
- N09: 6 → 8 s;
- Ch13's A01: 5 → 8 s;
- Ethren's rise (S05): 4 → 6 s.

What remains is staging that can spend a beat. Each row points at the direction note that already says how to stage it.
