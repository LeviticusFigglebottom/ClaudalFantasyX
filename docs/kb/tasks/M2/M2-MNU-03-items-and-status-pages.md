## Goal
The field has no way to see or use the bag, or to see a hero's numbers. Build an Items page and a Status page reached from the field menu.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Items: the bag by category, a description of each item, a sort, and field use of restoratives on a chosen hero (the item's own effects, written to the saved vitals; refusals in words).
- [ ] Status: per hero HP, MP, stats (base and growth), gear, Swell gauge and mode, Lattice progress (movement points, nodes woken).
- [ ] Keyboard, controller and mouse; UiSettings honoured; screenshots; tests.
